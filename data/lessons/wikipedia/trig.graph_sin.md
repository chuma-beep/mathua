# Graph of sine function

### Vector definition
The cross product and dot product are operations on two vectors in Euclidean vector space. The sine and cosine functions can be defined in terms of the cross product and dot product. If \(\mathbf{a}\) and \(\mathbf{b}\) are vectors, and \(\theta\) is the angle between \(\mathbf{a}\) and \(\mathbf{b}\), then sine and cosine can be defined as:

\[
\begin{align}
 \sin (\theta) &= \frac{|\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}|}{|\mathbf{a}| |\mathbf{b}|}, \\
 \cos (\theta) &= \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{|\mathbf{a}| |\mathbf{b}|}.
\end{align}
\]
