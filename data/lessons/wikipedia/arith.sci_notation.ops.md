# Operations with scientific notation

### Engineering notation

Engineering notation (often named "ENG" on scientific calculators) differs from normalized scientific notation in that the exponent *n* is restricted to multiples of 3. Consequently, the absolute value of *m* is in the range 1 ≤ |*m*| < 1000, rather than 1 ≤ |*m*| < 10. Though similar in concept, engineering notation is rarely called *scientific notation*. Engineering notation allows the numbers to explicitly match their corresponding SI prefixes, which facilitates reading and oral communication. For example, 12.5E-9 can be read as "twelve-point-five nanometres" and written as 12.5, while its scientific notation equivalent 1.25E-8 would likely be read out as "one-point-two-five times ten-to-the-negative-eight metres".
