# The birthday problem

The birthday problem asks how many people are needed before two share a birthday is more likely than not.

## Complement

No shared birthday means all birthdays are distinct, which has probability \(n!/(n^k)\) for \(k\) people out of \(n\) days.

## Threshold

The threshold grows like the square root of the number of days, about 23 for 365 days.

## Pairs

The expected number of shared pairs is the number of pairs divided by the number of days.

To check your understanding, count ordered birthdays, solve for half and count the pairs.

Collisions appear at the square-root scale.
