# Exact functors

An exact functor preserves short exact sequences. Left exact functors preserve kernels and right exact functors preserve cokernels.

## Definition

A functor is exact when it sends every short exact sequence to a short exact sequence.

## One-sided

A left exact functor preserves the kernel sequence, and a right exact functor preserves the cokernel sequence.

## Terms

A sequence with n terms stays a sequence with n terms under an exact functor.

To check your understanding, apply the functor, keep the kernel and count the terms.

Exactness is preservation of the shape of short exact sequences.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller numbers and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
