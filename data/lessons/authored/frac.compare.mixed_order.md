# Ordering mixed numbers

Mixed numbers can be ordered by comparing whole parts first and fractional parts second. Cross multiplication settles ties in the fractional parts.

## Whole parts first

The mixed number with the larger whole part is larger when the whole parts differ. The fractional parts cannot overcome a whole-number difference. Compare the whole parts before converting.

## Cross multiplication

When the whole parts are equal, compare a over b and c over d by cross multiplying. The larger cross product belongs to the larger fraction. The denominators must be positive.

## Benchmark comparison

A fractional part can be compared with one half by doubling the numerator. A fraction above one half is larger than one below it. Benchmarks give a quick check on the cross product.
