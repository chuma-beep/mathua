# Lambert series

A Lambert series sums a_n x to the n divided by one minus x to the n. Expanding the geometric factor turns it into a divisor series.

## The Lambert-series form

The n-th denominator expands as a geometric series in x to the n. Collecting powers of x gives a coefficient that sums a_d over the divisors d of n. The transformation is exact inside the radius.

## Divisor coefficients

For a_n equal to one, the coefficient of x to the n is the number of divisors of n. For a_n equal to n, the coefficient is the sum of the divisors. These arithmetic functions are the reason Lambert series are useful.

## Evaluating at zero

At x equal to zero every term with n greater than one vanishes. The value is a_1. This is the constant term of the series.
