# Young tableaux

Young diagrams index the irreducible representations of symmetric groups. Standard tableaux of a shape form a basis of the representation.

## Diagrams

A partition of n draws as rows of boxes, with row lengths weakly decreasing.

## Standard tableaux

A standard tableau fills the boxes with 1 to n so that rows and columns increase.

## Hook length

The hook length formula counts standard tableaux as n! divided by the product of hook lengths.

To check your understanding, draw the rows, fill the boxes and compute the hooks.

Young diagrams turn symmetric-group representations into combinatorics.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller numbers and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
