# Versine and coversine

The versine and coversine measure how far sine and cosine fall short of one. They turn small-angle and haversine calculations into integer arithmetic on a right triangle.

## The versine and coversine definitions

The versine of an angle is 1 minus its cosine. The coversine is 1 minus its sine. Both are zero at the angle where the corresponding function equals one and reach their largest values at the opposite extreme.

## Exact values from a right triangle

A 3-4-5 triangle supplies exact sine and cosine values. If the cosine is 3/5, the versine is 2/5; if the sine is 4/5, the coversine is 1/5. Multiplying by the denominator clears the fraction and gives an integer.

## Adding the two functions

The sum of versine and coversine is 2 minus the sum of sine and cosine. With a common denominator this becomes an integer calculation. This combination appears in identities that compare a function with its complement.
