# Surface Euler characteristic

Every closed surface has an Euler characteristic fixed by its classification. For orientable surfaces it is \(2 - 2g\), and for nonorientable surfaces it is \(2 - k\).

## Orientable surfaces

A closed orientable surface of genus \(g\) has \(\chi = 2 - 2g\). The sphere gives two, the torus zero and the double torus minus two.

## Nonorientable surfaces

A closed nonorientable surface with crosscap number \(k\) has \(\chi = 2 - k\). The projective plane gives one and the Klein bottle zero.

## Boundary

Each boundary circle lowers the Euler characteristic by one. A disc has \(\chi = 1\), an annulus zero and a pair of pants minus one.

To check your understanding, apply two minus twice the genus, apply two minus the crosscap number and subtract one per boundary circle.

The Euler characteristic is the single number that separates the surfaces.
