# Elasticity of demand

Elasticity measures the percentage change in demand for a one-percent change in price. It is a dimensionless ratio built from the derivative of the demand function.

## The elasticity formula

For demand q as a function of price p, elasticity is the price divided by the quantity times dq over dp. The sign is often omitted when speaking about magnitude. A power demand q equal to a p to the minus k has constant elasticity k.

## Elastic, inelastic and unit

Demand is elastic when the elasticity magnitude is greater than 1. It is inelastic when the magnitude is less than 1 and unit elastic when it equals 1. The labels describe how strongly quantity responds to price.

## Elasticity and revenue

Revenue is price times quantity. Its derivative with respect to price is positive when demand is inelastic and negative when demand is elastic. Unit elasticity is the boundary where revenue stops rising.

Practice connects the three sections: state the relation, carry out the computation and interpret the result. A second method is a useful check when one is available.
