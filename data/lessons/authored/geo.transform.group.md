# Transformation groups

The isometries of a figure form a group under composition. The group records which symmetries can be undone and combined.

## Closure

Composing two isometries gives another isometry, so the set is closed under composition.

## Inverses

Every isometry has an inverse isometry, which undoes it and returns every point.

## The dihedral count

A regular \(n\)-gon has \(2n\) symmetries: \(n\) rotations and \(n\) reflections.

To check your understanding, compose two maps, undo a rotation and count rotations.

Symmetries form a group because composition, identity and inverses all stay inside the set.
