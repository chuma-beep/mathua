# Discrete valuation rings

A discrete valuation ring is a local principal ideal domain with a valuation taking integer values. It has exactly one nonzero prime ideal.

## Valuation

The valuation sends a uniformiser to one and measures the order of vanishing.

## Ideals

Every nonzero ideal is a power of the maximal ideal, so the ring is a principal ideal domain.

## Residue field

The residue field is the quotient by the maximal ideal and can be any finite field.

To check your understanding, pick a uniformiser, list the powers and take the quotient.

A discrete valuation ring is the simplest local principal ideal domain.
