# Deck transformations

A deck transformation is a homeomorphism of a covering space that preserves the covering map. The deck transformations form a group that records the symmetry of the cover.

## The deck transformation group

A deck transformation of a covering p from E to B is a homeomorphism of E with p of f equal to p. It permutes the sheets above each point. The identity is always a deck transformation and composition gives the group law.

## The deck group

For the covering of the circle by itself of degree n, the deck group is cyclic of order n. A cyclic group of order n has phi of n generators. The deck group measures how the sheets are permuted.

## Regular coverings

A covering is regular when the deck group acts transitively on each fiber. The universal cover of a wedge of circles is regular, and its deck group is the free group on the same number of generators. The rank is therefore the number of circles.
