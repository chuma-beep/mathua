# Frobenius reciprocity

Frobenius reciprocity says induction and restriction are adjoint functors. Multiplicities can be computed on either side.

## Statement

The multiplicity of an irreducible in an induced representation equals the multiplicity of its restriction in the original.

## Dimension

Inducing a representation of dimension d along a subgroup of index k gives dimension dk.

## Multiplicity

Reciprocity computes a multiplicity as an inner product on the smaller group.

To check your understanding, induce the representation, multiply the dimension and take the inner product.

Reciprocity moves a computation between a group and a subgroup.
