# Complex exponentiation

A complex power is defined through the exponential and the logarithm. It can be multivalued when the exponent is not an integer.

## Evaluate a power

Write z to the w as e to the w log z. Use a branch of the logarithm. The result depends on that branch unless w is an integer.

## Apply the principal branch

The principal branch uses the principal logarithm. It gives one distinguished value of the power. Other branches differ by a factor of e to the 2 pi i w.

## Use the exponential identity

The identity e to the a plus b equals e to the a times e to the b holds for complex exponents. It is the fastest way to simplify a product of powers. The identity is not automatically true for arbitrary branches of the logarithm.
