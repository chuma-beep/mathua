# Amortisation

An amortised loan is repaid with equal payments whose present value equals the amount borrowed. Each payment covers the interest due and reduces the balance.

## The payment formula

The payment is the loan amount times the periodic rate divided by one minus the growth factor raised to the negative number of periods.

## Interest and principal

The interest part of a payment is the balance times the periodic rate. The remainder reduces the principal, so the split changes over the life of the loan.

## Remaining balance

The balance after a payment is the previous balance times the growth factor minus the payment.

To check your understanding, find the periodic rate, compute interest and apply the rate.

A larger payment shortens the loan superlinearly, because it also reduces future interest.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on.
