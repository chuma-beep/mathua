# Winding numbers

The winding number counts signed turns of a closed curve about a point.

## Definition

The winding number is the net change in argument along the curve divided by \(2\pi\).

## Multiple turns

Traversing the same circle twice gives winding number two, and reversing the orientation gives minus one.

## Orientation

Counterclockwise turns count positive and clockwise turns negative, so the sign records direction.

To check your understanding, track the argument, count the turns and reverse the curve.

A winding number is an integer because a closed argument change is a multiple of a full turn.
