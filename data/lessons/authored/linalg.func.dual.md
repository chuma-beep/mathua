# The dual space

The dual space consists of all linear functionals on a vector space. It has the same finite dimension, but its elements are covectors rather than vectors.

## Dimension

A finite-dimensional space and its dual have equal dimension. The dual basis pairs each functional with one basis vector and annihilates the others.

## Pairing

Evaluation is a bilinear pairing between the space and its dual. In coordinates it is the dot product of a row vector with a column vector.

## Double dual

The double dual has the same dimension again. The canonical map into it is injective, and in finite dimension it is an isomorphism.

To check your understanding, match the dimension, evaluate a functional and map into the double dual.

The dual space is where coordinates live, which is why row vectors and column vectors behave differently.
