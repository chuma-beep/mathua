# Borel summability

Borel summability assigns a value to some divergent series through an integral transform. It is useful when a power series has zero radius of convergence.

## Borel transform

The Borel transform replaces the coefficient a_n by a_n divided by n factorial. The new series often has a positive radius. Its integral then defines the Borel sum.

## Summability

A series is Borel summable when the Borel transform can be integrated along a suitable ray. The result agrees with the ordinary sum when the original series converges. The method is a generalized summation.

## Coefficients

The coefficients of a Borel-summable series can grow factorially. The factorial growth is exactly what the transform removes. The transformed series is better behaved.
