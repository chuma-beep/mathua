# Legendre polynomials

Legendre polynomials solve Legendre's equation on the interval from minus one to one. They are orthogonal with respect to the constant weight.

## Evaluate a Legendre polynomial

Use the Rodrigues formula or the recurrence. The first polynomials are 1, x, (3x squared minus 1) over 2 and (5x cubed minus 3x) over 2. The value at plus or minus one is plus or minus one raised to n.

## Use orthogonality

The integral of P sub m P sub n over the interval is zero when m and n differ. When they agree the integral is 2 over 2n plus 1. This is the factor that normalises a Legendre expansion.

## Apply the recurrence

The three-term recurrence relates P sub n plus 1, P sub n and P sub n minus 1. It is the fastest way to evaluate a high-degree polynomial at a point. The recurrence is stable for arguments in the interval.
