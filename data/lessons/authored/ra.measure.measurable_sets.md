# Measurable sets

A sigma-algebra is a collection of sets closed under complement and countable union. Its members are the measurable sets.

## Sigma-algebra closure

A finite sigma-algebra generated by k atoms contains every union of atoms. Each atom is either included or excluded. The count is 2 to the k.

## Preimages

A function pulls a measurable set back to a measurable set. On a finite set, count the points whose image lies in the target. The preimage is the set of those points.

## Countable unions

The measure of a countable union of disjoint measurable sets is the sum of their measures. On a finite example this is ordinary addition. The property is the defining additivity of measure.
