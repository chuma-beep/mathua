# Derivatives of floor and ceiling functions

The floor function is constant between integers and jumps at each integer. Its derivative is zero on every open interval between jumps.

## The floor function

On an interval between consecutive integers, the floor is constant. The derivative of a constant is zero. At an integer the function jumps and the derivative does not exist.

## Jumps

The jump occurs at every integer. The left and right values differ by one. A jump discontinuity prevents differentiability.

## Derivative on intervals

Away from integers the derivative is zero. The derivative is undefined at integers. This pattern is the simplest example of a piecewise constant function.
