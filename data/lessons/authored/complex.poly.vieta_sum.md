# Sums of roots

The sum of the roots is minus the coefficient of z^{n-1} divided by the leading coefficient.

## Quadratic case

For \(az^{2}+bz+c\) the two roots sum to \(-b/a\).

## General case

For a monic polynomial \(z^{n}+a_{n-1}z^{n-1}+\cdots\) the root sum is \(-a_{n-1}\).

## Roots of unity

The \(n\)-th roots of unity satisfy \(z^{n}-1=0\), so their sum is zero for every \(n\ge2\).

To check your understanding, identify the coefficients, read the next-to-leading coefficient and sum the roots.

Vieta's sum formula reads one coefficient as a symmetric function of the roots.
