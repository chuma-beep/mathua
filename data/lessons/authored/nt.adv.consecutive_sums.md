# Sums of consecutive integers

Many integers are sums of two or more consecutive positive integers. The obstruction is exactly a power of two.

## Odd divisors

The number of ways to write n as a sum of consecutive positive integers equals the number of odd divisors of n. Excluding the one-term sum leaves the odd divisors greater than 1. This is the counting principle behind the family.

## The shortest block

A block of length k starts at a and ends at a plus k minus 1. Its sum is k times 2a plus k minus 1 divided by 2. To find the shortest block, test k from 2 upward until a is a positive integer.

## Powers of two

A positive integer is a sum of two or more consecutive positive integers exactly when it is not a power of two. Powers of two have no odd divisor greater than 1. Every other integer has at least one such representation.
