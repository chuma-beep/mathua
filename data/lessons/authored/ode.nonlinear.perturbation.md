# Perturbation methods

Perturbation methods expand a solution in a small parameter. The leading term solves a simpler problem and later terms correct it.

## Find the leading term

Set the small parameter to zero and solve the reduced problem. This gives the zeroth-order approximation. It is the base around which the correction is built.

## Compute the first correction

Substitute the expansion into the equation and collect the first power of the parameter. Solve the resulting linear problem for the first correction. The correction is often a polynomial or a simple exponential.

## Check the validity range

The expansion is useful only while the correction is smaller than the leading term. A secular term that grows without bound signals a breakdown. Rescaling the variable can extend the range.

A complete answer states the governing relation, substitutes the given values and reads the result in the language of the problem.
