# The snake lemma

The snake lemma produces a connecting homomorphism from a commutative diagram with exact rows. The connecting map measures the obstruction to completing the diagram.

## The connecting map

The snake lemma yields one connecting homomorphism for a diagram of three columns. It maps the kernel of the right vertical map to the cokernel of the left one.

## The exact sequence

The six-term sequence runs through kernels, the middle group and cokernels. It has six terms and five maps.

## The alternating count

In the finite-rank case the alternating sum of the six ranks is zero. That identity is the reason the connecting map preserves Euler characteristics.

To check your understanding, count the connecting maps, count the terms and alternate the six ranks.

The snake lemma is the diagram chase that builds a long exact sequence.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on.
