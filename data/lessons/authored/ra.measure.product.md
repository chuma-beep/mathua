# Product measures

A product measure assigns to a rectangle the product of the measures of its sides. It extends to the product sigma-algebra.

## Rectangle measure

The measure of a rectangle is the product of its side lengths. This is the defining property of the product measure. The order of the factors does not matter.

## Product sigma-algebra

The product sigma-algebra is generated by measurable rectangles. A finite example has one rectangle for each pair of measurable sets. The count is the product of the two counts.

## Area of a product set

The product measure of a product set is the product of the factor measures. This is the finite-dimensional version of area. It is the bridge to iterated integrals.
