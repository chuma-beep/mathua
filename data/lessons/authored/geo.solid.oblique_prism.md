# Oblique prisms

An oblique prism has the same volume as a right prism with the same base and perpendicular height. The slant does not change the volume.

## Base and height

The volume of any prism is the base area times the perpendicular distance between the two parallel bases, not the slant edge length.

## Cavalieri

Parallel slices at equal heights have equal areas, so shearing a right prism into an oblique one preserves volume.

## Surface area

The lateral faces of an oblique prism are parallelograms, so the surface area differs from the right prism even though the volume does not.

To check your understanding, identify the base, compare slices and find parallelogram faces.

Slant changes the surface, never the volume.
