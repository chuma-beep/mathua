# Poincare duality

Poincare duality studies Poincare duality pairs cohomology in complementary degrees on a closed orientable manifold. The lesson separates the setup, the computation and the interpretation so practice can test each move.

## Betti numbers

Betti numbers starts from the defining objects. Identify what is given, state the governing relation and check the hypotheses before substituting numbers.

## Euler parity

Euler parity carries out the computation. Work with explicit small data, simplify exactly and keep the signs and dimensions consistent.

## Dimension

Dimension interprets the result and checks it against the definition. Compare with a second example and state what the answer means in poincare duality.

A complete answer names the relation, substitutes the data and states the result in the language of the problem. When a second method is available, compare the two results before moving on.
