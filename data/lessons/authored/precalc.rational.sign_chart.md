# Sign charts for rational functions

A sign chart records the sign of a rational function between its zeros and poles. It turns an inequality into a union of intervals.

## Test intervals

Factor the numerator and denominator. Mark the zeros and the poles on a number line. Choose one test point in each interval and evaluate the sign there.

## Zeros and poles

A zero can change the sign only when its multiplicity is odd. A pole can change the sign for the same reason. Even multiplicity leaves the sign unchanged.

## Reading the sign

The sign chart answers where the function is positive or negative. Endpoints that are zeros may be included in a non-strict inequality. Poles are never included.
