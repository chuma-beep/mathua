# Fitting an exponential model

An exponential model is fitted from two data points by solving for the growth factor and then for the initial value. The ratio of two outputs gives the factor directly.

## The ratio

Dividing the later output by the earlier one cancels the initial value and leaves the growth factor raised to the elapsed time.

## The growth factor

Take the appropriate root to recover the factor for one time unit. A factor above one means growth and a factor below one means decay.

## The initial value

Substitute one data point back into the model to solve for the initial value.

To check your understanding, divide the outputs, take the root and substitute a point.

Two points determine two parameters, which is exactly the number in the model.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller coefficients and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
