# The sum of residues

The sum of all residues of a rational function on the Riemann sphere is zero, so the residue at infinity is minus the finite sum.

## Finite sum

Sum the residues at every finite pole of a rational function.

## Infinity

The residue at infinity completes the sum, so it equals the negative of the finite total.

## Total

Including infinity, the residues of a rational function add to zero on the sphere.

To check your understanding, list the finite poles, negate the finite total and add every residue.

Residues are conserved on the sphere, with infinity carrying the balancing term.
