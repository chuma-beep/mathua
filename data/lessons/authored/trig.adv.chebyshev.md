# Chebyshev polynomials of the first kind

Chebyshev polynomials encode the multiple-angle formulas as a recurrence. They turn trigonometric identities into polynomial values and coefficients.

## The recurrence

The first two Chebyshev polynomials are T zero equal to one and T one equal to x. The recurrence T sub n plus one equals two x T sub n minus T sub n minus one generates every later polynomial.

## Values at special points

At x equal to negative one, T sub n is negative one to the n. At x equal to one, every T sub n is one. These values follow directly from the recurrence and provide quick checks.

## The leading coefficient

The leading term of T sub n is two to the n minus one times x to the n. The coefficient doubles at each step. Reading it from the recurrence is faster than expanding the polynomial.

The three sections form one chain. The first names the object, the second performs the computation and the third reads the result in context.

Practice should be answered without a calculator first. Write the relation, substitute the published values and state the integer result. Then check the result against the defining identity or a second representation before moving to the next question.
