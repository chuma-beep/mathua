# The wedge product

The wedge product multiplies differential forms. It is bilinear and alternating, so a repeated direction vanishes and reversing the order changes the sign.

## The wedge of two one-forms

For one-forms a dx plus b dy and c dx plus d dy, the wedge product expands by distributivity. The dx wedge dx and dy wedge dy terms vanish because the product is alternating. The surviving coefficient of dx wedge dy is a d minus b c, the determinant of the coefficient matrix.

## Order reversal

The alternating rule says dy wedge dx equals negative dx wedge dy. Reversing the order of two one-forms therefore changes the sign of every coefficient. The magnitude stays the same, but the oriented result does not.

## Wedge products in three dimensions

In R3, a one-form has dx, dy and dz coefficients. The wedge of two one-forms has three independent coefficients, one for each coordinate plane. The dy wedge dz coefficient is b r minus c q. It is the same determinant pattern as a cross product.

The three sections form one chain. The first names the object, the second performs the computation and the third reads the result in context. Practice asks one family from each section, so the three moves stay connected.
