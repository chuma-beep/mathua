# Toroidal graphs

Toroidal graphs embed on a surface with one handle, which admits the complete graph on seven vertices.

## The surface

The torus is orientable with genus one, so an embedding of a connected graph satisfies \(n-m+f=0\). A graph that embeds there is toroidal, and every planar graph is toroidal.

## Edge bound

A simple toroidal graph on \(n\ge 3\) vertices has at most \(3n\) edges. The bound comes from the Euler formula with every face of length at least three.

## Complete graphs

The complete graph \(K_7\) embeds on the torus and has \(21=3\cdot 7\) edges, so it meets the edge bound exactly. The complete graph \(K_8\) exceeds the bound and needs a surface of higher genus.

To check your understanding, set the Euler characteristic, bound the face lengths and count the edges of \(K_7\).

One handle raises the edge budget from \(3n-6\) to \(3n\), and \(K_7\) spends it completely.
