# Dehn functions

The Dehn function measures the area needed to fill a loop of a given length. Linear Dehn function means the group is hyperbolic, and quadratic means the filling is Euclidean.

## The line

The Dehn function of \(\mathbb{Z}\) is linear, of exponent one. A loop on a line can be contracted without leaving its length.

## The plane

The Dehn function of \(\mathbb{Z}^2\) is quadratic, of exponent two. Filling a large square costs area proportional to the square of its side.

## Hyperbolic groups

Hyperbolic groups have linear Dehn functions. The thin-triangle condition bounds the filling area by a constant times the boundary length.
