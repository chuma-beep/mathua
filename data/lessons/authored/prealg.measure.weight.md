# Converting units of weight and mass

## Ounces and Pounds

One pound holds 16 ounces. A package of 2.5 pounds weighs \\(2.5 \\times 16 = 40\\) ounces. In the other direction, 26 ounces is 1 pound and 10 ounces, since \\(26 \\div 16 = 1\\) with a remainder of 10.

The remainder stays in ounces, which is why the answer has two units. Writing 1.625 pounds is also correct, but a mixed unit reads better on a shipping label.

## Grams and Kilograms

Metric mass units step by powers of ten. One kilogram is 1,000 grams, so 2.5 kilograms is 2,500 grams. Also 0.75 kilograms is 750 grams. Divide by 1,000 to go back to kilograms.

The decimal point moves three places, which is the whole conversion. That simplicity is why science prefers metric units. It also makes an answer of 25,000 grams for 2.5 kilograms easy to spot as a slip.

## Pounds and Tons

One ton is 2,000 pounds in the customary system. A load of 3.5 tons weighs \\(3.5 \\times 2000 = 7000\\) pounds. In the other direction, 9,000 pounds is 4.5 tons.

Keep the short ton and the metric tonne apart. A metric tonne is 1,000 kilograms, close to 2,205 pounds, so the two units are similar but not interchangeable. Name the system whenever a weight crosses a border.
