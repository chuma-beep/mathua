# Annihilators

The annihilator of a subspace is the set of functionals that vanish on it. Its dimension is the codimension of the subspace.

## Dimension

If W has dimension k inside an n-dimensional space, its annihilator has dimension n minus k. This is the dual statement of rank-nullity.

## Duality

Taking annihilators reverses inclusions. The annihilator of the annihilator recovers the original subspace in finite dimension.

## Orthogonality

With an inner product, the annihilator corresponds to the orthogonal complement. The correspondence sends a functional to its Riesz vector.

To check your understanding, subtract the dimension, reverse an inclusion and identify the complement.

Annihilators turn statements about subspaces into statements about functionals without changing dimensions.
