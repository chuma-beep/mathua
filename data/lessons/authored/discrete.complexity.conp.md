# coNP

coNP is the class of problems whose complements lie in NP. A no-instance of a coNP problem has a short certificate, just as a yes-instance of an NP problem does.

## The definition

\(L\in\mathrm{coNP}\) exactly when its complement is in NP. Tautology, the complement of satisfiability, is the standard complete example.

## Certificates

A coNP certificate refutes membership. For a tautology, a falsifying assignment would be such a certificate, and none exists.

## The open question

\(\mathrm{NP}=\mathrm{coNP}\) is open. If it held, every NP problem would have short certificates for no-instances as well.

To check your understanding, complement the language, refute membership and state NP versus coNP.

coNP is not the complement of NP as a set of languages; it is the class of complements. The asymmetry between yes and no certificates is the whole content of the definition.
