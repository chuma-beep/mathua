# The Legendre transform

The Legendre transform replaces a function of a rate by a function of its conjugate slope. In mechanics it turns the Lagrangian into the Hamiltonian and exchanges velocities for momenta.

## Definition

The conjugate variable is the derivative of the original function. The transform is the product of the conjugate variable and the original argument minus the original function, evaluated at the argument that produces that conjugate.

## Involutivity

Applying the transform twice returns the original function when it is convex. The transform is therefore an involution on convex functions, which is why the Lagrangian and Hamiltonian pictures are equivalent rather than one being more fundamental.

## Mechanics

The momentum is the derivative of the Lagrangian with respect to velocity. The Hamiltonian is momentum times velocity minus the Lagrangian, and the velocity is eliminated in favour of the momentum.

To check your understanding, compute the conjugate variable, apply the transform again and identify the momentum.

The transform is the reason a mechanical system can be described by second-order equations in position or by first-order equations in phase space.
