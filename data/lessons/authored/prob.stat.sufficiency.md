# Sufficient statistics

A statistic is sufficient when the conditional distribution of the sample given the statistic does not depend on the parameter.

## Factorisation

The Fisher-Neyman criterion factors the likelihood into a term depending on the sample only through the statistic and a term free of the parameter.

## Examples

For a Bernoulli sample the sum of successes is sufficient, and for a normal sample the pair of sample mean and sample variance is sufficient.

## Meaning

A sufficient statistic carries all the information about the parameter that the sample holds.

To check your understanding, write the likelihood, count Bernoulli successes and condition on the statistic.

Sufficiency is about information, not about size.
