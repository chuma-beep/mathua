# The Weierstrass factorization theorem

The Weierstrass factorization theorem writes an entire function as a product over its zeros.

## List the zeros

The zeros of an entire function are isolated unless the function is identically zero. Their multiplicities are part of the data. The product must reproduce every zero with the correct order.

## Determine the order

The order of a zero is its multiplicity. A simple zero contributes one factor. A zero of order m contributes the factor raised to m.

## Build the product

The product uses elementary factors to make the infinite product converge. A finite number of zeros needs only the factors z minus z sub k. Infinitely many zeros need the exponential convergence factors.
