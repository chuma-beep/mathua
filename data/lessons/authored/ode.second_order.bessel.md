# Bessel functions

Bessel functions solve the Bessel equation and appear in cylindrical boundary-value problems.

## Identify the Bessel order

The order is the parameter nu in the Bessel equation. It controls the behaviour near the origin and the recurrence. Integer and half-integer orders have especially simple forms.

## Use the recurrence

The recurrence relates J sub nu plus 1, J sub nu and J sub nu minus 1. It lets a value at one order be computed from neighbouring orders. The recurrence is the discrete analogue of a derivative identity.

## Evaluate a Bessel value

Use the series or the recurrence at a chosen point. The value at zero is one for order zero and zero for positive order. The answer is a number, not a closed elementary function.
