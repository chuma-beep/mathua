# Finding a missing minuend

The minuend is the number a subtraction starts from. When it is unknown, it is recovered by adding the difference to the subtrahend.

## The method

From \(x - \frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}\), add \(\frac{a}{b}\) to both sides to obtain \(x = \frac{c}{d} + \frac{a}{b}\). Write both fractions over a common denominator, add the numerators and reduce. The unknown is always at least as large as the subtrahend when the difference is nonnegative. Addition and subtraction are inverse operations, so the recovery is exact. For \(x - \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{4}\) the minuend is \(\frac{3}{4}\).

## A worked difference

Solve \(x - \frac{3}{7} = \frac{2}{7}\). Adding gives \(x = \frac{2}{7} + \frac{3}{7} = \frac{5}{7}\). A case with unlike denominators is \(x - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{5}{6}\). Convert to twelfths: \(x = \frac{10}{12} + \frac{3}{12} = \frac{13}{12}\), which is \(1\frac{1}{12}\). The answer exceeds 1 and that is consistent with subtracting a quarter from a value near 1.1.

## Checking the result

Subtract the subtrahend from the found value. For the second case, \(\frac{13}{12} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{13}{12} - \frac{3}{12} = \frac{10}{12} = \frac{5}{6}\), which is the stated difference. In decimals the check reads \(1.0833 - 0.25 = 0.8333\), matching five sixths. A found minuend smaller than the subtrahend would contradict a positive difference.

The minuend is the subtrahend plus whatever the subtraction removed.
