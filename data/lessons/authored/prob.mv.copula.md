# Copulas

A copula separates the dependence structure of a random vector from its marginal distributions.

## Sklar's theorem

Every joint distribution can be written as a copula evaluated at the marginal distribution functions.

## Gaussian copula

The Gaussian copula applies the normal joint distribution to transformed uniforms, giving a dependence model with arbitrary margins.

## Invariance

Copulas are invariant under strictly increasing transformations of each coordinate, so they capture rank dependence only.

To check your understanding, apply the marginal CDFs, transform to uniforms and transform one coordinate.

A copula is dependence without margins.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller coefficients and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
