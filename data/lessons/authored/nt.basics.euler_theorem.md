# Euler's theorem

Euler's theorem says every unit modulo n becomes 1 when raised to the totient of n. It generalises Fermat's little theorem from primes to arbitrary moduli.

## Euler's totient

The totient phi of n counts the integers from 1 to n that are coprime to n. For a prime power p to the k it is p to the k minus p to the k minus 1. A product of coprime factors multiplies their totients.

## Reducing an exponent

When a is coprime to n, a raised to phi of n is congruent to 1 modulo n. An exponent can therefore be reduced modulo phi without changing the residue. This is the modular version of reducing an angle modulo a full turn.

## Multiples of phi

Any multiple of phi sends a unit to 1 modulo n. That fact converts a large power into a single residue. It is the reason RSA decryption returns the original message.
