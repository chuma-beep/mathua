# BCH codes

BCH codes are cyclic codes whose generator polynomial has a designed set of consecutive roots. The design distance guarantees a lower bound on the minimum distance.

## Cyclic structure

A cyclic shift of a codeword is another codeword. The code is an ideal in a polynomial ring modulo \(x^n-1\), generated by one polynomial.

## Designed distance

If the generator has \(d-1\) consecutive powers of a primitive element as roots, the minimum distance is at least \(d\).

## Decoding

Syndromes evaluate the received polynomial at the designed roots. Berlekamp-Massey or Peterson decoding then locates the errors.

To check your understanding, shift a codeword, count consecutive roots and evaluate syndromes.

BCH codes turn an algebraic root pattern into a distance guarantee. The designed distance is often met exactly for small lengths.
