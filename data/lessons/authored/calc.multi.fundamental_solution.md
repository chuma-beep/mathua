# The fundamental solution

The fundamental solution of the Laplacian is the potential produced by a point source. Its singularity encodes the delta function on the right-hand side.

## The power law

In dimension n, the fundamental solution grows or decays like r to the power 2 minus n. In two dimensions it is logarithmic. The exponent is the only part that depends on dimension.

## Normalisation

A normalising constant makes the Laplacian equal the delta function rather than a multiple of it. The constant depends on the surface area of the unit sphere. It does not change the location of the singularity.

## Source flux

The flux of the fundamental solution through a small circle or sphere equals the source strength. This is the divergence theorem applied to a punctured domain. It identifies the coefficient of the delta function.
