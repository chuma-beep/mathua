# The first variation

The first variation measures how a functional changes under a small perturbation of its argument. Setting it to zero gives the stationary condition.

## Variation of a functional

Perturb the curve by a small function delta y. The linear term in the expansion is the first variation. It contains an interior term and a boundary term after integration by parts.

## Stationary path

A stationary path makes the interior coefficient vanish. For the length functional, the stationary paths are straight lines. The slope is fixed by the endpoints.

## Boundary term

The boundary term involves y prime times delta y at the endpoints. If the variation vanishes at the endpoints, the boundary term disappears. This is the standard fixed-endpoint condition.
