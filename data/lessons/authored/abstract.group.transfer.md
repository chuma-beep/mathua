# The transfer homomorphism

The transfer is a homomorphism from a group to the abelianization of a subgroup. It turns index and Sylow data into constraints on simple groups.

## Construction

Choose coset representatives and multiply the representatives that appear when a group element acts on the cosets.

## Kernel

The transfer controls the intersection of the subgroup with the commutator subgroup.

## Sylow data

The number \(n_p\) of Sylow \(p\)-subgroups is congruent to one modulo \(p\) and divides the index of a Sylow subgroup.

To check your understanding, choose representatives, compute the commutator and apply the congruence.

The transfer converts a subgroup of small index into an abelian quotient.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller numbers and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
