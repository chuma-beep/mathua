# The inverse logistic function

The logistic function maps a real input into the interval between zero and one. Its inverse is the logit, which returns the original input.

## The logit

The logit of p is ln of p divided by one minus p. It is defined only for probabilities strictly between zero and one. The logit is positive above one half and negative below it.

## Inverting the logistic

Set p equal to the logistic value and solve for the input. The exponential form isolates the odds. Taking a logarithm gives the original input back.

## The restricted range

The logistic never reaches zero or one. The logit therefore has no value at the endpoints. A probability of one half gives a logit of zero.
