# Kronecker products

The Kronecker product builds a larger matrix from every pair of entries. It is the tensor product written in coordinates.

## Dimensions

If A is m by n and B is p by q, then A tensor B is m p by n q. A 2x2 tensor a 2x2 is therefore 4x4. The dimensions multiply independently in each direction.

## Entries

Each entry a sub i j of A is replaced by the whole block a sub i j times B. The (1,1) entry of the product is a sub 1 1 times b sub 1 1. The block structure repeats across the larger matrix.

## Trace

The trace of a Kronecker product is the product of the traces. Every diagonal block contributes the trace of B scaled by a diagonal entry of A. Summing the blocks gives trace A times trace B.
