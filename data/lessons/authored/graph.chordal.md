# Chordal graphs

A chordal graph has no induced cycle of length four or more. Every chordal graph has a perfect elimination ordering, and the ordering exposes its clique structure.

## Perfect elimination orderings

An ordering is a perfect elimination ordering when the later neighbours of every vertex form a clique. A graph is chordal exactly when such an ordering exists. The ordering is a certificate that can be checked vertex by vertex.

## Chordless cycles

A chordless cycle is an induced cycle with no edge between nonconsecutive vertices. Chordal graphs are exactly the graphs with no chordless cycle of length at least four. Checking all small induced subgraphs finds such a cycle in a finite graph.

## Clique trees

The maximal cliques of a chordal graph can be arranged in a clique tree. Consecutive cliques intersect, and the tree records how the graph decomposes. Counting maximal cliques is a direct clique-enumeration task on a small graph.
