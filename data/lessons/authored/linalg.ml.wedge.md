# Wedge products of vectors

The wedge product combines vectors into alternating multivectors. A wedge of k vectors is nonzero exactly when they are linearly independent.

## Construction

The wedge product is bilinear and alternating. In coordinates the components of a wedge are the minors of the matrix whose columns are the vectors.

## Independence

A wedge of k vectors vanishes exactly when the vectors are dependent. The top wedge in dimension n therefore detects a basis.

## Grading

The k-th exterior power has dimension C(n,k). Its basis is indexed by k-element subsets of a basis of the original space.

To check your understanding, expand a wedge, detect dependence and count k-subsets.

The wedge product packages determinants into a graded algebra.
