# Density and volume

## Density from mass and volume

Density is mass divided by volume. The units are mass units per volume unit, such as grams per cubic centimetre. Divide after converting both quantities to compatible units.

A large density means a lot of mass is packed into a small volume. A small density means the opposite.

## Mass from density

Multiply density by volume to recover mass. This is the inverse of the density formula. Keep the volume units consistent with the density units.

Check the size of the result. A dense object with a large volume should have a large mass.

## Volume from density

Divide mass by density to recover volume. The result is in the volume unit used by the density. If the units are mixed, convert before dividing.

Check by multiplying the recovered volume by the density. The product must return the original mass.

A final check is to restate the answer in the units and context of the original question. If the result is plausible and the units match, the calculation is ready.
