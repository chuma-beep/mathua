# Roots and multiplicity

A root of multiplicity k accounts for k factors, and the multiplicities sum to the degree.

## Multiplicity

The multiplicity is the largest \(k\) such that \((z-r)^{k}\) divides the polynomial.

## Distinct roots

The number of distinct roots is the degree minus the sum of the repetitions beyond the first.

## Degree count

The multiplicities of all distinct roots add up to the degree, which is the factorisation statement again.

To check your understanding, divide by the factor, count the distinct values and sum the multiplicities.

Multiplicity keeps the root count equal to the degree.
