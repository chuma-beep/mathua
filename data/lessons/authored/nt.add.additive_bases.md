# Additive bases

An additive basis of order h represents every positive integer as a sum of h elements of the set. Waring's problem is the statement that powers form such a basis.

## The order

The order of a basis is the smallest h for which every integer is a sum of h elements. Squares have order four by Lagrange's theorem.

## Counting elements

A basis of order h cannot be too sparse. Counting the possible sums of h elements bounds the size of the set below the h-th root of the target.

## Examples

Cubes have order nine, and the general power case is Waring's problem. The positive integers themselves form a basis of order one.
