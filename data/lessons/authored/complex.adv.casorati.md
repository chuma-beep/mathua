# The Casorati-Weierstrass theorem

Near an essential singularity an analytic function comes arbitrarily close to every complex value.

## Identify an essential singularity

Look at the Laurent series at the point. An essential singularity has infinitely many negative-power terms. A pole has only finitely many.

## Describe the local image

Every neighbourhood of an essential singularity has an image dense in the complex plane. The function does not need to attain the value. It only needs to come arbitrarily close.

## Apply Casorati-Weierstrass

The theorem rules out a removable singularity or a pole when the image is not dense. It is weaker than Picard's theorem, which gives an omitted value.
