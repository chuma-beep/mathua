# The nine-point circle

The nine-point circle passes through the three side midpoints, the three feet of the altitudes and the three midpoints from the orthocentre to the vertices.

## The nine points

Three midpoints of the sides, three altitude feet and three midpoints of the segments from the orthocentre to the vertices all lie on one circle.

## The radius

The nine-point circle has half the radius of the circumcircle. Its centre is the midpoint between the orthocentre and the circumcentre.

## The Euler line

The nine-point centre lies on the Euler line, halfway from the orthocentre to the circumcentre. This places it among the classical triangle centres.

To check your understanding, count the midpoints, halve the circumradius and place the centre.

Nine special points share one circle of half the circumradius.
