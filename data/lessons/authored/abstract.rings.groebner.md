# Groebner bases

A Groebner basis turns ideal membership into a division algorithm. Its leading terms generate the initial ideal, whose standard monomials give a basis of the quotient.

## Leading terms

A monomial order chooses a leading term for every polynomial, and the initial ideal collects those leading terms.

## Standard monomials

The monomials outside the initial ideal form a basis of the quotient ring.

## Monomial ideals

For the ideal \((x^a,y^b)\), the quotient has exactly \(ab\) standard monomials.

To check your understanding, choose an order, list the monomials and multiply the exponents.

The initial ideal carries the size and structure of the quotient.
