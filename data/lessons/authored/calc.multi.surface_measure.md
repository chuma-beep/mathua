# Surface measure

Surface measure is the area element on a parametrised surface. It is the length of the cross product of the parameter derivatives times the parameter area element.

## The area element

For r(u,v), the area element is the length of r_u cross r_v du dv. The cross product is normal to the surface. Its length converts parameter area into surface area.

## Area of a graph

For a graph z equal to f(x,y), the area element is the square root of one plus the squared partial derivatives. Integrating it over the domain gives the surface area. The formula is the graph version of the general area element.

## Scaling

Rescaling the parameters changes the cross product and the area element. A linear scaling by c in one direction and d in another multiplies area by c d. The geometric surface area is unchanged when the parametrisation is reparameterised without stretching.
