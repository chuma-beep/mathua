# Verifying inverse functions by composition

Two functions are inverses when each composition returns the input. Composition checks the inverse relationship without solving for a formula.

## Composition in both orders

Compute f of g of x and g of f of x at a chosen input. An inverse pair returns the original input in both orders. A mismatch in either order is enough to reject the pair.

## The inverse test

For linear functions, the composition coefficients must satisfy both the constant and the slope conditions. The composition f of g has slope m n and constant m b plus c. The pair is inverse only when the product of slopes is one and the constant cancels.

## Domain restrictions

An inverse pair may be valid only on a restricted domain. The composition test still works on that domain. State the restriction before claiming the functions are inverses everywhere.
