# Pentagonal numbers

Pentagonal numbers are the five-sided case of the polygonal family. They also appear in Euler's partition recurrence and in the theory of modular forms.

## The pentagonal sequence

The nth pentagonal number is n times 3n minus 1 divided by 2. The sequence begins 1, 5, 12, 22 and 35. It is the s equal to 5 case of the polygonal formula.

## The inverse index

Given a pentagonal value, solve n times 3n minus 1 divided by 2 equal to the value. The positive root is the index. For small values, testing successive indices is quicker.

## Counting pentagonal numbers

The pentagonal numbers grow quadratically, so only about the square root of a bound of them fit below it. Count by generating terms until the next one exceeds the bound. The count is the index of the largest term that fits.
