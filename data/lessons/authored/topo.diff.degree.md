# The degree of a smooth map

The degree of a map between closed oriented manifolds of the same dimension counts preimages with sign. It is invariant under homotopy and computes the induced map on top homology.

## The circle

The map \(z \mapsto z^k\) on the circle has degree \(k\). Each regular value has \(k\) preimages, all with the same sign.

## The antipodal map

The antipodal map on \(S^n\) has degree \((-1)^{n+1}\). It is orientation preserving on odd-dimensional spheres and reversing on even-dimensional ones.

## Constant maps

A constant map has degree zero because no regular value has a preimage. Degree zero is also the value for any map that factors through a lower-dimensional space.

To check your understanding, read the exponent, compute the exponent and count the preimages.

Degree is the signed count of preimages.
