# Tensor products of vector spaces

The tensor product of two vector spaces is the universal target for bilinear maps. Its dimension multiplies, which is why a tensor is indexed by a pair of indices.

## Dimension

If U has dimension m and V has dimension n, then U tensor V has dimension mn. A basis is the set of all tensor products of basis vectors.

## Universality

Every bilinear map on U times V factors uniquely through the tensor product. This property defines the tensor product up to canonical isomorphism.

## Indexing

An element of the tensor product is a formal sum of pure tensors. In coordinates it is an m by n array, not an m by n matrix of operators.

To check your understanding, multiply the dimensions, factor a bilinear map and expand a pure tensor.

The tensor product turns bilinear algebra into ordinary linear algebra on a larger space.
