# The Wilcoxon signed-rank test

The Wilcoxon signed-rank test compares paired differences without assuming normality.

## Rank the absolute differences

Discard zero differences. Rank the remaining absolute differences from smallest to largest. Tied ranks receive the average rank.

## Sum the signed ranks

Attach the sign of each difference to its rank. Sum the positive ranks and the negative ranks separately. The test statistic is usually the smaller of the two sums.

## Read the test conclusion

Compare the statistic with a table or a normal approximation. A small statistic rejects the null hypothesis of a symmetric median difference. The conclusion is about the median, not the mean.
