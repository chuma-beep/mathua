# Confidence procedures

A confidence interval is a random range that covers the parameter with a stated long-run frequency.

## Definition

A \(1-\alpha\) interval covers the parameter with probability \(1-\alpha\) under repeated sampling, before the data are seen.

## Construction

Pivotal quantities, such as the standardised sample mean, give intervals by inverting a probability statement.

## Interpretation

After the data are observed the interval either covers the parameter or does not; the confidence is a property of the procedure.

To check your understanding, fix the coverage level, find a pivot and condition on the data.

Confidence is a property of the procedure, not of one interval.
