# Regular tetrahedra

A regular tetrahedron has four equilateral triangular faces. It is the simplest Platonic solid and the three-dimensional simplex.

## Faces and vertices

A tetrahedron has four faces, four vertices and six edges. Each vertex meets three faces.

## The height

The height from a vertex meets the opposite face at its centroid. The volume is one third of the base area times the height.

## Symmetry

Every pair of vertices is joined by an edge, so the tetrahedron is a complete graph on four vertices. Its symmetry group permutes the four vertices.

To check your understanding, count the faces, find the base centroid and join every pair.

Four triangles make the smallest Platonic solid.
