# Making ten

Making ten is a mental addition strategy. It breaks one addend into the amount needed to reach ten and the amount left over.

## The complement to ten

For an addend a, the complement to ten is ten minus a. Adding that complement first produces a friendly ten. The complement is the missing addend in the equation a plus something equals ten.

## Adding across ten

To add a and b, first add the complement of a. The remaining amount is b minus that complement. The total is ten plus the remainder.

## The amount above ten

The amount above ten is a plus b minus ten. It is positive when the sum crosses ten. This quantity is the part left after the friendly ten is formed.

The three sections form one chain. The first names the object, the second performs the computation and the third reads the result in context.

Practice should be answered without a calculator first. Write the relation, substitute the published values and state the integer result. Then check the result against the defining identity or a second representation before moving to the next question.
