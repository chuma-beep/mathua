# Apothem and area

The apothem of a regular polygon is the perpendicular distance from its centre to a side. It turns the polygon into congruent isosceles triangles.

## The apothem

A regular polygon with \(n\) sides and side length \(s\) has apothem \(a\). The radius, the apothem and half a side form a right triangle.

## The perimeter

The perimeter is \(P=ns\), so the area \(\tfrac12 aP\) is the apothem times half the perimeter.

## Worked count

For a square of side \(s\), the apothem is \(s/2\) and the area is \(s^2\), which the formula reproduces.

To check your understanding, name the apothem, multiply sides by length and check the square.

The apothem converts a regular polygon into triangles whose areas add up.
