# Multivariable Fourier transform

The multivariable Fourier transform decomposes a function of several variables into frequencies. On a finite grid it becomes a finite sum with alternating signs.

## Zero frequency

The zero-frequency coefficient is the sum of all values on the grid. It measures the total mass of the signal. For a 2 by 2 array it is the sum of the four entries.

## An alternating coefficient

The coefficient at frequency one in the first direction alternates the signs across that direction. For a 2 by 2 array it is f00 minus f01 plus f10 minus f11. The pattern is the discrete version of a cosine wave.

## The inverse transform

The inverse transform reconstructs the array from its coefficients. Each value is an average of the coefficients with alternating signs. The two transforms are inverses up to the normalising factor.

The three sections form one chain. The first names the object, the second performs the computation and the third reads the result in context. Practice asks one family from each section, so the three moves stay connected.
