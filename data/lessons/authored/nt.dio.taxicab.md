# Taxicab numbers

A taxicab number is the smallest integer expressible as a sum of two positive cubes in a given number of ways. The first few are famous for their size.

## Two cubes

The smallest number that is a sum of two positive cubes in two ways is one thousand seven hundred and twenty-nine. Its representations are one cubed plus twelve cubed and nine cubed plus ten cubed.

## Three ways

The smallest number with three representations is eighty-seven million five hundred thirty-nine thousand three hundred nineteen. The count grows explosively with the number of representations.

## Checking a representation

A single representation is verified by computing two cubes and adding them. Verifying minimality requires checking every smaller integer, which is why the numbers are large.

To check your understanding, cube the pair, count representations and compute both cubes.

Taxicab numbers measure how often sums of two cubes collide, and collisions become rare as the bound grows.
