# Analytic continuation

Analytic continuation extends a power series beyond its original disk along paths that avoid singularities.

## Continue a power series

Re-expand the function around a new centre inside the old disk. The new series has its own radius. Repeating the process follows a path through the domain.

## Find the new radius

The radius is the distance from the new centre to the nearest singularity. The singularity may be a pole, a branch point or a natural boundary. The radius is not changed by the old series once the new centre is chosen.

## Identify the obstruction

A singularity blocks continuation along some paths. A branch point may allow continuation around it but not through it. A natural boundary blocks every path.
