# Trade-off rates

A trade-off rate states how much of one objective must be given up to improve another. It is the local slope of the front.

## Marginal rate

Along the front the rate is the change in one objective divided by the change in the other. At a smooth point it is the derivative of the front.

## Units

A rate inherits the units of the two objectives. Comparing rates across pairs therefore requires a common unit or a normalisation.

## Reading a front

Two nearby front points give a finite-difference slope. A shallow slope means the second objective is cheap to improve and a steep slope means it is expensive.

To check your understanding, compute a slope, attach units and use two points.

A trade-off rate is what a decision maker actually reads off a front.
