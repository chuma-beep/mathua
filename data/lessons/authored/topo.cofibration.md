# Cofibrations

Cofibrations studies a cofibration lets homotopies extend from a subspace to the whole space. The lesson separates the setup, the computation and the interpretation so practice can test each move.

## The mapping cylinder

The mapping cylinder starts from the defining objects. Identify what is given, state the governing relation and check the hypotheses before substituting numbers.

## Homotopy extension

Homotopy extension carries out the computation. Work with explicit small data, simplify exactly and keep the signs and dimensions consistent.

## Pushouts

Pushouts interprets the result and checks it against the definition. Compare with a second example and state what the answer means in cofibrations.

A complete answer names the relation, substitutes the data and states the result in the language of the problem. When a second method is available, compare the two results before moving on.
