# The Mittag-Leffler theorem

The Mittag-Leffler theorem builds a meromorphic function from its prescribed principal parts at its poles.

## List the poles

The poles are isolated and have prescribed orders. Each pole has a principal part, a finite sum of negative powers. The theorem constructs a function with exactly those poles.

## Find the principal parts

The principal part is the negative-power part of the Laurent series at the pole. For cosecant at k pi the pole is simple. Its residue is minus one raised to k.

## Build the series

The Mittag-Leffler expansion sums the principal parts plus convergence terms. The convergence terms are polynomials chosen to make the sum converge. The result is meromorphic with the prescribed poles.
