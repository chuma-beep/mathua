# Related rates: shadow length

A shadow problem relates the motion of a person to the motion of the shadow tip through similar triangles.

## Similar triangles

## Shadow rate

## The tip distance

The shadow tip distance is the sum of the person's distance and the shadow length. Differentiating gives the tip rate. The ratio of the tip rate to the person's rate is constant.
