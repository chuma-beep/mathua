# The midpoint formula

The midpoint of a segment averages the coordinates of its endpoints. It is the point halfway along the segment in both directions.

## The formula

The midpoint coordinates are the averages of the corresponding endpoint coordinates.

## Missing endpoint

Doubling the midpoint and subtracting the known endpoint recovers the other endpoint.

## Three dimensions

The same average applies to each coordinate, so the formula extends to space without a new idea.

To check your understanding, average the x values, double the midpoint and average each coordinate.

A midpoint is an average, so it always lies between the endpoints coordinate by coordinate.
