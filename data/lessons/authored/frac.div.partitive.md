# Sharing division with fractions

Sharing division, also called partitive division, splits a known amount into a known number of equal shares. The quotient is the size of one share.

## The method

Divide the amount by the number of shares: an amount \(\frac{a}{b}\) shared among \(n\) gives \(\frac{a}{b} \div n = \frac{a}{bn}\). The number of shares is a positive whole number, so it is never zero. The share is smaller than the amount whenever \(n\) exceeds 1. The denominator grows by the number of shares, which is why the share shrinks.

## A worked quotient

Share \(\frac{3}{4}\) among 3 people. Each share is \(\frac{3}{4} \div 3 = \frac{3}{12} = \frac{1}{4}\). The answer is smaller than the amount, as sharing must be. A second case shares \(\frac{2}{3}\) among 4, giving \(\frac{2}{12} = \frac{1}{6}\) per person.

## Checking the result

Multiply one share by the number of recipients: \(3 \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{4}\), which restores the amount. For the second case, \(4 \times \frac{1}{6} = \frac{4}{6} = \frac{2}{3}\). A share larger than the amount would contradict the division, so the direction of the check matters.

Sharing division asks for the size of one share and multiplication by the number of shares rebuilds the whole.
