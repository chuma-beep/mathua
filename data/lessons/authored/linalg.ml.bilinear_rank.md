# Rank of a bilinear form

A bilinear form has a rank, the rank of any matrix that represents it. The rank measures how much of the form survives after a change of basis.

## Matrix

Fixing bases turns a bilinear form into a matrix B. A change of basis sends B to P transpose times B times P, which preserves rank.

## Nullity

The rank plus the nullity equals the dimension. The nullity counts directions that pair to zero with every vector.

## Degeneracy

A form is non-degenerate exactly when its matrix is invertible. Degeneracy is a basis-independent property, not an artefact of coordinates.

To check your understanding, represent the form, apply rank-nullity and test invertibility.

Rank is the first invariant of a bilinear form, and non-degeneracy is its first qualitative distinction.
