# The Euler-Maclaurin formula

The Euler-Maclaurin formula relates a sum to an integral plus correction terms. It is the basis of many numerical integration rules.

## The formula

The sum from one to n of f of k equals the integral from one to n plus half the endpoint terms plus Bernoulli corrections. The first correction is the difference of endpoint derivatives. Later terms involve higher derivatives.

## Correction term

For a linear function, the integral and the sum differ by a simple endpoint term. The correction is not zero in general. The formula becomes exact when the higher derivatives vanish.

## Sum versus integral

The integral is the leading approximation. The correction accounts for the discrete sampling. The error term is controlled by the next derivative.
