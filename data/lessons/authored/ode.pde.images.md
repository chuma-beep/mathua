# The method of images

The method of images enforces a boundary condition by adding a mirror source outside the domain.

## Place the image source

Reflect the real source across the boundary. The image has the sign that makes the boundary value cancel or match. Its position is the mirror point of the real source.

## Enforce the boundary condition

The potential is the sum of the real and image contributions. On the boundary the two contributions combine to the prescribed value. This is why the image is placed outside the physical domain.

## Evaluate the potential

At a point inside the domain, add the distances to the real and image sources. The resulting expression satisfies Laplace's equation and the boundary condition. The method works best for simple geometries such as a line or a plane.
