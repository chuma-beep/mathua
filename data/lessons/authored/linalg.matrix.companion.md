# Companion matrices

A companion matrix turns a monic polynomial into a matrix whose characteristic polynomial is that polynomial.

## Companion matrix

For a monic quadratic with coefficients b and c, the companion matrix has first row 0 and 1 and second row minus c and minus b. Its characteristic polynomial is lambda squared plus b lambda plus c. The construction generalises to any degree.

## Trace

The trace of the 2x2 companion matrix is minus b. The sum of the eigenvalues is therefore minus b. The constant term c is the product of the eigenvalues.

## Characteristic polynomial

The characteristic polynomial is the determinant of lambda I minus C. For the 2x2 companion it is lambda squared plus b lambda plus c. The coefficients can be read directly from the matrix.
