# Trace identities

The trace is the sum of the diagonal entries. It is linear and invariant under cyclic permutations of a product.

## Trace of a product

The trace of A B equals the sum over i and j of A sub i j times B sub j i. This is the sum of the diagonal entries of the product. It also equals the trace of B A.

## Trace of a square

The trace of A squared is the sum of the diagonal entries of A times A. It is not the square of the trace in general. The difference measures the failure of the entries to commute.

## Linearity

The trace of a sum is the sum of the traces. The trace of a scalar multiple is the scalar times the trace. These two rules make the trace a linear functional on matrices.
