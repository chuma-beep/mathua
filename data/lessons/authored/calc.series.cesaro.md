# Cesaro summability

Cesaro summability assigns a limit to some series that do not converge in the ordinary sense. It averages the partial sums instead of taking their limit directly.

## The Cesaro mean

The nth Cesaro mean is the average of the first n partial sums. A series is Cesaro summable when these averages converge. Ordinary convergence implies Cesaro convergence with the same sum.

## Convergence tests

A series that converges ordinarily is Cesaro summable. A series that oscillates symmetrically can be Cesaro summable even when its partial sums have no limit. The converse fails in general.

## The Cesaro sum

The Cesaro sum is the limit of the averages of the partial sums. For a convergent series it equals the ordinary sum. For a symmetric oscillating series it can be a finite value such as zero.
