# Vector fields and flows

An autonomous system is a vector field, and its solutions are the curves that follow the field. The flow map collects the position at time t into a single map.

## The field

The right-hand side assigns a velocity to every point of the phase space. A solution is a curve whose tangent at each point equals that velocity, so the field is drawn as a set of arrows.

## The flow

The flow maps an initial point to its position after time t. It satisfies the group property that waiting s then t equals waiting s plus t, and it exists for all time when the field grows at most linearly.

## Orbits

The orbit is the image of one trajectory, with the time parameter forgotten. Distinct orbits cannot cross, because a crossing point would have two different velocities unless the field vanishes there.

To check your understanding, read the velocity at a point, write the flow map and trace one trajectory.

The flow is the object that turns a differential equation into a dynamical system, and every later concept is a property of that map.
