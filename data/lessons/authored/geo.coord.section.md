# The section formula

## Dividing a segment in a ratio

The section formula finds a point that divides a segment in a given ratio. For A = (x1, y1) and B = (x2, y2), a point P that divides AB in the ratio m:n has coordinates ((n x1 + m x2)/(m+n), (n y1 + m y2)/(m+n)). The ratio counts parts from A to P and from P to B. If m = 2 and n = 3, P is closer to A, at two fifths of the way from A to B.

## The midpoint as a special case

A midpoint divides a segment in the ratio 1:1. The section formula becomes the average of the endpoints: ((x1 + x2)/2, (y1 + y2)/2). If A = (2, 5) and B = (8, 1), the midpoint is (5, 3). The midpoint is the natural check for the general formula because the two weights are equal.

## Recovering an endpoint

If P, A and the ratio are known, recover B by reversing the division. The vector from A to P is m parts of the full vector, so the full vector is (m+n)/m times AP. Then B = A plus the full vector. Checking that P lies between A and B catches a reversed ratio. The same vector method works for negative coordinates.
