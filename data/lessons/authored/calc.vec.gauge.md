# Gauge transformations

A gauge transformation adds the gradient of a scalar function to a vector potential. The magnetic field, which is the curl of the potential, does not change.

## Adding a gradient

If A is a vector potential and g is a scalar function, the new potential is A plus grad g. Each component changes by the corresponding partial derivative of g. The field is the curl of the potential.

## The field is unchanged

The curl of a gradient is zero. Adding grad g therefore adds zero to the curl of A. The physical field described by the potential is unchanged.

## The gauge function

The scalar function g is called the gauge. Its value at a point is not physical by itself. Only differences of g and the resulting change in the potential matter.
