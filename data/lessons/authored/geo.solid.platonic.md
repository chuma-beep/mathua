# Platonic solids

There are exactly five Platonic solids: the tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, dodecahedron and icosahedron. Each has congruent regular faces and the same number of faces at every vertex.

## The five solids

The tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, dodecahedron and icosahedron exhaust the convex regular polyhedra. The proof uses the angle sum at a vertex being below a full turn.

## Faces and duals

The cube and octahedron are dual, as are the dodecahedron and icosahedron. The tetrahedron is self-dual.

## Euler's formula

Every Platonic solid satisfies vertices minus edges plus faces equal two. The counts are fixed by the number of faces meeting at a vertex.
