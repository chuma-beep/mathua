# Dirichlet series of arithmetic functions

A Dirichlet series encodes an arithmetic function as a sum over the positive integers. Multiplication of series becomes Dirichlet convolution of the coefficients.

## Definition

For a sequence a(n), the Dirichlet series is the sum of a(n) n^{-s}. It converges in a right half-plane, and its abscissa of convergence depends on how fast a(n) grows.

## Products and convolution

The product of two Dirichlet series has coefficients given by Dirichlet convolution. This is why the divisor function generates the square of the zeta function.

## Coefficients of powers

In the k-th power of the zeta function, the coefficient at a prime power p^a is the binomial coefficient that counts ordered factorisations into k parts.

A Dirichlet series turns convolution into multiplication, and prime powers expose the binomial coefficients behind that product.
