# The mean value property

A harmonic function equals its average over every circle or sphere centered at the point. This property characterizes harmonic functions among continuous functions.

## Average on a circle

For a harmonic function, the average of its values on a circle is the value at the center. For a linear function, the deviations from the center cancel in pairs. The radius does not change the average.

## Value at the centre

The center value is the common average. This gives a way to recover an interior value from boundary data. It also explains why harmonic functions cannot have interior peaks.

## Four cardinal points

Averaging the values at four equally spaced points on a circle gives the same center value for a linear harmonic function. This is a discrete shadow of the mean value property.
