# Centroids and averages

The centroid of a triangle is the average of its vertices, and in complex notation that is a single arithmetic mean.

## Average

The centroid of vertices \(a,b,c\) is \((a+b+c)/3\).

## Coordinates

The real and imaginary parts average separately, so the coordinates of the centroid are the coordinate averages.

## Invariance

Affine maps send centroids to centroids, which is why the centroid is independent of the chosen origin.

To check your understanding, sum the vertices, average the real parts and apply an affine map.

A centroid is an average, and complex addition performs the average coordinate by coordinate.
