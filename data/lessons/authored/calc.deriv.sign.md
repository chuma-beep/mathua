# Derivatives of sign functions

The sign function is minus one for negative inputs, zero at zero and one for positive inputs. It is constant on each side of zero.

## The sign function

The derivative is zero for every nonzero input. At zero the function jumps from minus one to one. The derivative does not exist at the jump.

## The jump

The jump size is two. The function is not continuous at zero, so it cannot be differentiable there. The derivative exists on both open sides.

## Products with sign

A product involving the sign function may have a product-rule derivative away from zero. The sign is constant there. At zero the product may still be differentiable if the other factor vanishes.
