# Euler line

The centroid, circumcentre and orthocentre of a triangle are collinear. The centroid divides the segment from the orthocentre to the circumcentre in the ratio two to one.

## Three centres

The centroid, the circumcentre and the orthocentre lie on one line, called the Euler line. An equilateral triangle collapses all three to one point.

## The ratio

The centroid is twice as far from the orthocentre as from the circumcentre. Equivalently it divides the segment in the ratio two to one.

## Special triangles

In an isosceles triangle the Euler line is the axis of symmetry. In a right triangle the orthocentre is the right-angle vertex.

To check your understanding, name the centres, measure from the centroid and use symmetry.

Three classical centres always share a line.
