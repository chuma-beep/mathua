# The law of sines

In any triangle the ratio of a side to the sine of its opposite angle is the same for all three sides. The common ratio is twice the circumradius.

## The rule

Side a over sine A equals side b over sine B equals side c over sine C, with each side opposite its named angle.

## The ambiguous case

When two sides and a non-included angle are given, the sine can produce two possible angles. Check whether the supplementary angle also fits the triangle.

## Circumference

The common ratio equals twice the circumradius, which links the rule to the circle through all three vertices.

To check your understanding, pair side and angle, find the sine and use the common ratio.

Pair each side with the angle opposite it, never with an adjacent angle.
