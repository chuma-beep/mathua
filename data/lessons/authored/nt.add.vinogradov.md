# Vinogradov's theorem

Vinogradov proved that every sufficiently large odd integer is a sum of three primes. It is the odd case of Goldbach's conjecture, settled asymptotically.

## The statement

There is a bound beyond which every odd integer is a sum of three primes. The bound is effective but enormous in the original proof.

## The method

The proof uses the circle method. Major arcs give the main term, and minor arcs are bounded by exponential sum estimates.

## Small cases

The theorem says nothing below its bound. Small odd integers are checked directly, and seven is the first odd integer above five.

To check your understanding, state the parity, split major and minor arcs and find the first odd case.

Vinogradov's theorem is Goldbach for odd numbers in the large, and the circle method is the engine that proves it.
