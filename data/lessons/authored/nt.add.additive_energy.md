# Additive energy

Additive energy counts the solutions of a plus b equals c plus d inside a set. It measures how many additive coincidences the set contains.

## The definition

The energy is the number of ordered quadruples with equal sums. Every quadruple is counted, including the trivial ones where the pairs coincide.

## Bounds

The energy is at least the cube of the set size and at most the cube of the set size times the set size. The lower bound comes from the trivial quadruples.

## Structure

Large energy forces additive structure, and small energy is a pseudorandomness condition. The energy is therefore a bridge between the two extremes.

To check your understanding, form all sums, lower bound and force a progression.

Additive energy counts coincidences, and coincidences are exactly what structural theorems try to force or forbid.
