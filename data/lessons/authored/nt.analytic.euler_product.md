# The Euler product

The Euler product factors a Dirichlet series over the primes. It is the bridge between additive questions about integers and multiplicative questions about prime factors.

## The identity

The zeta function equals the product over primes of one minus p^{-s} inverted. Expanding each geometric factor recovers unique factorisation into primes.

## Truncation

Cutting the product at a prime bound leaves only integers whose prime factors are all below the bound. The number of such exponents up to a cap is a product of binomial counts.

## The reciprocal

Inverting the product flips the sign of each local factor. The coefficients become the Möbius function, which records the parity of distinct prime factors.

To check your understanding, expand one prime factor, bound the primes and invert the local factor.

Every Euler product is a statement about unique factorisation, and truncating it restricts attention to smooth integers.
