# Nakayama's lemma

Nakayama's lemma studies Nakayama's lemma relates a finitely generated module to its quotient by the Jacobson radical. The lesson separates the setup, the computation and the interpretation so practice can test each move.

## The hypothesis

The hypothesis starts from the defining objects. Identify what is given, state the governing relation and check the hypotheses before substituting numbers.

## Minimal generators

Minimal generators carries out the computation. Work with explicit small data, simplify exactly and keep the signs and dimensions consistent.

## The quotient

The quotient interprets the result and checks it against the definition. Compare with a second example and state what the answer means in nakayama's lemma.
