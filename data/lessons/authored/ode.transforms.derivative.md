# Laplace transform of derivatives

The Laplace transform turns a derivative into multiplication by s minus an initial value. This is what converts an initial-value problem into algebra.

## Transform a derivative

The transform of y prime is s times the transform of y minus y at zero. For a second derivative, multiply by s squared and subtract s y at zero and y prime at zero. Each derivative adds one factor of s and one initial condition.

## Insert the initial condition

Substitute the given value of y at zero into the transform formula. The transformed equation is then an algebraic equation in the transform of y. No derivative remains.

## Solve the transformed equation

Collect the transform of y on one side. Divide by the polynomial in s that multiplies it. The inverse Laplace transform of the result is the solution in time.
