# Mersenne numbers and primes

A Mersenne number has the form 2 to the n minus 1. When it is prime, it is tied to an even perfect number by Euclid's construction.

## Mersenne numbers

The nth Mersenne number is 2 to the n minus 1. Its binary form is a string of n ones. The sequence begins 3, 7, 15, 31 and 63.

## Primality of Mersenne numbers

If 2 to the n minus 1 is prime, then n itself must be prime. The converse fails: n equal to 11 is prime, but 2047 factors as 23 times 89. A primality test is still required.

## Residues of Mersenne numbers

Computing a Mersenne number modulo m is a short repeated-squaring calculation. The residue is often easier to find than the full number. It is also the first step in a Lucas-Lehmer test.
