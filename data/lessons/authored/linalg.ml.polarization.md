# The polarization identity

The polarization identity recovers a symmetric bilinear form from its quadratic form. It expresses the pairing of two vectors as a combination of four squared norms.

## Identity

The pairing of x and y is one quarter of the sum of four quadratic values with signs. The four terms are the norms of x plus y, x minus y, x plus iy and x minus iy in the complex case.

## Real case

Over the reals two terms suffice: one quarter of the difference of the squared norms of x plus y and x minus y.

## Consequences

Polarisation shows that a norm coming from an inner product determines that inner product. It also proves that isometries preserving the norm preserve the pairing.

To check your understanding, write the four terms, use two norms and recover the pairing.

Quadratic data and symmetric bilinear data are interchangeable away from characteristic two.
