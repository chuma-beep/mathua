# The zeta functional equation

The functional equation relates the zeta function at s to its value at one minus s. It is the symmetry that organises the whole critical strip.

## The completed function

Multiplying the zeta function by a gamma factor and a power of pi produces a function invariant under s to one minus s.

## The exponent

The power of pi in the completed function is s over two. For an integer s, the numerator of that exponent is s itself.

## Consequences

The symmetry forces the trivial zeros and constrains the nontrivial ones. It also converts growth on the right into growth on the left.

To check your understanding, build the gamma factor, read the exponent and force a trivial zero.

The functional equation is a reflection symmetry, and every factor in it is there to make the reflection exact.
