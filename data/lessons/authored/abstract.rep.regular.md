# The regular representation

The regular representation lets a group act on its own group algebra. It contains every irreducible representation with multiplicity equal to its degree.

## Definition

The group acts on the group algebra by left multiplication, giving a representation of dimension equal to the group order.

## Decomposition

Each irreducible representation occurs with multiplicity equal to its degree.

## Trivial copy

The trivial representation occurs exactly once.

To check your understanding, take the group algebra, list irreducibles and find invariant vectors.

The regular representation is the universal source of all irreducibles.
