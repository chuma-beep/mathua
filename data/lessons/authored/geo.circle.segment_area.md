# Circular segments

A segment is the part of a disc between a chord and its arc. Its area is the sector minus the triangle formed by the two radii and the chord.

## Subtracting the triangle

The sector contains the triangle with the two radii and the chord as sides. Removing that triangle leaves the segment.

## The sector term

The sector area is theta over 360 times pi r squared. The triangle area is one half r squared sin theta.

## Small and large segments

A chord divides a disc into two segments whose areas add to the disc. The smaller segment uses the smaller central angle.

To check your understanding, name the two pieces, use the angle fraction and add the two segments.
