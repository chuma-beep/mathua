# Nilpotent matrices

A nilpotent matrix becomes the zero matrix after finitely many powers. Its only eigenvalue is zero and its trace and determinant both vanish.

## The square

A nilpotent matrix has a smallest positive power equal to zero. For a 2x2 matrix, a zero trace and a zero determinant are enough to make the square zero. The square is computed by the usual row-column product.

## Nilpotency index

The nilpotency index is the least k with A to the k equal to zero. The zero matrix has index 1 and every nonzero 2x2 nilpotent matrix has index 2. The index measures how many multiplications are needed to annihilate the matrix.

## Recognising nilpotence

For 2x2 matrices, trace zero and determinant zero are equivalent to nilpotence. A direct check is to square the matrix and inspect all four entries. The answer is 1 when every entry of the square is zero.
