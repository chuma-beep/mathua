# Cavalieri's principle

Cavalieri's principle says two solids with equal cross-sectional areas at every height have equal volume. The shape of the slices does not matter.

## Equal slices

If every horizontal slice of one solid has the same area as the corresponding slice of another at the same height, the volumes are equal.

## Shearing

A right cylinder can be sheared into an oblique cylinder without changing any slice area, so both have the same volume.

## Comparing pyramids

A pyramid and a cone with the same base area and height have equal volume by Cavalieri, because their slices scale identically with height.

To check your understanding, pair the heights, shear the solid and match base areas.
