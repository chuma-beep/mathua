# Banach spaces

A Banach space is a complete normed vector space. Completeness means every Cauchy sequence converges inside the space.

## Completeness test

Euclidean space is complete. Every Cauchy sequence of vectors converges to a vector in the same space. Finite dimension makes the test automatic.

## Cauchy sequences

A sequence is Cauchy when its terms eventually become close to one another. The sequence L plus one over n converges to L. The limit is the candidate for completeness.

## Finite-dimensional example

Every finite-dimensional normed space is complete. Its dimension is finite and all norms are equivalent. This supplies a large class of Banach spaces.
