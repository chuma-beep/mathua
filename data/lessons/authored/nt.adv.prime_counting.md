# The prime-counting function

The prime-counting function pi of x counts the primes at most x. It turns questions about individual primes into questions about a sequence of counts.

## The counting function

pi of x is the number of primes p with p at most x. For small x it is found by listing the primes. Its growth is the subject of the prime number theorem.

## The nth prime

The inverse view asks for the nth prime. Listing primes in order and taking the nth entry answers it for small n. The nth prime grows roughly like n log n.

## Intervals

The number of primes between a and b is pi of b minus pi of a minus 1, or a direct count in the inclusive interval. Interval questions test the definition rather than a formula.
