# Polar curves

Polar coordinates describe a point by distance and angle. Some curves, such as circles through the origin, have very short polar equations.

## Polar coordinates

A point at distance r and angle theta has x=r cos theta and y=r sin theta.

## Circles through the origin

The equation r=2a cos theta describes a circle of radius a centred at (a,0).

## Symmetry

Replacing theta by minus theta reflects the curve across the horizontal axis.

To check your understanding, read r, read the coefficient and replace theta.

Polar form is a coordinate choice, so the same curve can look simpler or harder.
