# Converting square units

## Square Feet and Square Yards

Area units square the linear factor. One yard is 3 feet, so one square yard is a square 3 feet on each side, which is \\(3 \\times 3 = 9\\) square feet. Converting 54 square feet to square yards means dividing by 9, giving 6 square yards.

Dividing by 3 instead of 9 is the common error. It leaves an answer three times too large. Say the factor aloud as nine square feet per square yard before computing.

## Square Centimeters and Square Meters

One meter is 100 centimeters, so one square meter is \\(100 \\times 100 = 10000\\) square centimeters. A sheet of 45,000 square centimeters is \\(45000 \\div 10000 = 4.5\\) square meters.

The linear factor of 100 becomes 10,000 for area. The same squaring applies to any metric area conversion. A patch of 2 square meters is 20,000 square centimeters, which sounds large until the units are compared.

## Square Inches and Square Feet

One foot is 12 inches, so one square foot is \\(12 \\times 12 = 144\\) square inches. A panel of 288 square inches is therefore \\(288 \\div 144 = 2\\) square feet.

Large factors make estimating worthwhile. A rectangle near 12 inches by 12 inches is about one square foot, so a result far from that scale signals a conversion factor applied only once instead of squared.
