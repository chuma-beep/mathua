# Permutation tests

A permutation test compares an observed statistic with the distribution obtained by relabelling the data.

## Compute the observed statistic

Choose a statistic such as the difference in group means. Compute it on the original labels. This is the value the test will compare against.

## Permute the labels

Randomly reassign the labels to the pooled observations. Recompute the statistic. Repeating this gives the permutation distribution under the null hypothesis.

## Read the permutation p-value

Count the permuted statistics at least as extreme as the observed one. Divide by the number of permutations. The result is the permutation p-value.
