# Geometric means

A geometric mean between two numbers is the middle term that makes the three numbers a geometric sequence. Its square equals the product of the endpoints.

## The mean

The geometric mean of a and b is the positive square root of their product, which lies between them when both are positive.

## Several means

Inserting k geometric means between two terms requires a common ratio whose power matches the number of steps.

## Growth averages

The geometric mean is the correct average for growth factors, because successive factors multiply rather than add.

To check your understanding, multiply the endpoints, count the gaps and multiply the factors.

Use a geometric mean for ratios and an arithmetic mean for differences.
