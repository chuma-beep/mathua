# The complex gamma function

The gamma function extends the factorial to the complex plane. It is meromorphic with simple poles at the nonpositive integers.

## Evaluate the gamma function

For a positive integer n, Gamma of n is n minus 1 factorial. The functional equation Gamma of z plus 1 equals z Gamma of z extends the values. The reflection formula relates Gamma of z and Gamma of 1 minus z.

## Use the reflection formula

The formula is Gamma of z Gamma of 1 minus z equals pi over sine of pi z. It gives values in the left half-plane from values in the right half-plane. The sine zeros produce the poles.

## Locate the poles

The poles are at 0, minus 1, minus 2 and so on. Each pole is simple. The order of the pole at minus k is one.
