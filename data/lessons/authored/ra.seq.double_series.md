# Double series

A double series sums a rectangular array of terms. It can be summed by rows, by columns or over finite grids.

## Finite rectangular sum

For a finite grid, the double sum is the sum of every entry. If the entries are i plus j, the row sums form an arithmetic progression. The total is the sum of those row sums.

## Convergence by rows

For a nonnegative array, summing each row and then summing the row totals gives the same result as summing columns first. This is Tonelli's theorem in the finite case. The order of summation does not matter.

## Absolute convergence

Absolute convergence replaces each term by its absolute value. A finite grid is always absolutely summable. The absolute sum is the ordinary sum when every term is nonnegative.
