# Transversality

Two submanifolds are transverse when their tangent spaces span the ambient tangent space at every intersection point. The intersection is then a submanifold of predictable dimension.

## Dimension of the intersection

For transverse submanifolds \(A\) and \(B\) of an \(n\)-manifold, \(\dim(A \cap B) = \dim A + \dim B - n\). Two curves in a surface meet in points when the sum equals the surface dimension.

## Codimension adds

The codimension of a transverse intersection is the sum of the codimensions. A codimension-one hypersurface meeting a curve transversely gives isolated points.

## Generic position

A small perturbation makes any pair of submanifolds transverse. When the expected dimension is negative, the generic intersection is empty.

To check your understanding, add the dimensions, add the codimensions and perturb into transverse position.

Transversality is the dimension count that makes intersections predictable.
