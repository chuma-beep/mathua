# The crosscap number

The crosscap number is the number of projective planes in a connected sum decomposition of a nonorientable surface. It plays the role that genus plays in the orientable classification.

## Euler characteristic

A closed nonorientable surface with crosscap number \(k\) has \(\chi = 2 - k\). The sphere has crosscap number zero, the projective plane one and the Klein bottle two.

## Recovering the number

Given the Euler characteristic, \(k = 2 - \chi\). A nonorientable surface with \(\chi = -2\) is the connected sum of four projective planes.

## Sums and boundaries

Crosscap numbers add under connected sum. A boundary circle lowers the Euler characteristic by one without changing the crosscap number.

To check your understanding, subtract the crosscap number from two, solve for the crosscap number and add crosscap numbers.

The crosscap number and the Euler characteristic determine each other for closed nonorientable surfaces.
