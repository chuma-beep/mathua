# Coefficients and constant terms

## Coefficients

The coefficient of a variable is the number multiplying it. In 7x the coefficient is 7. When several like terms appear, add their coefficients and keep the variable once.

A coefficient of 1 is usually not written. The expression x + 3 has coefficient 1 on x. Recognizing the hidden 1 prevents errors when combining terms.

## Constant terms

A constant term has no variable. In 4x + 9, the constant term is 9. Constants and variable terms are not like terms, so they cannot be combined into one term.

Keep the sign with the term. In 4x - 9 the constant term is -9, not 9. The sign belongs to the term that follows it.

## Counting terms

After combining like terms, count the separate pieces joined by plus or minus signs. The expression 5x + 2 has two terms. If the constant is zero, only the variable term remains.

Counting terms is a check on simplification. Combining like terms should reduce the number of variable terms without changing the number of constant terms.

A final check is to restate the answer in the units and context of the original question. If the result is plausible and the units match, the calculation is ready.
