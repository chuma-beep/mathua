# Resultants of split polynomials

The resultant of two polynomials vanishes exactly when they share a root, and for linear factors it is a product of differences.

## Definition

For monic polynomials with roots \(a_{i}\) and \(b_{j}\), the resultant is \(\prod_{i,j}(a_{i}-b_{j})\).

## Linear factors

For \((z-a)(z-b)\) and \((z-c)\), the resultant is \((a-c)(b-c)\).

## Common roots

The resultant is zero precisely when some \(a_{i}\) equals some \(b_{j}\), which is a shared root.

To check your understanding, form the root differences, list both root sets and test for a shared root.

The resultant is a single number that detects a shared factor.
