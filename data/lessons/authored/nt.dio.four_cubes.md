# Sums of four cubes

Sums of four cubes represent many integers, but a modulo nine obstruction excludes two residue classes. The problem is a famous open case of Waring's problem.

## The obstruction

A cube is congruent to zero, one or minus one modulo nine. A sum of four cubes therefore cannot be congruent to four or five modulo nine.

## Small solutions

Many small integers have explicit four-cube representations. One is one cubed plus one cubed plus minus one cubed, and the pattern extends.

## The open problem

Whether every integer avoiding the obstruction is a sum of four cubes is open. The answer for three cubes was settled only recently.

To check your understanding, list cube residues, cube each term and state the conjecture.

Four cubes are blocked by a congruence modulo nine, and clearing that block is one of the remaining hard problems in additive number theory.
