# The exterior derivative

The exterior derivative raises the degree of a form by one. It encodes the gradient, the curl and the divergence in a single operation.

## The differential of a function

For a scalar function f, the exterior derivative df is the one-form of partial derivatives. Its dx coefficient is the partial derivative with respect to x. The gradient is the vector of those coefficients.

## The derivative of a one-form

For a one-form with coefficients that depend on the coordinates, the exterior derivative differentiates each coefficient and wedges its differential with the basis form. The alternating rule cancels repeated directions. For a y dx plus b x dy, the dx wedge dy coefficient is b minus a.

## The three-dimensional exterior derivative

A two-form in R3 has coefficients on dy wedge dz, dz wedge dx and dx wedge dy. Its exterior derivative is a three-form with a single coefficient on dx wedge dy wedge dz. That coefficient is the divergence of the coefficient vector of the two-form.

The three sections form one chain. The first names the object, the second performs the computation and the third reads the result in context. Practice asks one family from each section, so the three moves stay connected.
