# Boundary connected sums

A boundary connected sum glues two surfaces along a disc in their boundary. The operation adds genera and boundary circles, but it subtracts only one from the Euler characteristic.

## Genus

The boundary connected sum of surfaces of genera \(g_1\) and \(g_2\) has genus \(g_1 + g_2\). The gluing disc lies on the boundary, so no interior handle is lost.

## Euler characteristic

One disc is removed from each surface and the two are glued along a single interval, so \(\chi = \chi_1 + \chi_2 - 1\). Compare the closed connected sum, which subtracts two.

## Boundary circles

The boundary connected sum of surfaces with \(b_1\) and \(b_2\) boundary circles has \(b_1 + b_2 - 1\) boundary circles. The two glued arcs become one.

To check your understanding, add the genera, subtract one and add and subtract one.

Boundary connected sum is the natural addition for surfaces with boundary.
