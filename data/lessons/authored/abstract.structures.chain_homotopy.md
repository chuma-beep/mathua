# Chain homotopies

A chain homotopy witnesses that two chain maps induce the same map on homology. It is the chain-level version of a homotopy.

## Definition

A homotopy \(s\) satisfies \(f-g=\partial s+s\partial\), so the two maps agree after passing to homology.

## Boundary

The boundary operator squares to zero, which is what makes homology well defined.

## Components

A homotopy on a complex of length n has one component per degree.

To check your understanding, write the relation, apply twice and index the degrees.

Chain homotopy is the correct notion of sameness for chain maps.
