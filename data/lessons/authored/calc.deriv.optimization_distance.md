# Optimization: closest point

The closest point on a line to a fixed point is found by minimizing the squared distance. The squared distance avoids the square root.

## Distance to a line

The distance from a point to a line is the perpendicular segment. The closest point is the foot of the perpendicular. Its coordinates can be found by projection or by calculus.

## The closest point

For the line y equals x, the closest point to a point on the perpendicular has coordinates equal to the average of the coordinates. The midpoint of the perpendicular segment gives the foot.

## Squared distance

Minimize the square of the distance to avoid the square root. The critical point of the squared distance is the same as the critical point of the distance. The second derivative confirms a minimum.
