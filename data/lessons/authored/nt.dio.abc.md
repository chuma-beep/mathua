# The abc conjecture

The abc conjecture bounds a sum by the product of the distinct primes dividing its parts. It is a deep statement about how multiplication and addition interact.

## The radical

The radical of an integer is the product of its distinct prime factors. It forgets multiplicity, so it is usually much smaller than the integer.

## The quality

The quality of a triple a plus b equals c compares the logarithm of c with the logarithm of the radical of the product. Values above one are exceptional.

## Consequences

The conjecture would imply finiteness results for many Diophantine equations. It would also give a new proof of Fermat's last theorem for large exponents.
