# The unit circle

The unit circle is the circle of radius one centred at the origin. A point on it at angle theta has coordinates cosine theta and sine theta.

## Coordinates

The x coordinate is the cosine and the y coordinate is the sine, so the defining identity sine squared plus cosine squared equals one is the circle equation.

## Quadrants

Signs follow the quadrant: both coordinates are positive in the first, only sine in the second, both negative in the third and only cosine in the fourth.

## Reference angles

Every angle reduces to a reference angle in the first quadrant plus a sign pattern, which produces exact values without memorising every case.

To check your understanding, read x as cosine, locate the quadrant and reduce the angle.
