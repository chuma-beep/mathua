# Dirichlet series

A Dirichlet series is a sum of coefficients divided by powers of n. Its convergence is controlled by an abscissa rather than a radius.

## Coefficients and exponents

A Dirichlet series has the form sum a_n over n to the s. The coefficients a_n and the complex exponent s both matter. A constant coefficient gives the Riemann zeta series.

## The abscissa of convergence

The set of s where the series converges is a half-plane. Its boundary is the abscissa of convergence. The series may converge conditionally on the boundary itself.

## Values at a point

Substitute a real or complex s to obtain a numerical series. Convergence at that point follows from the abscissa. The value is the sum of the resulting numerical series.
