# Long exact sequences

An exact sequence has the image of each map equal to the kernel of the next. Long exact sequences are the main computational tool that relates the homology of a space to that of a pair.

## Rank additivity

For a short exact sequence \(0 \to A \to B \to C \to 0\) of finite-rank groups, \(\operatorname{rank} B = \operatorname{rank} A + \operatorname{rank} C\). The middle rank is the sum of the outer two.

## Recovering a rank

Given two ranks in a short exact sequence, the third follows by subtraction. A sequence with middle rank seven and right rank three has left rank four.

## Length

A long exact sequence with groups in degrees zero through \(n\) has \(n+1\) groups. The number of maps between them is \(n\).

To check your understanding, add the outer ranks, subtract the known rank and count the groups.

Exactness turns ranks into arithmetic.
