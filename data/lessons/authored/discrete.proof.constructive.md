# Constructive proofs

A constructive proof of an existential statement exhibits the object it claims exists. The witness may come with an algorithm, which makes the proof computationally useful.

## Witnesses

To prove \(\exists x\,P(x)\), produce a specific \(x\) and verify \(P(x)\). The verification is part of the proof and cannot be omitted.

## Algorithms as witnesses

A procedure that computes the object for every admissible input is a constructive proof of existence. Its termination and correctness are the verification.

## Constructive logic

Some schools of mathematics accept only constructive existence. In that setting a proof by contradiction of a negated universal is not enough.

To check your understanding, exhibit the object, describe the procedure and contrast with contradiction.

Constructive existence is stronger than nonconstructive existence because it carries the object. In ordinary mathematics both are accepted, but the constructive version gives more information.
