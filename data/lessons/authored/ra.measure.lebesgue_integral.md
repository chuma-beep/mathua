# The Lebesgue integral

The Lebesgue integral extends the simple-function integral to a much larger class of functions. It agrees with the Riemann integral when both exist.

## Step functions

A step function is constant on finitely many measurable sets. Its integral is the sum of each constant times the measure of its set. The definition is the same as for simple functions.

## Comparison with Riemann

For a step function, the Riemann and Lebesgue integrals agree. The Lebesgue theory also integrates functions that are not Riemann integrable. The comparison is a consistency check, not a new value.

## Counting measure

Under counting measure, the integral is a weighted sum. The weights play the role of lengths. This finite model makes the abstract definition concrete.
