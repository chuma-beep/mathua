# Green's identities

Green's identities are integration-by-parts formulas for the Laplacian. They relate a boundary integral of u times the normal derivative of v to interior gradients and the Laplacian.

## The first identity

The first Green identity integrates u times the normal derivative of v over the boundary. The interior integral contains the dot product of the gradients plus u times the Laplacian of v. It is the divergence theorem applied to u grad v.

## Boundary term

For linear functions on the unit square, the gradients are constant. The boundary term becomes the area times their dot product. This makes the identity easy to check numerically.

## Symmetry

Swapping u and v leaves the gradient dot product unchanged. The boundary terms are symmetric when the functions are harmonic. The identity is the starting point for Green's functions.
