# Vectors in the plane

A plane vector records a displacement as two components. Addition adds components, and length comes from the Pythagorean theorem.

## Components

The vector from (x1,y1) to (x2,y2) is (x2-x1, y2-y1). Its squared length is the sum of the squared components.

## Addition

Adding two vectors adds their x components and their y components separately, so the result is another plane vector.

## Scaling

Multiplying a vector by a number scales both components, and the length scales by the absolute value of that number.

To check your understanding, subtract the coordinates, add the x components and multiply both components.

A vector is a displacement, so its components and its length are the only data it carries.
