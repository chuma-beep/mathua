# Tessellations and angle sums

## Angle sums

The interior angles of an n-gon sum to (n - 2) times 180 degrees. This follows by dividing the polygon into n - 2 triangles from one vertex.

The exterior angles of a convex polygon sum to 360 degrees. This is why the number of regular polygons meeting at a vertex is limited.

## Regular angles

In a regular polygon every interior angle has the same measure. Divide the angle sum by n to find one interior angle. The exterior angle is 180 degrees minus the interior angle.

The interior and exterior angles are supplementary at each vertex. Use this relation to check either angle.

## Polygons around a vertex

In a tessellation the angles around a vertex sum to 360 degrees. Divide 360 by the interior angle of one regular polygon to find how many copies meet at a vertex.

Only a few regular polygons tile the plane by themselves. The division must produce an integer, and that integer is the number of polygons around each vertex.

A final check is to restate the answer in the units and context of the original question. If the result is plausible and the units match, the calculation is ready.
