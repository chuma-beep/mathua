# Continuity of composite functions

A composition of continuous functions is continuous wherever the inner function lands in the domain of the outer function.

## Composition of continuous functions

If g is continuous at a and f is continuous at g of a, then f of g is continuous at a. The limit of the composition is the composition of the limits. The inner value must lie in the outer domain.

## The limit

Compute the inner limit first. If the inner limit is in the domain of the outer function, substitute it. A discontinuity in the outer function can break the conclusion.

## Domain conditions

The composition is undefined when the inner value is outside the outer domain. Continuity cannot repair an undefined value. State the domain before applying the theorem.
