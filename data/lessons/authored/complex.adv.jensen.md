# Jensen's formula

Jensen's formula relates the mean of the logarithm of the modulus on a circle to the zeros inside.

## Evaluate the integral

The integral of log modulus of f on the circle is the log of f at the centre plus the zero count. The mean-value property of harmonic functions gives the centre value. The zeros add one log term each.

## Count zeros inside

Each zero inside the circle contributes log of r over the modulus of the zero. The count is weighted by the zero positions. A zero on the boundary needs a limiting argument.

## Apply Jensen's formula

The formula converts a boundary integral into a zero count. It is a tool for entire and meromorphic functions. It also gives a growth estimate for the number of zeros.
