# Minimal surfaces

A minimal surface is a surface whose area is stationary under small deformations. Its mean curvature is zero.

## Area functional

The area of a graph is the integral of the square root of one plus the squared partial derivatives. For a plane, the integrand is constant. The area is the plane's slope factor times the domain area.

## The minimal-surface equation

The first variation of area gives the minimal-surface equation. Its coefficients involve the first and second derivatives of the height function. A plane satisfies the equation because its second derivatives vanish.

## The catenoid check

The catenoid is the surface of revolution of a catenary. It is a classic non-planar minimal surface. A plane is the simplest minimal surface and a useful check on the signs.
