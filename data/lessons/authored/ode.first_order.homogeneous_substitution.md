# Homogeneous substitutions for first-order ODEs

A homogeneous first-order equation can be turned into a separable one by substituting y equals vx.

## Identify the substitution

Write the equation so that every term has the same total degree in x and y. The substitution y equals vx makes y prime equal v plus x v prime. This separates the dependence on x from the dependence on v.

## Transform the equation

Replace y and y prime in the original equation. Collect the v terms and divide by the factor that multiplies v prime. The result is a separable equation in v and x.

## Solve the separated equation

Integrate both sides after separating dv and dx. Substitute v equals y over x back into the solution. The initial condition then fixes the constant of integration.
