# Periodic functions

A periodic function repeats its values at a fixed spacing called the period. The smallest positive spacing with that property is the fundamental period.

## Definition

A function is periodic with period p when f(x + p) = f(x) for every input x. Any multiple of a period is also a period.

## Fundamental period

The fundamental period is the smallest positive period. A graph that repeats every 4 units and also every 8 units has fundamental period 4.

## Scaled periods

For sin(bx) and cos(bx) the fundamental period is 2 pi divided by the absolute value of b, so a larger frequency shortens the period.

To check your understanding, state the repeat rule, find the smallest repeat and divide by the frequency.
