# Slope and inclination

Slope measures vertical change per unit of horizontal change. The inclination is the angle the line makes with the positive x-axis, and the two are related by the tangent.

## The formula

Slope is the difference of y coordinates divided by the difference of x coordinates, with the points taken in the same order.

## Inclination

The inclination is the angle between zero and pi whose tangent is the slope. A vertical line has inclination pi over two and no finite slope.

## Parallel and perpendicular

Parallel lines share a slope. Perpendicular lines have slopes whose product is minus one, with vertical and horizontal lines as the exceptional pair.

To check your understanding, subtract the y values, take the tangent and compare slopes.

Slope is a ratio, so it is unchanged by which two points on the line are chosen.
