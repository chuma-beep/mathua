# Hilbert spaces

A Hilbert space is a complete inner-product space. The inner product supplies angles, orthogonality and Parseval's identity.

## Inner product

The inner product of two vectors is the sum of the products of corresponding components. It is symmetric and linear in each argument. Its square is bounded by the product of the squared norms.

## Orthogonality

Two vectors are orthogonal when their inner product is zero. Orthogonal vectors satisfy the Pythagorean identity. A basis of orthogonal vectors simplifies every computation.

## Finite Parseval

For an orthonormal basis, the squared norm of a vector is the sum of the squares of its coordinates. This is Parseval's identity in finite dimensions. The identity is the Pythagorean theorem for the basis.
