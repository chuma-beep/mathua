# Completions of rings

Completion passes to inverse limits of quotients by powers of an ideal. It adds limits that the ring did not contain.

## Definition

The \(I\)-adic completion is the inverse limit of the rings \(R/I^n\).

## Example

The completion of \(\mathbb{Z}\) at \((p)\) is the ring of \(p\)-adic integers.

## Stages

The \(n\)-th stage is the quotient \(R/I^n\), and the first stage is \(R/I\).

To check your understanding, take quotients, localise at p and index the stages.

Completion replaces a ring by its formal neighbourhood.
