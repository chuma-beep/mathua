# Dedekind domains

Dedekind domains studies a Dedekind domain is an integrally closed Noetherian domain in which every nonzero ideal factors uniquely into primes. The lesson separates the setup, the computation and the interpretation so practice can test each move.

## Factorisation exponents

Factorisation exponents starts from the defining objects. Identify what is given, state the governing relation and check the hypotheses before substituting numbers.

## Unique factorisation

Unique factorisation carries out the computation. Work with explicit small data, simplify exactly and keep the signs and dimensions consistent.

## Squaring the ideal

Squaring the ideal interprets the result and checks it against the definition. Compare with a second example and state what the answer means in dedekind domains.

A complete answer names the relation, substitutes the data and states the result in the language of the problem. When a second method is available, compare the two results before moving on.
