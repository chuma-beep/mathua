# Sumsets

The sumset of two sets is the set of all pairwise sums. Its size measures additive structure, and small sumsets signal arithmetic progressions.

## The definition

The sumset A plus B contains every a plus b with a in A and b in B. Counting it is the basic problem of additive combinatorics.

## Intervals

For two intervals of integers, the sumset is an interval whose size is the sum of the sizes minus one. Overlap accounts for the missing one.

## Doubling

The ratio of the size of A plus A to the size of A is the doubling constant. A small doubling constant forces A to resemble a progression.

To check your understanding, form all sums, add the lengths and double the set.

Sumsets measure how much a set spreads under addition, and the doubling constant is the standard measure of that spread.
