# Semidirect products

A semidirect product combines two groups when one acts on the other by automorphisms. It is the smallest construction that records both a normal subgroup and a compatible action.

## The product set

The underlying set is the Cartesian product of the normal subgroup N and the acting group H. The order is therefore the product of the two group orders. The first component is written additively and the second multiplicatively in the examples here.

## The action

An action is a homomorphism from H into the automorphism group of N. The action decides how the first component changes when the second component moves past it. In the inversion action on a cyclic group, h sends n to minus n.

## Multiplication

The product of (n1,h1) and (n2,h2) is (n1 plus h1 applied to n2, h1 h2). The rule is not the direct-product rule unless the action is trivial. Computing the first component is the main exercise.

The three sections form one chain: the first names the object, the second carries out the computation and the third reads the result in context.
