# Amicable numbers

Amicable numbers form pairs in which each number is the aliquot sum of the other. The smallest pair is 220 and 284.

## Aliquot sums

The aliquot sum s of n adds the proper divisors of n. It is the first half of the amicable test. The sum can be smaller, equal or larger than n.

## Amicable pairs

A pair n and m is amicable when s of n equals m and s of m equals n, with n different from m. Applying the aliquot map twice returns the starting number. Perfect numbers are the fixed points that are excluded from the pair definition.

## Finding the partner

For a number known to be in an amicable pair, the partner is its aliquot sum. Applying the aliquot sum to the partner returns the original number. If the second application does not return the original, the number has no partner in this sense.
