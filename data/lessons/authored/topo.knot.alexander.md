# The Alexander polynomial

The Alexander polynomial is a Laurent polynomial invariant of a knot. It is defined up to multiplication by a power of the variable, and its degree bounds the Seifert genus.

## The degree

The trefoil has Alexander polynomial \(t^2 - t + 1\), of degree two. The degree equals twice the Seifert genus.

## Evaluation at one

Every Alexander polynomial satisfies \(\Delta(1) = \pm 1\). The absolute value is one for every knot.

## Torus knots

The torus knot \(T(p,q)\) has Alexander polynomial of degree \((p-1)(q-1)\). The trefoil gives degree two.

To check your understanding, read the polynomial degree, evaluate at one and multiply the shifted parameters.
