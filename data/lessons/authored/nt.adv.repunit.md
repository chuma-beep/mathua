# Repunits

A repunit is a number written with repeated ones. Its value is a geometric sum, and its residues explain many divisibility patterns.

## Repunits as geometric sums

The repunit R sub n is 1 plus 10 plus 10 squared up to 10 to the n minus 1. The geometric-series formula gives 10 to the n minus 1 divided by 9. The decimal form is n ones.

## Residues

Modulo m, a repunit is the geometric sum of the powers of 10. Repeated squaring or a short loop finds the residue. A repunit is divisible by 3 exactly when its digit sum n is divisible by 3.

## Digit sums

The digit sum of a repunit with n digits is n. That simple fact makes repunits useful in divisibility and digital-root questions. It also shows why repunits are never divisible by 2 or 5.
