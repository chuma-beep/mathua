# Burnside's p^q theorem

A group whose order has at most two prime divisors is solvable. Burnside proved this with character theory.

## Statement

If \(|G|=p^a q^b\) for primes p and q, then G is solvable.

## Character input

The proof studies a non-linear irreducible character and shows its degree is divisible by a prime power.

## Consequence

Groups of order p^a q^b have a nontrivial normal subgroup, so induction on the order applies.

To check your understanding, factor the order, take a non-linear character and find a normal subgroup.

Burnside's theorem is the classical application of characters to group structure.
