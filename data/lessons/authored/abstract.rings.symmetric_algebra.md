# Symmetric algebras

The symmetric algebra is the free commutative algebra on a module. Its degree-d part consists of symmetric powers of the module.

## Definition

The symmetric algebra is the quotient of the tensor algebra by the two-sided ideal generated by differences of swapped tensors.

## Graded pieces

The degree-\(d\) part of the symmetric algebra on an \(n\)-dimensional space has dimension \(\binom{n+d-1}{d}\).

## Polynomial model

For a free module the symmetric algebra is a polynomial ring in the same number of variables.
