# Circles in the complex plane

The set |z-c|=r is a circle centred at c. Squaring the modulus avoids square roots.

## Equation

The circle of radius \(r\) about \(c\) is \(|z-c|^{2}=r^{2}\), which expands to a real quadratic equation.

## Centre and radius

Completing the square recovers the centre and the squared radius from the coefficients.

## Diameter form

If \(p\) and \(q\) are endpoints of a diameter, the centre is their midpoint and the squared radius is a quarter of \(|p-q|^{2}\).

To check your understanding, write the squared equation, complete the square and average the endpoints.

Squaring the modulus turns a circle into an integer equation.
