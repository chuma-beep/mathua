# Power of a point

## Intersecting chords

The power of a point is a product built from the distances along chords, secants or tangents through one point. If two chords cross inside a circle, the products of their segments are equal. When one chord is split into 3 and 8 and the other into 4 and x, the equation is 3 x 8 = 4x. The missing segment is 24 / 4 = 6. The two products are equal because both measure the same power of the intersection point.

## Tangent and secant

For a point outside a circle, the tangent length squared equals the product of the secant's external part and its total length. A tangent from an external point satisfies t^2 = eL. If e = 4 and L = 25, then t^2 = 100 and t = 10. The tangent length is positive, so take the positive square root.

## Two secants

Two secants from the same point have equal power. If the first has external part 4 and total length 9, its power is 36. A second secant with external part 3 must have total length 36 / 3 = 12. Its internal part is 12 - 3 = 9. Equal power is the single principle behind all three configurations.
