# Tubular neighbourhoods

A tubular neighbourhood thickens a submanifold into an open set that looks like its normal bundle. It is the smooth analogue of a regular neighbourhood in topology.

## Normal rank

If \(M\) has dimension \(m\) inside \(N\) of dimension \(n\), the normal bundle has rank \(n - m\). The tubular neighbourhood has the same dimension as \(N\).

## The boundary sphere bundle

Taking unit normal vectors leaves a sphere of dimension \(n - m - 1\) in each fibre. The boundary of the tubular neighbourhood is therefore a sphere bundle of dimension \(n - 1\).

## Collars

A tubular neighbourhood of the boundary of a manifold gives a collar. The collar identifies a neighbourhood of the boundary with the product of the boundary and an interval.

To check your understanding, subtract the dimensions, subtract one more and identify the product.
