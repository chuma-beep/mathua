# Property (T)

Property (T) is a strong rigidity of unitary representations. Finite groups and higher-rank lattices have it, while free abelian groups of positive rank and free groups do not.

## Abelian groups

A free abelian group of positive rank does not have property (T). Its characters give almost invariant vectors without invariant ones.

## Finite groups

Every finite group has property (T). Averaging any vector over the group produces an invariant vector.

## Higher rank

The group \(\mathrm{SL}(3,\mathbb{Z})\) has property (T). Kazhdan's theorem covers lattices in higher-rank semisimple groups.

To check your understanding, recognise the failure, average over the group and recognise the lattice.

Property (T) is the opposite of amenability for many groups.
