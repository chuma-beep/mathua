# Convergence of Fourier series

Fourier coefficients measure the frequency content of a periodic function. Their decay controls the convergence of the Fourier series.

## Coefficient decay

Smooth functions have rapidly decaying Fourier coefficients. A coefficient of the form a over n to the p decays at rate p. The exponent is the relevant convergence parameter.

## The Dirichlet kernel

The Dirichlet kernel is the finite sum of exponentials. At zero its value is the number of terms, 2n plus 1. Its oscillations explain the Gibbs phenomenon.

## Pointwise values

At a point, a finite Fourier polynomial is the sum of its coefficients times the corresponding exponentials. At zero the exponentials are all one. The value is the sum of the coefficients.
