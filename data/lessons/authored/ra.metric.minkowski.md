# Minkowski's inequality

Minkowski's inequality is the triangle inequality for Lp norms. It says the norm of a sum is at most the sum of the norms.

## The triangle inequality

For two vectors, the norm of their sum is at most the sum of their norms. On Euclidean space this is the ordinary triangle inequality. The inequality is what makes Lp a normed space.

## Norm of a sum

Add the vectors componentwise and then compute the norm. The result is bounded by the two separate norms. The finite examples check the bound directly.

## Equality case

Equality holds when the vectors are nonnegative scalar multiples of one another. The proportionality constant is the scalar. This is the equality condition in the triangle inequality.
