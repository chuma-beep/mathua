# Matrix norms

A matrix norm measures the size of a matrix. The 1-norm and infinity norm are maximum absolute column and row sums, and the Frobenius norm is the Euclidean length of all entries.

## The 1-norm

The induced 1-norm is the largest absolute column sum. Add the absolute values down each column and take the maximum. It measures how much the matrix can stretch a vector in the 1-norm.

## The infinity norm

The induced infinity norm is the largest absolute row sum. Add the absolute values across each row and take the maximum. It is the natural norm for bounded componentwise input.

## The Frobenius norm

The Frobenius norm squares every entry, adds the squares and takes the square root. Its square is the sum of the squared entries. It is the Euclidean norm on the matrix viewed as a long vector.
