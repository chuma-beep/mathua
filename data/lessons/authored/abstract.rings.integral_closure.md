# Integral closure

The integral closure of a domain consists of the elements satisfying a monic polynomial over it. Integrally closed domains are normal.

## Definition

An element is integral when it is a root of a monic polynomial with coefficients in the base ring.

## Normal domains

A domain is normal when it equals its integral closure in its fraction field.

## Quadratic orders

The ring of integers of a quadratic field has rank two over the integers.
