# Mean time between failures

Mean time between failures is the expected operating time between consecutive failures of a repairable system.

## Constant hazard

For an exponential lifetime with rate \(\lambda\), the mean time between failures is \(1/\lambda\).

## Repair

With instantaneous repair the failure process is Poisson, so the mean inter-arrival time is the reciprocal rate.

## Distinction

Mean time to failure describes a non-repairable component, while mean time between failures describes a repaired one.

To check your understanding, take the rate, assume instantaneous repair and separate the two acronyms.

MTBF is a reciprocal rate under a constant hazard.
