# Symmetric squares

The symmetric square is the quotient of the tensor square by the relation that swaps the two factors. Its dimension counts unordered pairs of basis vectors.

## Basis

A basis is indexed by unordered pairs of basis vectors, including repeated vectors. For dimension n that gives n(n+1)/2 basis elements.

## Quadratic forms

Symmetric bilinear forms and quadratic forms on V correspond to linear functionals on the symmetric square. Polarisation recovers the form from its diagonal.

## Symmetry

The projection onto the symmetric square averages a tensor with its swap. A pure tensor maps to the symmetrised product of its two vectors.

To check your understanding, count unordered pairs, identify the dual and average with the swap.

The symmetric square carries the polynomial degree-two data of a vector space.
