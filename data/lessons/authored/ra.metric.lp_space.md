# Lp spaces

An Lp space consists of measurable functions whose p-th power is integrable. The Lp norm measures their size.

## The Lp norm

On a finite sequence, the Lp norm is the p-th root of the sum of the p-th powers. The l1 norm is the sum of absolute values. The l-infinity norm is the maximum absolute value.

## The p equals 2 case

The l2 norm is the Euclidean length. Its square is the sum of the squared components. This is the Hilbert-space case and the most geometric one.

## Finite sequence spaces

On a finite set, every Lp space is a finite-dimensional normed space. The dimension is the number of points. Completeness and equivalence of norms follow from finite dimension.
