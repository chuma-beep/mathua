# Residues from the derivative formula

At a simple pole c the residue is g(c)/h'(c) for f=g/h.

## Formula

If \(f(z)=g(z)/h(z)\) with \(h(c)=0\) and \(h'(c)\ne0\), then \(\operatorname{Res}_{c}f=g(c)/h'(c)\).

## Examples

The residue of \((z+A)/(z-B)\) at \(B\) is \(A+B\), and the residue of \(1/(z-c)\) is one.

## Pole count

A rational function written over distinct linear factors has one simple pole per factor.

To check your understanding, evaluate the numerator, compute a linear residue and factor the denominator.

A simple pole contributes one residue, computed from the numerator and the derivative.
