# Crossing numbers

The crossing number is the minimum number of double points in any diagram of a knot. It is the simplest measure of how tangled a knot is.

## Torus knots

The torus knot \(T(p,q)\) has crossing number \(pq - \max(p,q)\). The trefoil is \(T(2,3)\), and \(6 - 3 = 3\).

## Small knots

The trefoil has crossing number three and the figure-eight knot has crossing number four. These are the two simplest nontrivial knots.

## Connected sums

Crossing numbers are subadditive under connected sum. The connected sum of two trefoils is a knot with crossing number six.
