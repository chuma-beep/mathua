# Polar equations of conics

A conic with a focus at the pole has a simple polar equation. The eccentricity and the directrix distance determine the shape.

## Polar form of a conic

The equation r equals e d divided by one plus e cosine theta describes a conic with focus at the pole. The numerator sets the scale and the denominator controls the shape. A different trigonometric function rotates the conic.

## Eccentricity and directrix

Eccentricity below one gives an ellipse. Eccentricity equal to one gives a parabola and eccentricity above one gives a hyperbola. The directrix distance d fixes the size.

## Reading the denominator

At theta equal to zero the denominator is one plus e. The radius is largest there for an ellipse. The denominator vanishes at an asymptote direction for a hyperbola.
