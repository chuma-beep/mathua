# Reliability functions

A reliability function is the probability that a component performs its function for a stated period under stated conditions.

## Definition

Reliability at time \(t\) is the survival probability, so the two notions coincide for non-repairable components.

## Bath tub curve

Failure over a lifetime often combines infant mortality, a useful life and wear-out, giving a hazard that falls, stays flat and then rises.

## Design

Redundancy raises reliability at the cost of components, and the gain depends on the failure dependence between them.
