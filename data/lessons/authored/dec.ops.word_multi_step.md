# Multi-step decimal word problems

## Finding a Total Cost

A shopping problem usually asks for a sum of several decimal prices. Multiply before adding when an item appears more than once. Three notebooks at 2.75 each cost 8.25, because \\(3 \\times 2.75 = 8.25\\). Add a pen at 1.40 to reach 9.65.

Keep the decimal points aligned as you add. Write every price with two places. The total 9.65 means nine dollars and sixty-five cents, so the cents part is never allowed to exceed 99 in the answer.

## Working With Change

Change is the amount paid minus the total cost. A customer paying with a 20 dollar bill for a 9.65 purchase receives 10.35, since \\(20 - 9.65 = 10.35\\).

Estimate first to catch a misplaced point. Round 9.65 to 10, so the change should be near 10. An answer of 1.035 or 103.5 is off by a factor of ten. The rough estimate rejects either one before the receipt is printed.

## Combining Price and Fee

Some problems add a fee or a tax after the subtotal. Apply the fee to the subtotal, then add. A subtotal of 9.65 with shipping 4.95 and tax 0.87 becomes 15.47.

Work in a fixed order: multiply quantities, add to get the subtotal, add fees, then subtract from the amount paid. With 20 paid, the change is 4.53. Writing the steps in that order keeps each intermediate number meaningful and easy to check.
