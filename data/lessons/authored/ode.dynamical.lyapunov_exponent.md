# Lyapunov exponents

A Lyapunov exponent measures the exponential rate at which nearby trajectories separate. The largest exponent decides whether a trajectory is stable, neutral, or chaotic.

## Definition

The exponent is the limit of the logarithm of the separation divided by time. A positive value means the separation grows exponentially, a zero value means it grows subexponentially, and a negative value means it shrinks.

## The largest exponent

For most initial conditions the separation aligns with the direction of fastest growth, so a single number summarises the local instability. A positive largest exponent is the standard numerical signature of chaos.

## The spectrum

An n-dimensional system has n exponents, obtained from the growth rates of a basis of perturbations. Their sum is the average divergence of the field, which measures how fast volumes expand or contract.

To check your understanding, write the separation ratio, compare growth rates and count the exponents.

The exponent converts a qualitative question about sensitivity into a number that can be estimated from a single long trajectory.
