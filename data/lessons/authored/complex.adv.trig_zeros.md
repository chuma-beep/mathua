# Zeros of complex trigonometric functions

Complex sine and cosine are defined through exponentials. Their zeros are real and evenly spaced.

## Solve a trigonometric zero

Set sine z equal to zero. The exponential form gives e to the i z equal to e to the minus i z. The solutions are integer multiples of pi.

## State the period

Sine and cosine have period 2 pi. A scaled function sine of k z has period 2 pi over k. The period controls how often the zeros repeat.

## Count zeros in a range

The zeros of sine are k pi for integer k. Count the integers k whose multiples lie in the interval. A closed interval includes both endpoints when they are zeros.
