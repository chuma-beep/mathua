# The Kunneth formula

The Künneth formula computes the homology of a product from the homology of the factors. Over a field the answer is a direct sum of tensor products indexed by degree.

## Products of spheres

The homology of \(S^m \times S^n\) has one generator in degrees zero, \(m\), \(n\) and \(m+n\), with repeats when \(m = n\). The total Betti sum is four for \(m \ne n\).

## Tori

The \(k\)-th Betti number of the \(n\)-torus is \(\binom{n}{k}\). For \(n = 2\) the numbers are one, two and one.

## Total Betti number

Summing the binomial coefficients gives \(2^n\) for the \(n\)-torus. The total Betti number therefore doubles with each added circle.

To check your understanding, list the degrees, read the binomial coefficient and sum the binomial coefficients.

Künneth converts products of spaces into sums of tensor products.
