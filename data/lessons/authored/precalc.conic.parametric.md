# Parametric equations of conics

A conic can be described by a parameter instead of a single implicit equation. The parameter traces the curve as it varies.

## Parametric coordinates

A circle uses cosine and sine. An ellipse scales those coordinates by its semi-axes. A hyperbola uses hyperbolic functions or a rational parameterization.

## Eliminating the parameter

Solve one coordinate for the parameter and substitute into the other. The result is the implicit equation of the conic. The parameter range determines which arc is traced.

## Identifying the conic

The squared terms and their signs identify the conic type. Equal positive coefficients give a circle. Different positive coefficients give an ellipse and opposite signs give a hyperbola.
