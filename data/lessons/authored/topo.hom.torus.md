# Homology of tori

The \(n\)-torus is a product of circles, so its Betti numbers are binomial coefficients. Its homology is free in every degree.

## Betti numbers

The \(k\)-th Betti number of the \(n\)-torus is \(\binom{n}{k}\). For \(n = 3\) the numbers are one, three, three and one.

## Total Betti number

The sum of the Betti numbers is \(2^n\). The four-torus has total Betti number sixteen.

## The first Betti number

The first Betti number of the \(n\)-torus is \(n\). That is the rank of the abelianized fundamental group.

To check your understanding, read the binomial coefficient, raise two to the dimension and read the dimension.
