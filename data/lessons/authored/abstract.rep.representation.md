# Group representations

A representation turns each group element into a linear map on a vector space. It lets group questions be answered with matrices and characters.

## Representations

A representation is a homomorphism from a group into the general linear group of a vector space. Its degree is the dimension of that space. The regular representation acts on a space with one basis element per group element.

## Characters

The character of a representation is the trace of each representing matrix. At the identity the trace is the degree. Characters of direct sums add, so the degree of a direct sum is the sum of the degrees.

## Permutation representations

A group acting on a finite set gives a permutation representation on the vector space with basis the set. The character at an element counts its fixed points. A transposition in the natural action of the symmetric group fixes all but two points.

The three sections form one chain: the first names the object, the second carries out the computation and the third reads the result in context.
