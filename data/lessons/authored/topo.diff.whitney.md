# The Whitney embedding theorem

Every smooth \(n\)-manifold embeds in Euclidean space of dimension \(2n\). The bound is sharp for projective spaces and gives the standard way to treat abstract manifolds as subsets of a Euclidean space.

## The embedding dimension

A smooth compact \(n\)-manifold embeds in \(\mathbb{R}^{2n}\). The dimension \(2n\) is the Whitney strong embedding bound.

## Immersions

An immersion only needs \(\mathbb{R}^{2n-1}\). The weaker bound allows crossings but keeps the derivative injective at every point.

## Codimension

The codimension of the Whitney embedding is \(n\). A surface therefore embeds in four-space, and a curve in the plane.

To check your understanding, double the manifold dimension, subtract one from twice the dimension and read the codimension.

Whitney doubles the dimension once and for all.
