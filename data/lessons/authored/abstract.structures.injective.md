# Injective modules

An injective module extends maps along injections. Over the integers, the rationals are injective and the integers are not.

## Definition

Every injection into an injective module extends a map out of it to any larger module.

## Examples

\(\mathbb{Q}\) is injective over \(\mathbb{Z}\), while \(\mathbb{Z}\) is not.

## Divisibility

Over a principal ideal domain, injective modules are exactly the divisible ones.

To check your understanding, draw the extension, test the rationals and divide by integers.

Injectivity is the dual of projectivity.
