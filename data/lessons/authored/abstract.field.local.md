# Local fields

A local field is complete with respect to a discrete valuation and has a finite residue field. Its extensions are described by ramification and residue degree.

## Definition

A local field carries a discrete valuation, is complete in that valuation and has a finite residue field.

## Ramification

The degree of an extension factors as the product of the ramification index and the residue degree.

## Residue field

The residue field is finite, with size a prime power.

To check your understanding, find the valuation, compute e and take the quotient.

Local fields package arithmetic into a valuation and a residue field.
