# Maximum likelihood

The maximum likelihood estimator chooses the parameter that makes the observed sample most probable.

## Definition

The estimator maximises the likelihood, or equivalently the log likelihood, over the parameter space.

## Properties

Under regularity the estimator is consistent and asymptotically normal, with asymptotic variance given by the inverse Fisher information.

## Computation

Setting the score to zero gives the likelihood equations, which are often solvable in closed form for exponential families.
