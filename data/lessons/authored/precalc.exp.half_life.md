# Half-life

Half-life is the time a decaying quantity needs to fall to half its current size. The model is exponential with a base of one half per half-life.

## The model

After t time units the amount is the initial amount times one half raised to t over the half-life.

## Solving for time

Take a logarithm of both sides to bring the exponent down, then divide by the logarithm of one half.

## Remaining fraction

After n half-lives the remaining fraction is one over two to the n, which is the cleanest way to check an answer.

To check your understanding, write the base, take logarithms and count half-lives.

Half-life is a unit of time, so it never appears inside a logarithm without its time unit.
