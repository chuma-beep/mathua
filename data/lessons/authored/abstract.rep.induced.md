# Induced representations

Induced representations studies an induced representation lifts a representation from a subgroup to the whole group. The lesson separates the setup, the computation and the interpretation so practice can test each move.

## Degree of an induced representation

Degree of an induced representation starts from the defining objects. Identify what is given, state the governing relation and check the hypotheses before substituting numbers.

## Character at the identity

Character at the identity carries out the computation. Work with explicit small data, simplify exactly and keep the signs and dimensions consistent.

## Frobenius reciprocity

Frobenius reciprocity interprets the result and checks it against the definition. Compare with a second example and state what the answer means in induced representations.

A complete answer names the relation, substitutes the data and states the result in the language of the problem. When a second method is available, compare the two results before moving on.
