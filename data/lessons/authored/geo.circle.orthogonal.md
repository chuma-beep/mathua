# Orthogonal circles

Two circles meet orthogonally when their tangents at an intersection point are perpendicular. The condition is a relation among the two radii and the centre distance.

## The right angle

At an intersection point the two radii point along the two tangent directions. Perpendicular tangents mean perpendicular radii.

## The squared condition

The triangle formed by the two centres and an intersection point is right at the intersection. Hence the square of the centre distance equals the sum of the squared radii.

## Using the condition

A circle orthogonal to a given one has its centre on a circle determined by the two radii. The condition is symmetric in the two circles.

To check your understanding, draw the radii, square the distance and swap the circles.

Orthogonality is Pythagoras applied to the two centres.
