# Burnside's orbit formula

Burnside's lemma counts orbits as the average number of fixed points over the group.

## Formula

The number of orbits is \(\frac{1}{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}|X^g|\).

## Identity

The identity fixes every point, so its fixed-point count is the size of the set.

## Rotations

A cyclic rotation group acts transitively on a regular polygon's vertices, so the orbit count is one.

To check your understanding, sum fixed points, count the set and check transitivity.

Averaging fixed points replaces an orbit count with a group sum.
