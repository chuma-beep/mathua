# Truncated cones

A truncated cone is the part of a cone between two parallel planes. Its volume depends on the two radii and the height between them.

## The volume formula

A frustum with radii R and r and height h has volume pi h over 3 times R squared plus R r plus r squared.

## The quadratic factor

The factor R squared plus R r plus r squared is the average of the three pairwise products of the two radii. It reduces to three r squared when the radii are equal.

## Slant height

The slant height is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs equal to the height and the difference of the radii.

To check your understanding, square both radii, set the radii equal and form the right triangle.
