# Elliptic functions

Elliptic functions are meromorphic and doubly periodic. They are the natural functions on a torus.

## Find the periods

A nonconstant elliptic function has two independent periods. The period lattice generates all periods. The quotient of the plane by the lattice is a torus.

## Locate the poles

In a fundamental parallelogram the function has at least two poles counted with multiplicity. The sum of the residues is zero. A single simple pole is impossible.

## Use the addition relation

The Weierstrass elliptic function satisfies a differential equation with a cubic right-hand side. Its addition formula combines two points on the associated cubic. The relation is the group law on the torus.
