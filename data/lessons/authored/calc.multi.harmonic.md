# Harmonic functions

A harmonic function has zero Laplacian. Harmonic functions satisfy the mean value property and the maximum principle and are the steady states of the heat equation.

## The Laplacian test

The Laplacian is the sum of the pure second partial derivatives. For a quadratic with coefficients a, b and c, it is twice their sum. The function is harmonic exactly when that sum is zero.

## Choosing a coefficient

In two dimensions, a x squared plus b y squared is harmonic when b equals negative a. In three dimensions the third coefficient is the negative sum of the other two. The condition is linear in the coefficients.

## Sums of harmonic functions

The Laplacian is linear, so the sum of two harmonic functions is harmonic. A constant multiple of a harmonic function is harmonic. These two facts generate a large family of examples.
