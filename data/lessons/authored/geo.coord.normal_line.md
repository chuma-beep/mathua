# The normal form of a line

Writing a line as ax+by=c exposes its normal vector (a,b). The distance from the origin follows by dividing c by the normal length.

## The normal vector

The coefficients a and b form a vector perpendicular to the line, because moving along the line leaves ax+by unchanged.

## Distance to the origin

The distance is the absolute value of c divided by the square root of a squared plus b squared.

## Parallel lines

Two lines in normal form are parallel exactly when their normal vectors are scalar multiples.

To check your understanding, read the coefficients, take absolute c and compare normals.

The normal form reads the geometry of a line directly from its coefficients.
