# Riccati equations

A Riccati equation is first-order and quadratic in the unknown. One particular solution converts it into a linear equation.

## Identify a particular solution

Substitute a simple trial such as a constant into the Riccati equation. A constant works when the coefficient functions cancel at that value. The particular solution does not need to satisfy the initial condition.

## Apply the Riccati substitution

Set y equal to the particular solution plus one over u. Differentiate and substitute into the Riccati equation. The quadratic terms cancel and leave a linear equation for u.

## Solve the resulting linear equation

Solve the linear equation for u with an integrating factor. Invert u to recover y. The constant is fixed by the original initial condition, not by the particular solution.

A complete answer states the governing relation, substitutes the given values and reads the result in the language of the problem. Where a second method is available, compare the two results before moving to the next question.
