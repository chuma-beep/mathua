# Fermat numbers

Fermat numbers have the form 2 to the 2 to the n plus 1. Fermat conjectured that all of them are prime, and the fifth one is the first counterexample.

## The Fermat sequence

The nth Fermat number is 2 to the power 2 to the n, plus 1. The first values are 3, 5, 17, 257 and 65537. They satisfy the recurrence that each one is the product of all earlier ones plus 2.

## Primality

The first five Fermat numbers are prime. F5 equals 4294967297 and factors as 641 times 6700417. A primality test must therefore run beyond the small cases.

## Modular residues

A Fermat number modulo m is computed by repeated squaring of 2. The exponent is itself a power of 2, so the calculation is short. Residues are used to test the known factors of F5.
