# Stable matching

Stable matching pairs two equal-sized groups using preference lists. The Gale-Shapley algorithm lets one side propose and produces a stable matching.

## Proposals

Each proposer approaches partners in preference order. A free partner accepts. A partner who is already matched compares the new proposal with the current match and keeps the preferred one.

## The stable matching

The algorithm ends with a perfect matching. No unmatched pair would both prefer each other to their current partners. That is the definition of stability.

## Counting proposals

Every approach is a proposal, including the ones that are rejected. The total count measures the work done by the algorithm. It depends on the preference lists, not only on the number of participants.
