# Asymptotic series

An asymptotic series approximates a function as a parameter becomes small or large. The error is smaller than the last retained term in the limit.

## Asymptotic expansion

The series is ordered by decreasing size as the parameter tends to a limit. Each term is smaller than the previous one in that limit. The series need not converge.

## Leading term

The leading term controls the first approximation. Its coefficient is the limit of the function divided by the leading power. Keeping more terms improves the approximation in the asymptotic sense.

## Error order

The error after truncation is of the order of the next term. This is weaker than convergence but useful for approximation. A divergent asymptotic series can still give accurate numerical values.
