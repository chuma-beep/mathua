# The multinomial theorem

The multinomial theorem expands a power of a sum with more than two terms. Each monomial coefficient counts the ways to choose the exponents.

## The multinomial coefficient

The coefficient of a product of powers is the factorial of the total exponent divided by the product of the factorials of the exponents. The exponents must sum to the total. The coefficient is an integer.

## Number of terms

The number of monomials in the expansion of a sum of m terms raised to n is the combinations of n plus m minus one choose m minus one. For three terms this is n plus two choose two. The count includes monomials with zero exponents.

## Evaluating at one

Setting every variable to one turns the expansion into the sum of all coefficients. The sum equals m to the n. This is a quick check on a computed expansion.
