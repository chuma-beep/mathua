# The universal coefficient theorem

The universal coefficient theorem relates cohomology to homology with a tensor term and an Ext term. The Ext term records torsion and vanishes when the homology is free.

## The circle

The first cohomology of the circle is \(\mathbb{Z}\), of rank one. The homology is free, so the Ext term vanishes.

## The Ext term

For free homology groups the Ext term is zero. Cohomology then has the same rank as homology in each degree.

## Projective space

The first integral cohomology of the projective plane is \(\mathbb{Z}/2\), of order two. The torsion comes from the Ext term applied to the two-torsion in homology.

To check your understanding, read the rank, decide whether homology is free and read the torsion order.

Cohomology equals homology up to a torsion correction.
