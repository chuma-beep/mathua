# Conjugacy classes

Two elements are conjugate when some inner automorphism carries one to the other. Conjugacy classes partition a group and index its irreducible characters.

## Counting classes

For the symmetric group \(S_n\), conjugacy classes correspond to partitions of \(n\), because a permutation is determined up to conjugacy by its cycle type.

## Dihedral classes

In \(D_n\), rotations fall into classes according to the rotation angle, and reflections split into one or two classes according to the parity of \(n\).

## Abelian case

In an abelian group every conjugacy class is a singleton, so the number of classes equals the order of the group.

To check your understanding, count partitions, separate rotations and identify singletons.

Conjugacy is an equivalence relation, and its classes are the orbits of the conjugation action.
