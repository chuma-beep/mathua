# Free resolutions

A free resolution presents a module as the homology of a complex of free modules. Its length measures homological dimension.

## Construction

Choose a free module surjecting onto the module, then repeat with the kernel until the kernel is free or zero.

## Betti numbers

The ranks of the free modules are the Betti numbers of the resolution.

## Length

A module of projective dimension n has a resolution of length n.

To check your understanding, cover the module, count the generators and count the maps.

Free resolutions replace a module by a chain of free ones.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller numbers and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
