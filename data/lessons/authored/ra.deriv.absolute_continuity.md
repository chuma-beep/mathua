# Absolutely continuous functions

An absolutely continuous function is the integral of its derivative. It is stronger than uniform continuity and weaker than continuous differentiability.

## The fundamental theorem

For a linear function, the difference across an interval is the derivative times the length. Absolute continuity extends this identity to the integral of the derivative. The constant term cancels.

## The Cantor function

The Cantor function is continuous and nondecreasing. Its derivative is zero almost everywhere. It is not absolutely continuous because it is not constant.

## Epsilon-delta estimate

Absolute continuity controls the total variation of small intervals. For a linear function, the total variation is the absolute slope times the interval length. The estimate is exact.
