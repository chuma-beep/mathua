# Properties of summation notation

Summation notation is linear. A sum can be split, scaled or combined with another sum over the same index range.

## Linearity

The sum of a constant times a term is the constant times the sum. The sum of a sum is the sum of the sums. These rules let a complicated sum be separated into known pieces.

## Splitting a sum

A sum from one to n can be split at an intermediate index. The two pieces cover the same terms without overlap. Reversing the order is also allowed.

## Changing the index

A shift in the index changes the limits and every occurrence of the index. The total value stays the same. A correct shift preserves the number of terms.
