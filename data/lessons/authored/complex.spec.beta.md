# Euler integrals of the first kind

The beta function is a ratio of gamma values, and at positive integers it is a ratio of factorials.

## Definition

\(B(p,q)=\Gamma(p)\Gamma(q)/\Gamma(p+q)\) for positive real parts.

## Integer values

\(B(m,n)=\frac{(m-1)!(n-1)!}{(m+n-1)!}\), a rational number with an integer denominator.

## Symmetry

\(B(p,q)=B(q,p)\), visible from the definition and from the integral representation.

To check your understanding, write the gamma ratio, build the factorial ratio and swap the arguments.

The beta function packages a ratio of factorials as a single symbol.
