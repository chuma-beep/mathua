# The theta function

The theta function is a sum of q^{n^2}, so its coefficients count representations by squares.

## Series

\(\theta_{3}(q)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}q^{n^{2}}=1+2q+2q^{4}+2q^{9}+\cdots\).

## Coefficients

The coefficient of \(q^{k}\) is the number of integers \(n\) with \(n^{2}=k\), which is two for a nonzero square and one for zero.

## Modularity

Under \(q\mapsto e^{-2\pi/t}\) the theta function transforms into itself up to a factor, which is the prototype of a modular form.
