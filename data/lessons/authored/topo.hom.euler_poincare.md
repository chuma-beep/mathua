# The Euler-Poincare formula

The Euler-Poincaré formula equates the alternating sum of Betti numbers with the Euler characteristic. It holds for every finite CW complex.

## The formula

For a finite complex, \(\chi = \sum_k (-1)^k b_k\). The sum is finite because the Betti numbers vanish above the dimension.

## The torus

The two-torus has Betti numbers one, two and one. The alternating sum is \(1 - 2 + 1 = 0\), which equals the Euler characteristic.

## The total Betti number

The sum of the Betti numbers of the two-torus is four. For the \(n\)-sphere the total is two.

To check your understanding, alternate the Betti numbers, compute the alternating sum and sum the Betti numbers.
