# The Poisson kernel

The Poisson kernel solves the Dirichlet problem in the unit disk. It averages boundary values with a harmonic weight.

## Evaluate the kernel

At the centre the kernel is 1. At radius r and angle zero it is 1 plus r over 1 minus r. The kernel is positive and integrates to 1 over the circle.

## Integrate against a boundary value

The harmonic extension is the integral of the boundary value times the kernel. The integral runs over the boundary circle. A constant boundary value gives the same constant inside.

## Recover the boundary limit

As the interior point approaches a boundary point, the kernel concentrates at that point. The extension tends to the boundary value. This is the defining approximation property of an approximate identity.
