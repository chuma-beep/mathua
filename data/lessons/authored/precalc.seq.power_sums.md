# Sums of powers

The sums of the first n integers, squares and cubes have closed formulas. They turn many finite sums into arithmetic.

## Sum of the first n integers

The sum from one to n of k is n times n plus one divided by two. Pairing the first and last terms explains the formula. The result is always an integer.

## Squares and cubes

The sum of squares is n times n plus one times 2n plus one divided by six. The sum of cubes is the square of the sum of the first n integers. Both formulas are exact for every positive n.
