# Maschke's theorem

Maschke's theorem studies Maschke's theorem turns a representation into a direct sum of irreducibles when the characteristic does not divide the group order. The lesson separates the setup, the computation and the interpretation so practice can test each move.

## The characteristic condition

The characteristic condition starts from the defining objects. Identify what is given, state the governing relation and check the hypotheses before substituting numbers.

## Averaging

Averaging carries out the computation. Work with explicit small data, simplify exactly and keep the signs and dimensions consistent.

## Semisimple decomposition

Semisimple decomposition interprets the result and checks it against the definition. Compare with a second example and state what the answer means in maschke's theorem.

A complete answer names the relation, substitutes the data and states the result in the language of the problem. When a second method is available, compare the two results before moving on.
