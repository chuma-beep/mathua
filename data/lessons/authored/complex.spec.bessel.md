# Bessel functions of complex order

Bessel functions solve Bessel's equation and have simple values at the origin.

## At zero

\(J_{0}(0)=1\) and \(J_{n}(0)=0\) for every nonzero integer \(n\).

## Parity

\(J_{-n}(z)=(-1)^{n}J_{n}(z)\), so negative orders differ only by a sign.

## Series

The leading term of \(J_{n}\) is \(z^{n}/(2^{n}n!)\), which fixes the behaviour near the origin.

To check your understanding, evaluate order zero, apply the sign rule and read the leading power.

Bessel functions are the cylindrical analogue of sines and cosines.
