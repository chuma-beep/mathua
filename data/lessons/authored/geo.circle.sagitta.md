# Sagitta and chord height

## Chord and sagitta

A sagitta is the perpendicular distance from the midpoint of a chord to the arc. It measures how far the arc rises above the chord.

The sagitta and half the chord form a right triangle with the radius. The distance from the center to the chord is the radius minus the sagitta.

## Radius from chord and sagitta

If the half-chord is a and the sagitta is s, the radius satisfies R squared equals a squared plus (R - s) squared. Expanding and solving gives R = (a squared + s squared) / (2s).

The radius must be at least the sagitta. An answer smaller than the sagitta means the formula was rearranged incorrectly.

## The central angle

The central angle can be found from the half-chord and the radius using trigonometry. The sine of half the central angle is the half-chord divided by the radius.

Check the angle against the diagram. A large sagitta corresponds to a large central angle, and a small sagitta corresponds to a shallow arc.
