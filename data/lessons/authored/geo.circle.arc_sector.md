# Arcs and sectors

An arc is a fraction of the circumference and a sector is the same fraction of the disc. The fraction is the central angle over a full turn.

## The arc length

A central angle of theta degrees cuts an arc of length theta over 360 times the circumference. With radius r the arc length is theta pi r over 180.

## The sector area

The sector bounded by the same angle has area theta over 360 times pi r squared. The angle fraction applies to the whole disc.

## Choosing the fraction

The same fraction scales length and area, so an angle of 90 degrees gives a quarter of the circumference and a quarter of the disc.

To check your understanding, form the angle fraction, square the radius and compare two angles.
