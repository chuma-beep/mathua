# Alternating multilinear forms

An alternating multilinear form changes sign when two arguments are swapped. Such forms are the dual objects to multivectors.

## Dimension

The space of alternating k-forms on an n-dimensional space has dimension C(n,k). A basis is dual to the k-element subsets used for multivectors.

## Sign

Swapping two arguments multiplies the form by minus one. Any repeated argument therefore forces the value to be zero.

## Top form

The space of alternating n-forms is one-dimensional. The determinant is its normalised generator, and it vanishes on dependent arguments.

To check your understanding, count k-subsets, swap two arguments and identify the determinant.

Alternating forms are determinants in disguise, one argument at a time.
