# The minimal polynomial

The minimal polynomial of a matrix is the monic polynomial of least degree that annihilates it. It divides the characteristic polynomial and shares its roots.

## Degree

The minimal polynomial has degree at most the size of the matrix. Equality holds exactly when the matrix is cyclic.

## Roots

Its roots are precisely the eigenvalues. The exponent of a root is the size of the largest Jordan block for that eigenvalue.

## Divisibility

The minimal polynomial divides the characteristic polynomial. The quotient records the repeated-block structure.

To check your understanding, bound by the size, list the eigenvalues and divide the characteristic polynomial.

The minimal polynomial is the smallest algebraic certificate that a matrix satisfies.
