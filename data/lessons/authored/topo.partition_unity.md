# Partitions of unity

Partitions of unity studies a partition of unity turns local data into a global sum of functions. The lesson separates the setup, the computation and the interpretation so practice can test each move.

## The sum

The sum starts from the defining objects. Identify what is given, state the governing relation and check the hypotheses before substituting numbers.

## Functions subordinate to a cover

Functions subordinate to a cover carries out the computation. Work with explicit small data, simplify exactly and keep the signs and dimensions consistent.

## Support

Support interprets the result and checks it against the definition. Compare with a second example and state what the answer means in partitions of unity.

A complete answer names the relation, substitutes the data and states the result in the language of the problem. When a second method is available, compare the two results before moving on.
