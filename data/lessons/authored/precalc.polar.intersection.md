# Intersections of polar curves

Two polar curves can meet at a point even when their equations do not give the same radius at the same angle. Intersection questions need both algebra and geometry.

## Solving polar equations

Set the two radius expressions equal and solve for theta. Each solution gives a common point. Check the point in both equations.

## Angles and common points

Polar coordinates are not unique. The same point can be written with a negative radius and an angle shifted by pi. This is why algebra can miss an intersection.

## Counting intersections

Plot or sample the curves to catch coincident points. A circle and a line through the pole can meet at the pole even when their equations look different. Count distinct points, not representations.
