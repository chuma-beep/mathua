# Restriction of representations

Restriction turns a representation of a group into one of a subgroup. Induction goes the other way and the two are adjoint.

## Definition

Restriction forgets the action of elements outside the subgroup.

## Dimension

Restriction preserves the dimension of the representation.

## Index

The index of the subgroup is the number of cosets used in the induced representation.

To check your understanding, keep the subgroup action, compare dimensions and count cosets.

Restriction is the forgetful half of the induction adjunction.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller numbers and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
