# Quotient spaces

A quotient space collapses a subspace to a single point. Its elements are cosets, and its dimension is the codimension of the subspace.

## Cosets

Two vectors are equivalent when their difference lies in the subspace. Each coset is a translate of the subspace, and the quotient is the set of translates.

## Dimension

The quotient of an n-dimensional space by a k-dimensional subspace has dimension n minus k. The quotient map is linear and surjective.

## Isomorphism

The first isomorphism theorem identifies the quotient by a kernel with the image. Dimension counting then gives rank-nullity.

To check your understanding, form a coset, subtract dimensions and apply the first isomorphism theorem.

Quotients are how linear algebra forgets a subspace while keeping the rest of the structure.
