# Hilbert's theorem 90

Hilbert's theorem 90 identifies a first cohomology group with a quotient of the multiplicative group by norms.

## Statement

For a cyclic Galois extension with group generated by \(\sigma\), elements of norm one are exactly those of the form \(x/\sigma(x)\).

## Vanishing

The first cohomology group \(H^1(G,E^\times)\) is trivial for a cyclic group.

## Norm kernel

The kernel of the norm map has size equal to the degree of the extension.

To check your understanding, compute the norm, take the cocycle and count the kernel.

Theorem 90 is the multiplicative form of the vanishing of H^1.
