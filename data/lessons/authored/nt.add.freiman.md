# Freiman's theorem

Freiman's theorem says that a set with small doubling is contained in a generalised arithmetic progression. Additive structure is geometric structure.

## Small doubling

If the sumset is at most a constant multiple of the set, the set has strong additive structure. The constant controls the dimension of the progression.

## The 3k minus 4 bound

A set of k integers with sumset of size at most 3k minus 4 lies in an interval of length at most 2k minus 3. The bound is sharp.

## Consequences

Freiman's theorem is a inverse theorem: it starts from a small sumset and concludes a progression. It underpins the structure theory of additive combinatorics.
