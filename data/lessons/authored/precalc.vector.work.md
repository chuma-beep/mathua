# Work from the dot product

Work is the component of force along a displacement multiplied by the displacement length. The dot product computes it directly.

## Work as a dot product

Work equals force dot displacement. Only the force component parallel to the displacement contributes. A perpendicular force does no work.

## Projection

The scalar projection of force onto displacement is the dot product divided by the displacement length. Multiplying by the length returns the work. The sign records whether the force assists or opposes the motion.

## Sign of work

Positive work adds energy to the object. Negative work removes energy. Zero work means the force is perpendicular to the displacement.
