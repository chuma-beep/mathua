# Group cohomology

Group cohomology computes derivations modulo inner derivations. It classifies extensions and fixed points across a resolution.

## Definition

\(H^1(G,A)\) is the quotient of crossed homomorphisms by principal ones, and \(H^0(G,A)\) is the fixed subgroup.

## Vanishing

For a cyclic group acting trivially on a module, the positive cohomology groups have order one in the simplest cases.

## Free groups

For a free group of rank \(r\), \(H^1\) with trivial integer coefficients has rank \(r\).

To check your understanding, take crossed maps, use the resolution and count generators.
