# A percentage of a fraction

A percentage of a fraction is the product of the percentage rate and the fraction. The result can be left as a fraction before simplifying.

## Percent as a fraction

A percentage p percent is p over one hundred. Multiplying by a fraction a over b gives p a over one hundred b. The numerator and denominator are multiplied separately.

## Multiplying fractions

Multiply the numerators and multiply the denominators. Reduce the result if a common factor appears. The unsimplified form is useful for checking the arithmetic.

## Comparing to one half

Double the numerator and compare it with the denominator. A larger doubled numerator means the result is above one half. The comparison avoids converting to a decimal.
