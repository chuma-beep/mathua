# Partial products

Partial products split a two-digit factor into tens and ones. Multiplying each part by the other factor and adding the results gives the full product.

## Splitting the first factor

Write the first factor as ten times its tens digit plus its ones digit. For example, 34 is 30 plus 4. The second factor stays whole during the first pass.

## The two partial products

Multiply the tens part by the second factor and multiply the ones part by the second factor. Each product is an integer. Keeping the two products separate makes the place value visible.

## Adding to the product

Add the two partial products to obtain the full product. The tens product contributes a multiple of ten and the ones product contributes the rest. The sum is the same as multiplying the original two-digit factors.

The three sections form one chain. The first names the object, the second performs the computation and the third reads the result in context.

Practice should be answered without a calculator first. Write the relation, substitute the published values and state the integer result. Then check the result against the defining identity or a second representation before moving to the next question.
