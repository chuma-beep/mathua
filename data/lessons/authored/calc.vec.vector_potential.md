# Vector potentials

A vector potential is a vector field whose curl is a prescribed field. It exists when the prescribed field is divergence-free and the domain has no obstruction.

## Finding a potential

For a constant field pointing along z, a potential can point along y with a linearly growing component. The curl of that potential returns the constant field. The choice is not unique.

## Verifying the curl

Compute the curl of the proposed potential and compare each component with the prescribed field. For A equal to (0,c x,0), the z-component of the curl is c. The x and y components vanish.

## Gauge freedom

Adding the gradient of any scalar function to a vector potential leaves its curl unchanged. The new potential describes the same field. This freedom is the gauge freedom of the potential.
