# Counting Goldbach partitions

A Goldbach partition writes an even number as a sum of two primes. Counting them turns Goldbach's conjecture into a quantitative statement.

## The count

The number of Goldbach partitions of n counts unordered pairs of primes summing to n. It is zero exactly when Goldbach fails for n.

## Small values

Eight has one partition, ten has two, and twelve has one. The count fluctuates with the arithmetic of n rather than growing monotonically.

## The asymptotic

The Hardy-Littlewood circle method predicts the count up to a singular series. The prediction is accurate and remains unproved in full.

To check your understanding, list prime pairs, test a small even and name the method.

Counting Goldbach partitions is the quantitative form of the conjecture, and the count depends on the prime factors of n.
