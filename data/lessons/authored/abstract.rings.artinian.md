# Artinian rings

An Artinian ring satisfies the descending chain condition on ideals. In the commutative case it is a finite product of Artinian local rings.

## Definition

Every descending chain of ideals stabilises, which is the mirror image of the Noetherian condition.

## Structure

A commutative Artinian ring is a finite direct product of local Artinian rings.

## Examples

The quotient \(k[x]/(x^n)\) has dimension \(n\) and is Artinian.

To check your understanding, take a descending chain, factor the ring and compute the dimension.

Artinian rings are built from finitely many local pieces.
