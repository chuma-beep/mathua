# Closed and exact forms

A form is closed when its exterior derivative is zero. It is exact when it is the exterior derivative of another form. Exact forms are always closed, but the converse depends on the domain.

## The closed test

For a one-form in the plane, closed means the mixed partials of its coefficients agree. For a y dx plus b x dy, the coefficient of dx wedge dy is b minus a. The form is closed exactly when that coefficient is zero.

## Finding a potential

An exact one-form is the differential of a scalar function. For constant coefficients, the potential is a x plus b y. Evaluating the potential at a point gives the value the form integrates to along a path from the origin.

## The punctured plane

The angle form is closed on the plane with the origin removed. It is not exact there because its integral around a loop around the origin is nonzero. The hole in the domain is the obstruction.

The three sections form one chain. The first names the object, the second performs the computation and the third reads the result in context. Practice asks one family from each section, so the three moves stay connected.
