# Fredholm integral equations

A Fredholm equation has fixed limits. A separable kernel reduces it to a finite linear system.

## Identify the kernel

The kernel K of x and t multiplies the unknown inside the integral. A separable kernel is a finite sum of products of a function of x and a function of t. The limits are fixed constants.

## Reduce a separable kernel

When the kernel is a product, the integral becomes a finite sum of moments of the unknown. The unknown appears outside and inside the same moments. This gives a linear system for those moments.

## Evaluate the resolvent

The resolvent is the inverse of the operator that defines the equation. For a separable kernel it is a finite matrix inverse. The solution is the resolvent applied to the forcing term.
