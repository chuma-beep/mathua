# Abel's theorem for power series

Abel's theorem extends a power series to the boundary of its interval when the boundary series converges. The boundary value is the limit of the interior sums.

## Power series at the boundary

A power series converges inside its radius and may converge at an endpoint. Abel's theorem relates the endpoint sum to the limit of the function from inside. The theorem requires convergence at the endpoint.

## Abel's theorem

If the series converges at the endpoint, the sum equals the limit of the function as x approaches the endpoint from inside. The limit is one-sided. The theorem does not claim convergence when the endpoint series diverges.

## An interior check

At a point strictly inside the radius, the geometric series can be summed directly. For a ratio of one half, the sum is two. This checks the formula before taking a boundary limit.
