# The genus formula

The genus is recovered from the Euler characteristic by a linear formula. With boundary circles present, the formula carries one extra term.

## Closed orientable surfaces

For a closed orientable surface, \(g = (2 - \chi)/2\). The numerator is always even because the Euler characteristic of an orientable surface is even.

## With boundary

For a connected orientable surface with \(b\) boundary circles, \(g = (2 - b - \chi)/2\). A pair of pants has \(\chi = -1\) and \(b = 3\), so the genus is zero.

## Nonorientable surfaces

For a closed nonorientable surface, the crosscap number is \(k = 2 - \chi\). The Klein bottle has \(\chi = 0\) and crosscap number two.

To check your understanding, subtract from two, include the boundary count and subtract from two.
