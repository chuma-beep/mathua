# Discriminants of polynomials

Discriminants of polynomials studies the discriminant records when a polynomial has a repeated root and how its roots are distributed. The lesson separates the setup, the computation and the interpretation so practice can test each move.

## The quadratic discriminant

The quadratic discriminant starts from the defining objects. Identify what is given, state the governing relation and check the hypotheses before substituting numbers.

## Repeated roots

Repeated roots carries out the computation. Work with explicit small data, simplify exactly and keep the signs and dimensions consistent.

## The sign

The sign interprets the result and checks it against the definition. Compare with a second example and state what the answer means in discriminants of polynomials.
