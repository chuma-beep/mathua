# Factoring over the complex numbers

Every polynomial with real coefficients factors into linear factors over the complex numbers. Complex roots occur in conjugate pairs, and the factor count follows the degree.

## Linear factors over the complex numbers

A real polynomial of degree n has exactly n linear factors when complex factors are allowed. A real root a contributes x minus a. A conjugate pair a plus or minus b i contributes the quadratic x minus a squared plus b squared.

## Counting the factors

Count one linear factor for each real root and two for each complex conjugate pair. The total equals the degree of the polynomial. A cubic with one real root and one complex pair therefore has three linear factors.

## Reconstructing the polynomial

Multiplying the linear factors returns the original polynomial up to a constant. The sum of the roots and the product of the roots follow from Vieta. Both are useful checks on a factorisation.

Practice connects the three sections: state the relation, carry out the computation and interpret the result. A second method is a useful check when one is available.
