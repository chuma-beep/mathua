# Ext and Tor

Ext and Tor are the two derived functors of Hom and tensor. On cyclic abelian groups they reduce to a gcd, which makes their size easy to compute.

## Ext

Ext one measures extensions of one module by another. Over the integers, Ext one of Z over m and Z over n is cyclic of order the gcd of m and n. The size of the group is therefore a gcd.

## Tor

Tor one measures the failure of tensor product to be exact. Over the integers it agrees with Ext one on cyclic groups, so its size is the same gcd. The two functors are different in general but coincide in this example.

## Exact sequences

A short exact sequence of vector spaces has dimensions that add. If the sequence runs from A through B to C, the dimension of B is the dimension of A plus the dimension of C. This is the rank-nullity theorem in sequence form.
