# Mertens theorems

Mertens theorems describe the average behaviour of the Möbius function. They say that cancellations are strong enough to keep the summatory function small.

## The summatory function

The Mertens function sums the Möbius function up to n. Its value records the imbalance between integers with an even and an odd number of prime factors.

## The square-root bound

Elementary estimates keep the Mertens function below the square root of n. The bound is not sharp, but it is enough for many arguments.

## Squarefree density

The Möbius function is nonzero exactly on squarefree integers. Their density is six over pi squared, so most integers contribute nothing to the sum.

To check your understanding, sum the Möbius values, state the bound and count squarefree integers.

Mertens theorems are statements about cancellation, and cancellation is what makes the Möbius function useful in analytic estimates.
