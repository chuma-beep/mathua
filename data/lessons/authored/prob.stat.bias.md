# Bias of an estimator

The bias of an estimator is the difference between its expected value and the true parameter. An unbiased estimator is correct on average.

## Definition

Bias is \(E[\hat\theta]-\theta\). It can be positive, negative or zero.

## Sample variance

Dividing by \(n-1\) instead of \(n\) removes the bias caused by using the sample mean.

## Trade-off

A small bias can be worth a large reduction in variance, which is why biased estimators are sometimes preferred.

To check your understanding, take the expectation, compute the plug-in variance and compare bias.

Bias measures average error, not error on one sample.
