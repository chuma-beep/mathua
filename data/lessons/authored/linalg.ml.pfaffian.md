# The Pfaffian

The Pfaffian is the square root of the determinant of an alternating matrix. It is defined only in even dimension and is a polynomial in the entries.

## Definition

For an alternating matrix of order 2m, the Pfaffian is a sum over perfect matchings of the indices. Each matching contributes the product of its entries with a sign.

## Terms

The number of perfect matchings of 2m indices is (2m-1)!!. For order four that is three terms, and for order six it is fifteen.

## Square

The square of the Pfaffian equals the determinant. This identity is why Pfaffians appear in fermionic integrals and in the theory of alternating forms.

To check your understanding, sum over matchings, count matchings and relate to the determinant.

The Pfaffian is the alternating analogue of the determinant, defined by pairings rather than permutations.
