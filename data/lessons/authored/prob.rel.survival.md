# Survival functions

The survival function gives the probability that a lifetime exceeds a given time. It is one minus the distribution function.

## Properties

Survival is non-increasing, starts at one and tends to zero, with the limit equal to the probability of immortality.

## Moments

The mean lifetime is the integral of the survival function over the positive half-line.

## Comparison

Stochastic ordering compares survival functions pointwise, which is stronger than comparing means.
