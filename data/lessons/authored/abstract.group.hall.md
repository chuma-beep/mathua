# Hall subgroups

A Hall subgroup has order coprime to its index. Hall's theorem says finite solvable groups contain a Hall subgroup for every set of prime divisors.

## Definition

For a set of primes, a Hall subgroup has order built from those primes and index built from the remaining primes.

## Existence

Solvable groups contain Hall subgroups for every prime set, and any two such subgroups are conjugate.

## Counting

The number of Hall subgroups divides the index and is congruent to one modulo each prime in the set.

To check your understanding, split the primes, assume solvability and use the divisibility.

Hall subgroups are the solvable analogue of Sylow subgroups.
