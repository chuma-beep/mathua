# Simplicial homology

Simplicial homology studies simplicial homology computes invariants from the combinatorics of a simplicial complex. The lesson separates the setup, the computation and the interpretation so practice can test each move.

## Counting simplices

Counting simplices starts from the defining objects. Identify what is given, state the governing relation and check the hypotheses before substituting numbers.

## Euler characteristic

Euler characteristic carries out the computation. Work with explicit small data, simplify exactly and keep the signs and dimensions consistent.

## Vertices

Vertices interprets the result and checks it against the definition. Compare with a second example and state what the answer means in simplicial homology.
