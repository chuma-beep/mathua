# Averaging

Averaging replaces a rapidly oscillating vector field by its mean over one period. The averaged system is autonomous, and its equilibria and stability describe the slow drift of the original.

## The averaged field

The right-hand side is integrated over one period of the fast oscillation while the slow variable is held fixed. The result depends only on the slow variable and defines an autonomous system.

## The error estimate

The true trajectory and the averaged trajectory stay within a distance of order the parameter over times of order one over the parameter. Beyond that time the two can separate, so the approximation is valid on a long but finite window.

## Use

Fixed points of the averaged system are periodic or quasi-periodic states of the original. Their stability decides whether an oscillation is captured into resonance, and the averaged phase equation describes the approach.

To check your understanding, hold the slow variable fixed, compare the two trajectories and find the averaged fixed point.

Averaging is the rigorous version of the multiple-scales idea, and it turns a non-autonomous problem into an autonomous one.
