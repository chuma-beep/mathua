# The triangle inequality for moduli

The triangle inequality |z+w| <= |z|+|w| follows from the geometry of vector addition.

## The inequality

The length of a sum is at most the sum of the lengths, with strict inequality unless the vectors point the same way.

## Squared form

Squaring gives \(|z+w|^{2}=|z|^{2}+|w|^{2}+2\operatorname{Re}(z\bar w)\), and the cross term is bounded by twice the product of the moduli.

## Equality case

Equality holds exactly when one vector is a nonnegative real multiple of the other.

To check your understanding, add the moduli, expand the square and test the direction.

The triangle inequality is the statement that a straight segment is the shortest route.
