# Orientation double covers

Every surface has an orientable double cover. The cover is connected exactly when the surface is nonorientable, and its Euler characteristic is twice the original.

## Euler characteristic

An \(n\)-sheeted covering multiplies the Euler characteristic by \(n\). The orientation double cover is two-sheeted, so \(\chi = 2\chi(M)\) in every case.

## Genus of the cover

The orientation double cover of the nonorientable surface with crosscap number \(k\) is orientable of genus \(k - 1\). The projective plane is covered by the sphere, and the Klein bottle by the torus.

## Disconnected case

When the surface is already orientable, the double cover splits into two copies. The cover is connected precisely for nonorientable surfaces.

To check your understanding, double the characteristic, subtract one from the crosscap number and count the components.

The double cover turns a crosscap into a handle.
