# Surfaces with boundary

A surface with boundary has a boundary consisting of circles. The Euler characteristic, the genus and the number of boundary circles satisfy one linear relation.

## The relation

For a connected orientable surface of genus \(g\) with \(b\) boundary circles, \(\chi = 2 - 2g - b\). The closed surface is the case \(b = 0\).

## Recovering the boundary

Given the genus and the Euler characteristic, \(b = 2 - 2g - \chi\). A genus-one surface with \(\chi = -1\) has three boundary circles.

## Small examples

The disc has genus zero, one boundary circle and \(\chi = 1\). The annulus has genus zero, two boundary circles and \(\chi = 0\). The pair of pants has three boundary circles and \(\chi = -1\).

To check your understanding, subtract twice the genus, solve for the boundary count and compute the disc.

Boundary circles are the third coordinate of the surface classification.
