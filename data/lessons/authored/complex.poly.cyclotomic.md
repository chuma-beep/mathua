# Polynomials for primitive roots

The n-th cyclotomic polynomial has degree φ(n) and its roots are the primitive n-th roots of unity.

## Definition

\(\Phi_{n}(z)=\prod_{\gcd(k,n)=1}(z-\zeta^{k})\), so its roots are exactly the primitive roots.

## Degree

The degree is Euler's totient \(\varphi(n)\), the count of residues coprime to \(n\).

## Prime case

For a prime \(p\), \(\Phi_{p}(z)=1+z+\cdots+z^{p-1}\) and the degree is \(p-1\).

To check your understanding, list the primitive roots, compute the totient and write the prime polynomial.
