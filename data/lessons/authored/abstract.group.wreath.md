# Wreath products

Wreath products studies a wreath product combines a base group of coordinatewise symmetries with a permutation group that moves the coordinates. The lesson separates the setup, the computation and the interpretation so practice can test each move.

## Order of a wreath product

Order of a wreath product starts from the defining objects. Identify what is given, state the governing relation and check the hypotheses before substituting numbers.

## Size of the base group

Size of the base group carries out the computation. Work with explicit small data, simplify exactly and keep the signs and dimensions consistent.

## Permutation action on coordinates

Permutation action on coordinates interprets the result and checks it against the definition. Compare with a second example and state what the answer means in wreath products.

A complete answer names the relation, substitutes the data and states the result in the language of the problem. When a second method is available, compare the two results before moving on.
