# Difference in differences

Difference in differences compares the change in a treated group with the change in an untreated group. The common trend assumption supplies the counterfactual change.

## The estimator

The estimate is the treated change minus the control change. Each change is a post-period mean minus a pre-period mean.

## The table

A two-by-two table has four cells. The estimate combines them with signs plus, minus, minus, plus.

## The assumption

Without treatment the two groups would have followed parallel trends. That assumption is not testable from the post period alone.

To check your understanding, take the treated change, fill the four cells and state parallel trends.

Difference in differences removes a fixed group effect by differencing twice.
