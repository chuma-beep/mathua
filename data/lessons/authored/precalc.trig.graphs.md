# Graphs of sine and cosine

The graphs of sine and cosine are waves with the same shape shifted by a quarter turn. Amplitude, period, phase shift and midline describe every transformation.

## Parameters

The coefficient in front sets the amplitude, the coefficient of the angle sets the period, the constant inside sets the phase shift and the constant outside sets the midline.

## Five key points

One period is divided into four equal parts at which the wave reaches its midline, maximum, midline, minimum and midline again.

## Phase shift

A shift inside the angle moves the wave horizontally. Factoring the frequency out of the angle reveals the shift with the correct sign.

To check your understanding, read the amplitude, mark the midline and factor the frequency.
