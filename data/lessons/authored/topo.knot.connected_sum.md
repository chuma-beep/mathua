# Connected sums of knots

The connected sum cuts a small arc from each knot and joins the four endpoints. Additive invariants turn the operation into arithmetic.

## Crossing numbers

Crossing numbers add under connected sum. The sum of the trefoil and the figure-eight has crossing number seven.

## Genera

Seifert genera add under connected sum. Two trefoils have genus two.

## Alexander polynomials

Alexander polynomials multiply under connected sum. Their degrees therefore add, because the leading terms multiply.

To check your understanding, add the crossing numbers, add the genera and multiply the polynomials.

Connected sum is the addition of the knot monoid.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller values and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
