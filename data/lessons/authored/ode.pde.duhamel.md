# Duhamel's principle

Duhamel's principle builds the response to a time-dependent forcing from the response to an impulse.

## Identify the impulse response

Solve the homogeneous problem with a unit impulse at time zero. The response is the fundamental solution. It is the building block for every later forcing.

## Write the convolution

The response to a forcing f is the convolution of f with the impulse response. The convolution integrates the impulse responses started at every earlier time. This is the continuous superposition of impulses.

## Evaluate the response

Substitute the forcing into the convolution integral. The integral can be evaluated directly or with a Laplace transform. The initial condition is added separately if it is nonzero.
