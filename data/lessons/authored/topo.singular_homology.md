# Singular homology

Singular homology studies singular homology uses all continuous simplices rather than a fixed triangulation. The lesson separates the setup, the computation and the interpretation so practice can test each move.

## Components

Components starts from the defining objects. Identify what is given, state the governing relation and check the hypotheses before substituting numbers.

## Wedge of circles

Wedge of circles carries out the computation. Work with explicit small data, simplify exactly and keep the signs and dimensions consistent.

## Euler characteristic

Euler characteristic interprets the result and checks it against the definition. Compare with a second example and state what the answer means in singular homology.

A complete answer names the relation, substitutes the data and states the result in the language of the problem. When a second method is available, compare the two results before moving on.
