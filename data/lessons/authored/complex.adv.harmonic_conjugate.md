# Harmonic conjugates

A harmonic conjugate v pairs with a harmonic function u to form an analytic function u plus i v.

## Find the conjugate

Integrate the Cauchy-Riemann equations. The x derivative of v equals minus the y derivative of u. The y derivative of v equals the x derivative of u. The two integrations must agree up to a constant.

## Verify the Cauchy-Riemann equations

Differentiate the proposed pair. The two Cauchy-Riemann identities must hold at every point. A pair that satisfies them is analytic as a complex function.

## Check harmonicity

The Laplacian of u must be zero. A harmonic conjugate exists locally when u is harmonic. The conjugate is unique up to an additive constant.
