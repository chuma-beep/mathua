# Estimators

An estimator is a statistic used to guess a population parameter. It is a random variable because it depends on the sample.

## Definition

A point estimator maps the sample to a value in the parameter space. Its distribution is the sampling distribution.

## Examples

The sample mean estimates a population mean and the sample variance estimates a population variance.

## Quality

An estimator is judged by bias, variance and mean squared error, and no single estimator optimises all three at once.

To check your understanding, map the sample to a value, estimate a mean and measure bias.

An estimator is a rule, and its value is random.
