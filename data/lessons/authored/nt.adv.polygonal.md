# Polygonal numbers

Polygonal numbers arrange dots into regular polygons. The s-gonal sequence is quadratic in the index, and its consecutive differences are arithmetic.

## The polygonal formula

The nth s-gonal number is ((s minus 2) n squared minus (s minus 4) n) divided by 2. For s equal to 3 it gives the triangular numbers, and for s equal to 4 it gives the squares. The formula is a quadratic polynomial in n.

## Recovering the index

Given an s-gonal value, solve the quadratic for the positive integer n. Testing successive indices is a safe method for small values. The index is unique because the sequence is increasing.

## Consecutive differences

The difference between the next and current s-gonal number is (s minus 2) n plus 1. This is an arithmetic sequence in n. It explains why polygonal numbers form a quadratic family.
