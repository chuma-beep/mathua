# The Schwarz-Christoffel mapping

The Schwarz-Christoffel formula maps the upper half-plane conformally onto a polygon.

## Identify the vertices

The preimages of the polygon vertices lie on the real axis. An n-gon has n such prevertices, with one often at infinity. The order of the vertices determines the order of the factors.

## Apply the exponent formula

The derivative is a product of z minus x sub k raised to alpha sub k minus 1. The exponent is the exterior angle divided by pi minus 1. For a right angle the exponent is minus one half.

## Check the mapping

The exponents sum to minus 2 for a bounded polygon. Integrating the derivative gives the map. The constants of integration translate and rotate the polygon.
