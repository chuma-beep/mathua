# Radian measure

A radian is the angle that subtends an arc equal to the radius. A full turn is two pi radians, which makes radian measure the natural unit for calculus.

## Conversion

Multiply degrees by pi over 180 to obtain radians and multiply radians by 180 over pi to return. The conversion is a change of unit, not of angle.

## Arc length

On a circle of radius r, an angle of theta radians cuts an arc of length r theta, which is the definition restated.

## Common angles

Thirty degrees is pi over six, forty-five is pi over four and sixty is pi over three. These three anchor most exact values.

To check your understanding, multiply by pi over 180, multiply radius by angle and recall the fractions.

Radian measure is dimensionless, which is why it appears inside every trigonometric derivative.
