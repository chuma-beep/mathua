# The explicit formula

The explicit formula writes the Chebyshev function as a main term minus a sum over the zeros. It is the exact bridge between primes and the zeta function.

## The main term

The dominant contribution to the Chebyshev function is the argument itself. Every other term is a correction controlled by a zero.

## The zero sum

Each nontrivial zero contributes a power of the argument divided by the zero. Conjugate zeros pair up, so the sum is real.

## The constant term

The constant correction involves the logarithm of two pi. It is small compared with the main term but essential for exactness.

To check your understanding, identify the identity term, pair conjugate zeros and name the constant.

The explicit formula is an equality, not an approximation: primes on one side, zeros on the other, with the main term doing most of the work.
