# Linking numbers

The linking number measures how two components of a link wind around each other. It is half the signed sum of the crossings between the two components.

## The crossing sum

For a two-component link, the linking number is half the signed sum of mixed crossings. Only crossings between the two components are counted.

## The Hopf link

The Hopf link has two mixed crossings of the same sign. Its linking number is one.

## The unlink

Two separated circles have no mixed crossings. Their linking number is zero, and any link with linking number zero may still be nontrivial.

To check your understanding, sum the mixed crossings, count the mixed crossings and count the mixed crossings.

Linking number is the simplest invariant of a two-component link.
