# Symmetry tests for polar graphs

Polar graphs can have symmetry about the polar axis, the vertical axis or the pole. Substitution tests detect each kind.

## Three symmetry tests

Replace theta by minus theta for polar-axis symmetry. Replace r by minus r for symmetry about the pole. Replace theta by pi minus theta for symmetry about the vertical axis.

## Substitution

Apply one replacement and simplify. If the equation is unchanged, the graph has that symmetry. A failed test does not prove the absence of symmetry because another representation may pass.

## Graphing consequences

A symmetry reduces the amount of curve that must be plotted. It also explains repeated petals and mirrored branches. The tests are a guide rather than a complete classification.
