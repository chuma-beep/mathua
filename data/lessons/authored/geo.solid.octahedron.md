# Regular octahedra

A regular octahedron has eight equilateral triangular faces. It is dual to the cube: its six vertices sit at the face centres of a cube.

## Faces and edges

An octahedron has eight faces, six vertices and twelve edges. Four faces meet at every vertex.

## The dual cube

Placing a vertex at each face centre of a cube gives an octahedron, and placing a vertex at each face centre of an octahedron gives a cube.

## Coordinates

The six vertices can be placed at the six signed coordinate directions. The distance between opposite vertices is twice the circumradius.
