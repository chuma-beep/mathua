# Rotation by multiplication

Multiplication by i rotates the plane by ninety degrees about the origin. Repeated multiplication cycles through four positions.

## Quarter turns

Multiplying \(z=a+bi\) by \(i\) gives \(-b+ai\), which swaps the coordinates and changes one sign.

## Coordinates

The four products \(z,iz,-z,-iz\) form a square centred at the origin.

## Cycles

Four quarter turns return every point to itself, so the rotation has order four.

To check your understanding, apply one quarter turn, list the four positions and count the applications.

The imaginary unit is the quarter-turn operator of the plane.
