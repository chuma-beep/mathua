# Local rings

A local ring has a unique maximal ideal. Localising at a prime produces one, and local information often determines global behaviour.

## Definition

A ring is local when it has exactly one maximal ideal, which then contains every non-unit.

## Localisation

Localising at a prime ideal \(\mathfrak{p}\) gives a local ring whose maximal ideal is the image of \(\mathfrak{p}\).

## Residue field

The quotient by the maximal ideal is a field, called the residue field.

To check your understanding, count maximal ideals, invert the complement and take the quotient.
