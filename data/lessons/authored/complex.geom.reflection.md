# Reflection in the axes

Conjugation reflects across the real axis, and negation of the conjugate reflects across the imaginary axis.

## Real axis

\(\bar z=a-bi\) has the same real part and the opposite imaginary part, so it is the mirror image in the real axis.

## Imaginary axis

\(-\bar z=-a+bi\) reflects \(z\) in the imaginary axis.

## Two reflections

Applying the same reflection twice returns the original point, and composing the two axis reflections gives a half turn.

To check your understanding, conjugate a point, negate the conjugate and apply twice.

Conjugation is reflection, and units generate the symmetries of the square.
