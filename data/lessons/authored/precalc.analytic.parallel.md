# Line parallelism and perpendicularity

Parallel lines never meet and share a slope. Perpendicular lines meet at a right angle and have slopes whose product is minus one.

## Parallel test

Two non-vertical lines are parallel exactly when their slopes are equal. Vertical lines are parallel to each other.

## Perpendicular test

A line with slope m is perpendicular to every line with slope minus one over m. A horizontal line is perpendicular to every vertical line.

## Building a line

Use the required slope through a given point in point-slope form, then rearrange if standard form is wanted.

To check your understanding, compare slopes, negate the slope and choose the slope.

Perpendicularity is a negative reciprocal, not a negative slope.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller coefficients and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
