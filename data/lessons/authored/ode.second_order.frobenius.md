# The Frobenius method

The Frobenius method finds a series solution near a regular singular point. The indicial equation gives the possible exponents.

## Find the indicial roots

Substitute a series with exponent r into the differential equation. The lowest power of x gives the indicial equation. Its roots r1 and r2 are the possible leading exponents.

## Build the recurrence

The next powers of x give a recurrence for the coefficients. The recurrence starts from the indicial root and determines every later coefficient. A repeated root or roots differing by an integer can require a logarithmic term.

## State the solution near the singularity

The series with the larger real part is the first solution. A second independent solution may need a logarithm or a second series. The radius of convergence reaches the next singular point.
