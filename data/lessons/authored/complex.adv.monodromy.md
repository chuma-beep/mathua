# Monodromy

Monodromy measures how an analytic continuation changes when the path winds around a singularity.

## Follow a loop

Continue the function along a closed loop that avoids the singularities. The endpoint may lie on a different branch. The change is the monodromy of the loop.

## Compute the change of branch

For the logarithm, one counterclockwise loop adds 2 pi i. For a power z to the alpha, the change is a factor of e to the 2 pi i alpha. The winding number multiplies the change.

## Describe the monodromy

The monodromy group collects the changes from all loops. A trivial monodromy means the continuation is single-valued on the punctured domain. A nontrivial group signals a branch point.
