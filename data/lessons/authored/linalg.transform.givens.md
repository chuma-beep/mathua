# Givens rotations

A Givens rotation rotates a plane by an angle with a prescribed cosine and sine. It is used to introduce zeros one entry at a time.

## Rotation matrix

A 2x2 Givens rotation has cosine c and sine s on the diagonal and minus s and s off the diagonal. Its determinant is c squared plus s squared, which is 1. The matrix is orthogonal.

## Scaling the matrix

Multiplying the rotation by a common denominator clears the fractions. The (2,1) entry becomes the sine numerator. Scaling does not change the rotation, only the representation.

## Rotating a vector

The first component of G times (x,y) is c x minus s y. Multiplying by the common denominator clears the fraction. The result is an integer when the data are chosen from a Pythagorean triple.
