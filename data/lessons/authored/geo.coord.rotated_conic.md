# Rotations of conics

Rotating the plane changes the coefficients of a conic but preserves its shape. The discriminant and the trace are invariants.

## The quadratic form

A general conic has quadratic, linear and constant terms, and a rotation mixes only the quadratic coefficients.

## Invariants

The trace and the determinant of the quadratic matrix survive a rotation, so the conic type does not change.

## Right angles

A rotation by a right angle exchanges the squared terms and changes the sign of the cross term.

To check your understanding, collect quadratic terms, add the diagonal and swap squared terms.

Rotation is a change of coordinates, not a change of the curve.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller coefficients and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
