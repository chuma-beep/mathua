# Normal extensions

A normal extension contains every conjugate of each of its elements. Equivalently, it is a splitting field of a family of polynomials.

## Definition

Every embedding of the extension into an algebraic closure lands back in the extension.

## Splitting fields

An extension is normal exactly when it is the splitting field of a set of polynomials.

## Roots of unity

The splitting field of \(x^n-1\) has degree \(\varphi(n)\) over the rationals.

To check your understanding, embed the field, factor the polynomial and count primitive roots.

Normality is stability under conjugation.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller numbers and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
