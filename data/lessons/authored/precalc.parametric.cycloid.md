# The cycloid

A cycloid is traced by a point on a rolling circle. Its parametric equations combine a linear motion with a rotation.

## The cycloid equations

For a circle of radius a rolling along the x-axis, x is a times t minus sine t and y is a times one minus cosine t. The parameter t is the rolling angle. One arch corresponds to t from zero to 2 pi.

## Maximum height

The cosine term ranges from minus one to one. The height y therefore ranges from zero to 2a. The maximum occurs at t equal to pi.

## Period

The horizontal distance between successive arches is 2 pi a. The vertical period is zero because the curve stays above the axis. The arch repeats after a full rotation.
