# Delay differential equations

A delay differential equation uses a past value of the solution. The history on the delay interval must be prescribed.

## Evaluate the history function

Before the initial time the solution is given by a history function. The delay equation reads that past value. The history is part of the data, not an initial condition at one point.

## Advance one delay step

On the first interval the delayed term is known from the history. The equation becomes an ordinary differential equation on that interval. Its endpoint supplies the history for the next interval.

## Find the characteristic roots

Substitute an exponential into the linear delay equation. The characteristic equation contains an exponential of minus lambda times the delay. It usually has infinitely many roots.
