# Tensor products of representations

The tensor product of two representations is again a representation. Its character is the pointwise product of the characters.

## Definition

The group acts diagonally on the tensor product, and dimensions multiply.

## Characters

The character of a tensor product is the product of the two characters.

## Symmetric and exterior

For a representation of dimension \(n\), the symmetric square has dimension \(n(n+1)/2\) and the exterior square has dimension \(n(n-1)/2\).

To check your understanding, act diagonally, evaluate both and count symmetric pairs.

Tensor products build new representations from old ones.
