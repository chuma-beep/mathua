# Krull dimension

The Krull dimension is the supremum of lengths of chains of prime ideals. It measures the algebraic size of a ring.

## Definition

A chain of prime ideals has length equal to the number of strict inclusions, and the dimension is the supremum of those lengths.

## Examples

A field has dimension zero, the integers have dimension one, and \(k[x_1,\dots,x_n]\) has dimension \(n\).

## Local rings

In a Noetherian local ring the dimension is finite and bounded by the number of generators of the maximal ideal.

To check your understanding, build a chain, check the field and find the maximal ideal.

Krull dimension is the algebraic counterpart of geometric dimension.
