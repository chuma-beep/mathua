# Connected sums of surfaces

The connected sum removes a disc from each surface and glues the two boundary circles. The operation is commutative, associative, and additive on genus.

## The Euler characteristic

For closed surfaces \(M_1\) and \(M_2\), \(\chi(M_1 \# M_2) = \chi(M_1) + \chi(M_2) - 2\). Two discs are removed and one annulus is inserted, which accounts for the subtraction of two.

## Genus adds

The connected sum of orientable surfaces of genera \(g_1\) and \(g_2\) has genus \(g_1 + g_2\). The neck identifies one boundary circle from each handle body, and the two handles become independent.

## Nonorientable sums

The connected sum of \(k\) projective planes has nonorientable genus \(k\). The sum of a torus and a projective plane is the nonorientable surface with crosscap number three.

To check your understanding, subtract two from the sum, add the two genera and add crosscap numbers.

The connected sum is the addition of the surface monoid.
