# Flat modules

A module is flat when tensoring with it preserves injective maps. Flatness is the module-level form of having no torsion obstructions.

## Definition

An exact sequence stays exact after tensoring with a flat module, for every choice of the other module.

## Examples

Projective modules are flat, \(\mathbb{Q}\) is flat over \(\mathbb{Z}\), and \(\mathbb{Z}/n\) is flat only when \(n=1\).

## Local test

A module is flat exactly when its localisations at maximal ideals are flat.

To check your understanding, tensor a sequence, test the rationals and localise at maximals.

Flatness is tested by tensoring short exact sequences.
