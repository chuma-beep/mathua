# The Lebesgue differentiation theorem

The Lebesgue differentiation theorem says the average of an integrable function over small intervals converges to the function at almost every point.

## Averages over intervals

The average of a linear function over an interval is its value at the midpoint. The midpoint is the average of the endpoints. The integral divided by the length gives the average.

## Derivative of an integral

The derivative of the integral of a continuous function recovers the function. This is the fundamental theorem of calculus. The Lebesgue version extends it to integrable functions almost everywhere.

## Lebesgue points

A point is a Lebesgue point when the averages shrink to the function value. Almost every point has this property. The polynomial examples are Lebesgue points everywhere.
