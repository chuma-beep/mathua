# Homothety

A homothety is a dilation about a fixed centre. It multiplies every distance from the centre by the same scale factor.

## The scale factor

A homothety of factor \(k\) sends a point at distance \(d\) from the centre to distance \(kd\).

## Areas

Lengths scale by \(k\) and areas by \(k^{2}\), because both dimensions grow.

## The centre

The centre is the only fixed point when \(k\neq 1\). A negative factor also reflects through the centre.

To check your understanding, multiply distances, square the factor and identify fixed points.

A homothety is determined by its centre and its scale factor.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller coefficients and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
