# The Pareto front

The Pareto front is the set of feasible points that no other feasible point dominates. It is the answer to a multiobjective problem before a preference is chosen.

## The set

A point belongs to the front when every other point is worse in some objective. The front can be a curve, a surface or a finite set of points.

## Finding a front

A pairwise scan marks a point dominated as soon as one other point beats it. The survivors form the front, and the scan costs one comparison per pair.

## Front size

The size of the front is a property of the instance rather than of the algorithm. A front with many points offers the decision maker a rich trade-off set and a heavier presentation.

To check your understanding, test front membership, scan pairs and count front points.

The front separates the question of what is achievable from the question of what is preferred.
