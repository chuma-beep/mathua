# The plane dot product

The dot product multiplies matching components and adds the products. It measures how much two displacements point the same way.

## The formula

For vectors (a,b) and (c,d), the dot product is ac+bd, a single number rather than a vector.

## Perpendicularity

Two nonzero vectors are perpendicular exactly when their dot product is zero, because the cosine of a right angle vanishes.

## The cosine

Dividing the dot product by both lengths gives the cosine of the angle between the vectors.

To check your understanding, multiply matching components, set the dot product to zero and divide by both lengths.

The dot product turns two displacements into one number, and that number carries the angle.
