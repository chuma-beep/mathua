# Vector fields on manifolds

A vector field chooses a tangent vector at every point. The Euler characteristic decides whether a closed manifold admits a field that never vanishes.

## The vanishing condition

A closed manifold admits a nowhere-zero vector field exactly when its Euler characteristic is zero. The torus admits one, while the sphere does not.

## Index sum

For a vector field with isolated zeros on a closed surface, the sum of the indices equals the Euler characteristic. A sphere has total index two.

## The sphere

Every vector field on the two-sphere has at least one zero. Counting with index, the minimum is two zeros of index one, or one zero of index two.

To check your understanding, read the Euler characteristic, sum the indices and count the minimum zeros.

Vector fields detect the Euler characteristic locally at their zeros.
