# Limit cycles

A limit cycle is an isolated closed orbit that nearby trajectories approach or leave. It is the standard model of a self-sustained oscillation, and its amplitude is set by the system rather than by the initial data.

## An isolated closed orbit

A closed orbit is a periodic solution. It is a limit cycle when a neighbourhood contains no other closed orbit, so the period and amplitude are not shared by a continuous family.

## Stability

The Poincare map sends a point on a cross-section to its next return. The derivative of that map at the fixed point decides whether the cycle attracts, repels, or attracts from one side and repels from the other.

## Amplitude and frequency

On a linear centre the amplitude is fixed by the initial condition. On a limit cycle the amplitude is fixed by the balance of energy gain and loss, so trajectories started inside and outside settle onto the same oscillation.

To check your understanding, identify a periodic solution, define the Poincare map and compare two initial conditions.

A limit cycle is the mechanism behind a clock, a heartbeat and a predator-prey oscillation, and all three share the same isolation property.
