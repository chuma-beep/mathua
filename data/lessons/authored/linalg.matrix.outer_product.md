# Outer products

An outer product multiplies a column vector by a row vector. The result is a matrix whose entries are all pairwise products.

## The outer-product formula

For vectors u and v, the outer product u v transpose has entry u sub i times v sub j at row i and column j. Every row is a multiple of v transpose. Every column is a multiple of u.

## Trace

The trace of an outer product is the dot product of u and v. Only the products u sub i v sub i lie on the diagonal. The trace can be positive, negative or zero.

## Rank

A nonzero outer product has rank 1. Its column space is the line through u and its row space is the line through v transpose. The outer product is the building block of every rank-one matrix.
