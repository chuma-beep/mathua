# Area from complex coordinates

The signed area of a triangle is half the imaginary part of (b-a) times the conjugate of (c-a). Integer coordinates make the doubled area an integer.

## The formula

For vertices \(a,b,c\), twice the signed area is \(\operatorname{Im}\big((b-a)\overline{(c-a)}\big)\).

## Doubled area

With integer coordinates the determinant is an integer, so the doubled area avoids fractions.

## Collinearity

The three points are collinear exactly when the doubled area is zero.

To check your understanding, form the two differences, compute the determinant and set the area to zero.

A determinant in complex notation measures area and detects collinearity.
