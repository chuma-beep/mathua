# Orthogonality relations

Characters of irreducible representations are orthonormal. The regular character decomposes with multiplicities equal to degrees.

## Inner product

The inner product of two irreducible characters is one when they agree and zero otherwise.

## Sum of squares

The sum of the squares of the irreducible degrees equals the order of the group.

## Class count

The number of irreducible characters equals the number of conjugacy classes.

To check your understanding, average over the group, square the degrees and count classes.

Orthogonality turns character theory into linear algebra.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller numbers and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
