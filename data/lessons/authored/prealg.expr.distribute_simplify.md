# Distributing and simplifying

## Distributing

The distributive property multiplies each term inside parentheses by the factor outside. The expression 3(x + 4) becomes 3x + 12. Every term inside must be multiplied.

A common mistake is to multiply only the first term. The factor outside applies to the whole group, including the constant. Draw an arrow to each term before multiplying.

## Combining terms

After distributing, combine like terms. Add the coefficients of the x terms and add the constants separately. The result should be shorter than the original expression.

Keep the signs attached to the terms. Subtracting a group changes the sign of every term inside the parentheses before the terms are combined.

## Evaluating the result

Substitute a value for x into both the original expression and the simplified expression. The two results must match. This is a strong check because it tests distribution and combining together.

The check works for any value of x, so choose a small integer that is easy to compute. If the results differ, the distribution or the sign of a term is wrong.
