# Riemann surfaces

A Riemann surface is a surface on which a multivalued function becomes single-valued. Its sheets are glued along branch cuts.

## Identify the sheets

The function z to the 1 over n has n sheets. Each sheet carries one branch of the root. The sheets are permuted when the argument winds around the origin.

## Locate a branch point

A branch point is where the sheets meet. For z to the 1 over n the branch point is the origin. A loop around it permutes the sheets cyclically.

## Compute the local model

Near a branch point the surface looks like a power map. The local exponent is the reciprocal of the sheet count. The model explains why the function is single-valued on the surface but not on the plane.
