# Parity of functions

An even function satisfies f(-x) = f(x) and an odd function satisfies f(-x) = -f(x). The first graph is symmetric about the y-axis and the second about the origin.

## Testing a rule

Substitute -x for x and simplify. If the result equals the original rule the function is even; if it is the negative of the original rule it is odd.

## Powers

A single power x^n is even when n is even and odd when n is odd. A sum of powers is even or odd only when every term has the same parity.

## Symmetry

Even and odd are symmetry statements, so a graph can be tested by reflecting it across the y-axis or rotating it by a half turn about the origin.

To check your understanding, substitute -x, check the exponent and reflect across the y-axis.

Most functions are neither even nor odd, and a sum of mixed parities is one of them.
