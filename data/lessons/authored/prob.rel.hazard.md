# The hazard rate

The hazard rate is the instantaneous failure rate conditional on survival to the current time. It describes ageing directly.

## Definition

The hazard is the density divided by the survival function, \(h(t)=f(t)/S(t)\).

## From survival

The survival function is the exponential of the negative cumulative hazard, so the hazard determines the lifetime law.

## Shapes

A decreasing hazard describes infant mortality, a constant hazard describes memorylessness and an increasing hazard describes wear-out.

To check your understanding, take the density, integrate the hazard and name a decreasing case.

The hazard rate is failure risk per unit time among survivors.
