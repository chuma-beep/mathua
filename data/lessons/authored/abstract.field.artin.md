# Artin's theorem

Artin's theorem identifies the fixed field of a finite automorphism group with a Galois extension whose group is the given one.

## Statement

If \(G\) is a finite group of automorphisms of a field \(E\), then \(E\) is Galois over the fixed field \(E^G\) with group \(G\).

## Degree

The degree \([E:E^G]\) equals the order of \(G\).

## Converse

Every finite Galois extension arises this way from its Galois group.

To check your understanding, take the fixed field, count automorphisms and start from a Galois extension.
