# Conjugate pairs

A real polynomial has nonreal roots in conjugate pairs, so their number is even.

## The pairing

If \(p\) has real coefficients and \(p(z)=0\), then \(p(\bar z)=\overline{p(z)}=0\) as well.

## Counting

Nonreal roots therefore come in pairs, and the count of nonreal roots is even.

## Odd degree

An odd-degree real polynomial has at least one real root, because the nonreal roots cannot account for the odd degree alone.

To check your understanding, conjugate a root, count nonreal roots and use the degree parity.

Complex conjugation pairs the nonreal roots of every real polynomial.
