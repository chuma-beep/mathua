# Rank factorization

A rank factorization writes a rank-one matrix as an outer product of two vectors. It exposes the row space and the column space at once.

## Rank-one factorisation

A matrix of the form u times v transpose has rank 1 when both vectors are nonzero. Every entry is the product of a component of u and a component of v. The row space is spanned by v transpose and the column space by u.

## Trace

The trace of u v transpose is the dot product of u and v. Only the diagonal entries contribute. The trace is therefore a scalar summary of the two factors.

## Sum of entries

The sum of all entries of u v transpose is the product of the sum of the components of u and the sum of the components of v. This follows by expanding the outer product. It is a quick check on a factorisation.
