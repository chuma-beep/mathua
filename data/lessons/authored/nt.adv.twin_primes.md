# Twin primes

Twin primes are pairs of primes that differ by 2. Their distribution is one of the oldest open problems in number theory.

## Twin pairs

A twin-prime pair has the form p and p plus 2, with both numbers prime. The first pairs are 3 and 5, 5 and 7, 11 and 13 and 17 and 19. The gap between the primes is always 2.

## Searching upward

To find the first pair above a bound, test successive candidates p and p plus 2. The first p that makes both prime is the answer. The search is finite but the bound should stay small enough to check by hand.

## Testing a candidate

A single number is the smaller member of a twin pair exactly when both it and the number two larger are prime. Primality of both numbers is the whole test. A prime gap larger than 2 rules the candidate out.
