# Perfect fields

A field is perfect when every algebraic extension is separable. Finite fields and fields of characteristic zero are perfect.

## Definition

Every irreducible polynomial over a perfect field has distinct roots in a splitting field.

## Examples

Finite fields are perfect, and so is every field of characteristic zero. The field \(\mathbb{F}_p(t)\) is not perfect.

## Inseparable degree

A purely inseparable extension of degree \(p^e\) has \(e\) inseparable layers.

To check your understanding, take an irreducible, check a finite field and take the p-th root.

Perfect fields are exactly those with no inseparable extensions.
