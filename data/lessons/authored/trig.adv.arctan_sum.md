# The arctangent addition formula

The tangent addition formula gives the tangent of a sum of two arctangents. Its numerator and denominator are simple combinations of the two inputs, but the quadrant needs care.

## The tangent addition formula

For arctan a plus arctan b, the tangent of the sum is a plus b divided by one minus ab. The numerator is the sum of the two inputs. The denominator is one minus their product.

## The denominator and the quadrant

The denominator can be positive, zero or negative. A zero denominator means the sum is plus or minus pi over two. A negative denominator signals that the principal arctangent value needs a quadrant correction.

## When the correction is needed

For positive a and b, the sum stays strictly between negative pi over two and pi over two exactly when ab is less than one. When ab is at least one, the sum reaches or crosses the boundary and the correction code is one.
