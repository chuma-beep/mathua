# Records of a sequence

A record occurs when a new observation exceeds every earlier one. For independent continuous observations the record indicators are independent.

## Record probability

The \(k\)-th observation is a record with probability \(1/k\), because all orders of the first \(k\) values are equally likely.

## Expected count

The expected number of records among \(n\) observations is the harmonic number \(H_n\).

## Independence

The record indicators are independent, a stronger fact than the product of their probabilities.

To check your understanding, compare the k-th value, sum the probabilities and take two record indicators.

Records are rare in a harmonic pattern.
