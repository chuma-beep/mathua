# Adding mixed numbers with carrying

Adding mixed numbers can produce a fraction whose numerator is at least the denominator. That extra whole must be carried into the whole-number part.

## When the fractions carry

Add the fractional parts using a common denominator. If the resulting numerator is at least the denominator, write one whole and keep the remainder. The carry is 1 in that case and 0 otherwise.

## The whole-number sum

Add the whole-number parts and then add the carry from the fractions. The new whole-number part is the sum plus the carry. Keep the fractional remainder separate.

## Regrouping the result

Write the final answer as the whole-number part and the remaining fraction over the common denominator. Reduce the fraction when possible. The sum is a mixed number in simplest form.

Practice connects the three sections: state the relation, carry out the computation and interpret the result. A second method is a useful check when one is available.

A complete answer states the governing relation, substitutes the given values and reports the result with the correct sign and units.
