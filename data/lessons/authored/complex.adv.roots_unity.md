# Roots of unity

The nth roots of unity are the complex solutions of z to the n equals 1. They divide the unit circle into n equal arcs.

## List the roots

The roots are e to the 2 pi i k over n for k from 0 to n minus 1. They are equally spaced around the unit circle. The first root is 1 and the last approaches 1 from below.

## Sum the roots

The sum of all nth roots of unity is zero for n greater than 1. The roots are the roots of z to the n minus 1, whose coefficient of z to the n minus 1 is zero. For n equal to 1 the only root is 1.

## Identify primitive roots

A primitive root generates all the others by its powers. It corresponds to an exponent k coprime to n. The number of primitive roots is the totient of n.
