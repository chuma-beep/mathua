# Levy's characterisation

Levy's characterisation says a continuous local martingale with quadratic variation \(t\) is a Brownian motion.

## Statement

If \(M\) is a continuous local martingale started at zero and \([M]_t=t\), then \(M\) is a standard Brownian motion.

## Why continuity matters

Without continuity the jump part can carry the same quadratic variation, so the conclusion fails.

## Use

The criterion identifies Brownian motion from its martingale property and its variation alone, without checking Gaussian increments.

To check your understanding, start at zero, add a jump component and check the martingale property.

Variation plus the martingale property identifies Brownian motion.
