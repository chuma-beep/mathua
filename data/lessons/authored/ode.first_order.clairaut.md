# Clairaut equations

A Clairaut equation has the form y equals x y prime plus f of y prime. Differentiating once turns it into a relation between y prime and x.

## Differentiate the Clairaut form

Differentiate y equals x p plus f of p with respect to x, where p is y prime. The terms x p and p cancel after the product rule. The result factors as x plus f prime of p times p prime equals zero.

## Find the general solution

The factor p prime equals zero gives p equal to a constant c. Substituting back gives the family y equals c x plus f of c. This is the general solution, one line for each choice of c.

## Verify the singular solution

The other factor x plus f prime of p equals zero gives the envelope. Eliminating p between that relation and the original equation gives the singular solution. Differentiating the envelope recovers the original equation.

A complete answer states the governing relation, substitutes the given values and reads the result in the language of the problem. Where a second method is available, compare the two results before moving to the next question. The three sections are one chain, and the practice sequence asks one family from each section so the learner keeps the definition, the computation and the interpretation connected.
