# Hyperbolic groups

A hyperbolic group has a Cayley graph in which every geodesic triangle is thin. Trees are the extreme case, with thinness constant zero.

## Trees

Every geodesic triangle in a tree is a tripod. Each side lies in the union of the other two, so the thinness constant \(\delta\) is zero.

## Hyperbolic space

Real hyperbolic \(n\)-space has boundary a sphere of dimension \(n - 1\). The visual boundary is one dimension lower than the space.

## Growth

Non-elementary hyperbolic groups have exponential growth. The growth rate is a quasi-isometry invariant of the group.

To check your understanding, read the thinness constant, subtract one from the dimension and recognise exponential growth.

Thin triangles are the negative-curvature condition for groups.
