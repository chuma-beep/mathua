# Sylvester's law of inertia

Over the real numbers a quadratic form has a signature: the numbers of positive, negative and zero squares in any diagonal form. The first two counts are invariant.

## Inertia

The inertia is the triple of positive, negative and zero counts. Sylvester's law says every diagonalising change of basis gives the same triple.

## Signature

The signature is the positive count minus the negative count. Together with the rank it determines the inertia.

## Definiteness

A form is positive definite when the negative and zero counts both vanish. It is indefinite when both positive and negative counts are nonzero.

To check your understanding, count positive squares, subtract the counts and test positive definiteness.

Inertia is the complete real invariant of a symmetric bilinear form up to change of basis.
