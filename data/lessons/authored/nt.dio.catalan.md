# Catalan's conjecture

Catalan's conjecture says that eight and nine are the only consecutive perfect powers. Mihailescu proved it, so the equation has exactly one solution above one.

## The statement

The only solution of a to the m minus b to the n equals one, with all bases and exponents greater than one, is three squared minus two cubed.

## The small case

The solution is eight and nine. The difference is one, and both numbers are perfect powers with exponents two and three.

## Why it is hard

The equation mixes two different exponent structures. Deep methods from algebraic number theory are needed even though the solution is tiny.
