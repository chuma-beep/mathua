# Related rates: angle of elevation

An angle of elevation changes as an object moves. The tangent ratio connects the height and the horizontal distance.

## The tangent ratio

Tangent theta equals height divided by horizontal distance. The height is constant when the observer moves. The angle is a function of the distance.

## Differentiating

Differentiate both sides with respect to time. The derivative of tangent is secant squared. The angle rate is proportional to the distance rate divided by the squared distance.

## The angle rate

A small distance rate produces a larger angle rate when the observer is close. The rate decreases as the distance grows. The sign records whether the angle is increasing or decreasing.
