# Exterior squares

The exterior square is the quotient of the tensor square by the relation that identifies a tensor with the negative of its swap. It records oriented area.

## Dimension

A basis is indexed by unordered pairs of distinct basis vectors. For dimension n that gives n(n-1)/2 basis elements.

## Alternation

The exterior product of a vector with itself is zero, because swapping the two factors multiplies the tensor by minus one. This is the defining sign rule.

## Area

In two dimensions the exterior square is one-dimensional. Its coordinate is the signed area of the parallelogram spanned by the two vectors.

To check your understanding, count distinct pairs, apply the sign rule and read the two-dimensional case.

Exterior algebra is the algebra of oriented volume, and its dimension counts subsets of a basis.
