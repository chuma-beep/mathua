# Quantile regression

Quantile regression estimates conditional quantiles rather than the conditional mean.

## Evaluate the quantile loss

The check loss weights positive and negative residuals by the quantile and one minus the quantile. The loss is asymmetric unless the quantile is one half. Minimising it gives the quantile fit.

## Estimate a coefficient

The coefficient describes how the conditional quantile changes with the predictor. It is not the same as a mean slope. Different quantiles can have different slopes.

## Predict a quantile

Evaluate the fitted line at the chosen predictor value. The result is the estimated conditional quantile. The median regression is the special case at one half.
