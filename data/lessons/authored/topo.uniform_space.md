# Uniform spaces

Uniform spaces studies a uniform space abstracts the idea of points being uniformly close without a metric. The lesson separates the setup, the computation and the interpretation so practice can test each move.

## Entourages

Entourages starts from the defining objects. Identify what is given, state the governing relation and check the hypotheses before substituting numbers.

## Uniform continuity

Uniform continuity carries out the computation. Work with explicit small data, simplify exactly and keep the signs and dimensions consistent.

## Completion

Completion interprets the result and checks it against the definition. Compare with a second example and state what the answer means in uniform spaces.

A complete answer names the relation, substitutes the data and states the result in the language of the problem. When a second method is available, compare the two results before moving on.
