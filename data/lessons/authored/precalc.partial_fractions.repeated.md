# Partial fractions with repeated factors

A repeated linear factor needs one term for each power of the factor. The coefficients are found by clearing denominators.

## Repeated linear factors

For a denominator with a squared linear factor, the decomposition includes a term over the factor and a term over its square. The numerator of the squared term is a constant. The other term may also be constant when the factor is linear.

## Clearing denominators

Multiply both sides by the full denominator. The result is a polynomial identity. Evaluate at the repeated root to isolate the highest-power coefficient.

## Evaluating coefficients

Compare coefficients or substitute convenient values to find the remaining constants. A coefficient can also be read from the numerator at a chosen point. Check the decomposition by combining the terms again.
