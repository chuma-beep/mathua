# Hadamard products

The Hadamard product multiplies matrices entry by entry. It is not the usual matrix product and it does not mix rows and columns.

## Entrywise product

The Hadamard product of A and B has entry A sub i j times B sub i j. The matrices must have the same shape. Each output entry depends only on the two entries in the same position.

## Trace

The trace of a Hadamard product adds the products of the diagonal entries. Off-diagonal entries do not contribute. The trace is therefore a sum of pairwise products along the diagonal.

## Sum of entries

The sum of all entries of a Hadamard product is the sum of the pairwise products. It can be computed directly from the two matrices. The operation is commutative and distributive over addition.
