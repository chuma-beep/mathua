# The Poisson equation

The Poisson equation prescribes the Laplacian of an unknown function. It models steady heat flow with sources, electrostatics and potential flow.

## The Laplacian

For a quadratic function, the Laplacian is constant. The coefficients determine the source strength. A positive Laplacian means a source and a negative Laplacian means a sink.

## A particular solution

For a constant source, a radial quadratic is a particular solution. In two dimensions, k times x squared plus y squared has Laplacian 4k. The coefficient k is one quarter of the source strength.

## Compatibility

On a closed surface, the total source must be balanced by the boundary flux. A Neumann problem needs the integral of the source plus the boundary data to vanish. This is the compatibility condition.
