# Average treatment effects

The average treatment effect is the mean difference between treated and control potential outcomes. It is the most common single-number summary of a causal question.

## The definition

The effect is the mean treated outcome minus the mean control outcome. It averages over the whole population, not only the treated.

## The ATT

The effect on the treated restricts the average to treated units. It differs from the ATE whenever the effect is heterogeneous.

## Estimation

Randomisation makes the difference in means unbiased. Observational data needs adjustment before the same difference is meaningful.

To check your understanding, average the treated outcomes, restrict to the treated and randomise.

The ATE is one number, and it hides the heterogeneity it averages.
