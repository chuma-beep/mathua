# The Neumann problem

The Neumann problem prescribes the normal derivative on the boundary. A solution exists only when the boundary flux is compatible with the sources.

## Normal derivative

The normal derivative is the rate of change across the boundary. On an interval it reduces to the derivative at the endpoints. The sign depends on the chosen outward normal.

## Compatibility integral

The divergence theorem turns the boundary flux into the integral of the Laplacian. For a harmonic function the total flux must be zero. In one dimension the two endpoint derivatives must be opposites.

## Constant shift

If a function solves the Neumann problem, adding a constant gives another solution. The boundary derivative is unchanged. The solution is unique only up to that constant.
