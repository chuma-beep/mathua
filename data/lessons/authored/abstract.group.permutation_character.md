# Permutation characters

A permutation character records the fixed points of each group element. Its inner product with the trivial character counts orbits.

## Definition

The character of a permutation representation evaluated at an element is the number of points that element fixes.

## Orbits

The multiplicity of the trivial character equals the number of orbits of the action.

## Cycles

A \(k\)-cycle acting on \(n\) points fixes exactly \(n-k\) of them.

To check your understanding, evaluate on elements, take the inner product and count moved points.
