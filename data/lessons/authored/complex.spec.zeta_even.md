# Zeta at even integers

At even integers the zeta function is a rational multiple of pi^{2n}, and the rational number is built from Bernoulli numbers.

## Values

\(\zeta(2)=\pi^{2}/6\), \(\zeta(4)=\pi^{4}/90\) and in general \(\zeta(2n)\) is \(\pi^{2n}\) times a rational number.

## Denominators

The denominator comes from the Bernoulli number \(B_{2n}\) and a factorial, so it grows quickly with \(n\).

## Trivial zeros

At negative even integers the zeta function vanishes, which is why those zeros are called trivial.

To check your understanding, read the value at two, use the Bernoulli number and test a negative even integer.

Even zeta values are rational multiples of powers of pi.
