# The pH scale

The pH of a solution is the negative base-ten logarithm of its hydrogen ion concentration. A decrease of one pH unit means ten times more acidity.

## The definition

pH equals minus the logarithm of the concentration in moles per litre, so a concentration of ten to the minus seven has pH seven.

## Converting back

The concentration is ten raised to the negative pH, which turns a pH difference into a ratio of concentrations.

## Neutrality

Pure water at room temperature has pH seven. Values below seven are acidic and values above seven are basic.

To check your understanding, take the concentration, negate the pH and compare with seven.

The minus sign in the definition is what makes a larger pH mean a smaller concentration.
