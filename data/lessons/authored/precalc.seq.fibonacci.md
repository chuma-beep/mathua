# Fibonacci numbers

The Fibonacci sequence starts with two ones and adds the previous two terms. Its ratios approach the golden ratio and its closed form involves that ratio.

## The recurrence

Each term is the sum of the two before it, so the sequence grows exponentially rather than linearly.

## The golden ratio

The ratio of consecutive terms tends to the positive root of x squared equals x plus one, which is the golden ratio.

## Identities

Sums of consecutive Fibonacci numbers produce a shifted Fibonacci number, a fact proved by induction on the index.

To check your understanding, add two terms, form the ratio and sum consecutive terms.

The Fibonacci recurrence is second order, so it needs two initial values.
