# The monotone convergence theorem

The monotone convergence theorem says the integral of an increasing sequence of nonnegative functions converges to the integral of the pointwise limit.

## Increasing integrals

If the functions increase pointwise, their integrals increase. The limit of the integrals is the integral of the limit. No domination is needed because the functions are nonnegative and monotone.

## Indicator sequence

The indicators of expanding intervals increase to the indicator of the union. Their integrals are the measures of the intervals. The limit integral is the measure of the union.

## Finite measure

On a set of finite measure, a constant sequence has a finite integral. The theorem still applies because the sequence is increasing. The finite measure keeps the limit finite.
