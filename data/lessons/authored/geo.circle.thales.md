# Thales' theorem

An angle inscribed in a semicircle is right. The diameter is the longest chord, and it subtends a right angle at every other point of the circle.

## The semicircle

If a triangle is inscribed in a circle and one side is a diameter, the opposite angle is a right angle. The converse also holds: a right angle subtends a diameter.

## The radius

The radius is half the diameter. A circle of diameter 14 has radius 7, and every point of the circle is that distance from the centre.

## Computing with the theorem

Given a right triangle with hypotenuse equal to the diameter, the hypotenuse is twice the radius. This relates the circumcircle of a right triangle to its longest side.

To check your understanding, state the right angle, halve the diameter and double the radius.

A right angle in a circle always faces a diameter.
