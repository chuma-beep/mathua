# Series systems

A series system works only when every component works, so its reliability is the product of the component reliabilities.

## Reliability

With independent components, series reliability is the product \(R_1\cdots R_n\).

## Hazard

Hazards add in series, because the log reliability is a sum of log reliabilities.

## Weakest link

The system is no more reliable than its least reliable component, so a long series degrades quickly.

To check your understanding, multiply the components, take logs and find the smallest factor.

Series systems multiply reliability and add hazard.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller coefficients and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
