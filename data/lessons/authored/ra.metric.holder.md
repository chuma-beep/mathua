# Holder's inequality

Holder's inequality bounds the integral of a product by the product of Lp and Lq norms. The exponents p and q are conjugate.

## The inequality

For conjugate exponents, the absolute value of the inner product is at most the product of the norms. For p equal to q equal to 2, it reduces to Cauchy-Schwarz. The dot product is the finite model.

## The equality case

Equality holds when one vector is a scalar multiple of the other. The scalar is the proportionality constant. This characterizes the extremal case.

## Conjugate exponents

The exponents satisfy one over p plus one over q equals one. For p equal to 2, q is also 2. The finite examples keep the calculations exact.
