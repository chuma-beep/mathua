# The dominated convergence theorem

The dominated convergence theorem lets a limit pass through an integral when a single integrable function dominates the sequence.

## Limit of integrals

If the functions converge pointwise and are dominated by an integrable function, the integrals converge to the integral of the limit. The dominating function prevents mass from escaping.

## The dominating function

A bound on the absolute values of the functions gives a bound on the absolute values of their integrals. The bound is the integral of the dominating function. This is the key estimate.

## Indicator sequence

Indicators of expanding intervals converge to the indicator of the union. The constant function 1 dominates them. The limit of the integrals is the measure of the union.
