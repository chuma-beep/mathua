# Symmetric polynomials

A symmetric polynomial is unchanged by every permutation of its variables. The elementary symmetric polynomials generate all of them.

## Elementary symmetric polynomials

The elementary symmetric polynomial e k is the sum of all products of k distinct variables. For three variables, e2 is ab plus ac plus bc and e3 is abc. These are the coefficients that appear in a monic polynomial with roots the variables.

## Power sums

A power sum p k is the sum of the k th powers of the variables. Newton identities convert power sums into elementary symmetric polynomials. For three variables, p2 is e1 squared minus 2e2.

## The fundamental theorem

Every symmetric polynomial in n variables is a polynomial in the elementary symmetric polynomials. This is why symmetric questions can be answered by computing e1, e2 and e3. The representation is unique when the variables are independent.
