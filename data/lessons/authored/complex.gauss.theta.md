# Theta series of the square lattice

The theta series of the square lattice counts representations of n as a sum of two squares. Its coefficients are the r2 function.

## The series

\(\theta(q)=\sum_{m,n}q^{m^{2}+n^{2}}=\sum_{k\ge0}r_{2}(k)q^{k}\), so each exponent records a squared distance.

## Coefficients

The coefficient of \(q^{k}\) is the number of signed lattice points on the circle of squared radius \(k\).

## Modularity

Under a modular transformation the theta series changes by a factor, which is the first example of a modular form.

To check your understanding, count the terms with small indices, count signed points and state the transformation.

The theta series packages the sum-of-two-squares counts into one generating function.
