# Higher homotopy groups

Higher homotopy groups studies higher homotopy groups record maps from spheres into a space. The lesson separates the setup, the computation and the interpretation so practice can test each move.

## Spheres

Spheres starts from the defining objects. Identify what is given, state the governing relation and check the hypotheses before substituting numbers.

## Vanishing range

Vanishing range carries out the computation. Work with explicit small data, simplify exactly and keep the signs and dimensions consistent.

## Commutativity

Commutativity interprets the result and checks it against the definition. Compare with a second example and state what the answer means in higher homotopy groups.

A complete answer names the relation, substitutes the data and states the result in the language of the problem. When a second method is available, compare the two results before moving on.
