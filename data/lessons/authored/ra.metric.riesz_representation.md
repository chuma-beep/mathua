# The Riesz representation theorem

The Riesz representation theorem says every bounded linear functional on a Hilbert space is given by an inner product with a unique vector.

## Representing a functional

A linear functional assigns a scalar to each vector. On finite-dimensional Euclidean space, it has the form a x plus b y. The representing vector is (a,b).

## The inner-product form

Evaluating the functional at a point is the inner product of the representing vector with that point. The components of the point weight the coefficients. The value is the dot product.

## Norm of the representative

The norm of the representing vector is the operator norm of the functional. Its square is the sum of the squared coefficients. The representative is unique.
