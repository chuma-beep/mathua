# Jordan's lemma

Jordan's lemma bounds the semicircular part of a contour integral when the integrand carries e^{iz} and the denominator has larger degree.

## Degree gap

The semicircular contribution vanishes as the radius grows when the denominator degree exceeds the numerator degree by at least one and the exponential decays in the upper half-plane.

## Semicircle

The bound is uniform in the radius, so the arc integral tends to zero even though its length grows.

## Half-plane poles

Only poles in the chosen half-plane contribute, so the residue sum runs over those poles.

To check your understanding, compare the degrees, bound the arc and locate the poles.

Jordan's lemma lets a closed contour replace an integral over the real line.
