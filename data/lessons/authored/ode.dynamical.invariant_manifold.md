# Invariant manifolds

At a hyperbolic fixed point the stable and unstable eigenspaces of the linearisation persist as curved invariant manifolds. They organise which initial conditions approach the point and which leave.

## Stable and unstable sets

The stable set consists of points whose orbit tends to the fixed point as time goes forward. The unstable set consists of points whose orbit tends to it as time goes backward.

## The manifold theorem

Near a hyperbolic fixed point the stable and unstable sets are smooth manifolds. Their dimensions equal the number of eigenvalues with negative and positive real parts, and their tangent spaces are the corresponding eigenspaces.

## Global consequences

The manifolds extend beyond the neighbourhood and can meet again. A transverse intersection of a stable and an unstable manifold produces a homoclinic tangle, which is the geometric source of chaotic transport.

To check your understanding, define the stable set, count the stable eigenvalues and extend the manifolds globally.

The manifolds are the skeleton of the phase portrait, and the linear eigenspaces give their tangent directions without any nonlinear computation.
