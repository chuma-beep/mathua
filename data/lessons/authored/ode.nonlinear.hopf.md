# The Hopf bifurcation

A Hopf bifurcation occurs when a pair of complex eigenvalues crosses the imaginary axis. A steady state then gives way to a small limit cycle whose amplitude grows from zero.

## The critical parameter

The bifurcation happens at the parameter value where the eigenvalue pair reaches the imaginary axis. Below that value the steady state is stable and above it the cycle appears.

## The frequency

At the crossing the imaginary part gives the angular frequency of the new cycle. For a linear part with squared frequency \(k\), the frequency itself is \(\sqrt{k}\).

## The amplitude

Just past the bifurcation the cycle amplitude grows like the square root of the distance from the critical parameter. The squared amplitude is therefore proportional to that distance.

To check your understanding, locate the imaginary crossing, read the imaginary part and measure the parameter gap.

A Hopf bifurcation trades a steady state for a limit cycle.
