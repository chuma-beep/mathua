# Simplifying with the greatest common factor

A fraction is simplified by dividing its numerator and denominator by their greatest common factor. The value does not change and the result is in lowest terms in one step.

## The rule

For positive whole numbers \(a\) and \(b\), let \(g\) be the greatest common factor of \(a\) and \(b\). Then \(\frac{a}{b} = \frac{a \div g}{b \div g}\). Dividing both parts by the same nonzero number keeps the quotient and choosing the largest common factor leaves no common factor in the result. For 18 and 24 the factor 6 gives \(\frac{3}{4}\).

## A worked example

Simplify \(\frac{18}{24}\). The factors of 18 include 1, 2, 3, 6, 9 and 18, while those of 24 include 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 and 24. The largest shared value is 6, so divide both parts by 6 and obtain \(\frac{3}{4}\). A larger case is \(\frac{84}{126}\), where the greatest common factor is 42 and the result is \(\frac{2}{3}\). The same answer arrives in two steps through \(\frac{42}{63}\) and then \(\frac{14}{21}\).

## Checking the result

Cross multiply the simplified fraction against the original: \(18 \times 4 = 72\) and \(24 \times 3 = 72\), so the value is unchanged. Then confirm that the new numerator and denominator share no factor above 1, which holds for 3 and 4. One step suffices because every common factor divides the greatest one, so nothing is left to remove.

The greatest common factor is the largest single move toward lowest terms.
