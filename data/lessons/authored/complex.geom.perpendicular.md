# Perpendicularity conditions

Two vectors are perpendicular when their dot product vanishes, and parallel when their determinant vanishes.

## Dot product

For \(u=a+bi\) and \(v=c+di\), the dot product is \(ac+bd\), the real part of \(u\bar v\).

## Determinant

The determinant \(ad-bc\), the imaginary part of \(u\bar v\), measures the signed area of the parallelogram.

## Right angles

Perpendicularity is the vanishing of the dot product, which in complex form is \(\operatorname{Re}(u\bar v)=0\).

To check your understanding, multiply the real parts, cross multiply and test the dot product.

Real and imaginary parts of one product test perpendicularity and parallelism.
