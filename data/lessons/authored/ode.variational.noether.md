# Noether's theorem

Noether's theorem pairs every continuous symmetry of a Lagrangian with a conserved quantity. A translation gives momentum, a rotation gives angular momentum and a time shift gives energy.

## A one-parameter symmetry

A symmetry is a one-parameter family of transformations of the dependent and independent variables that leaves the action unchanged. The Lagrangian may change by a total derivative, which does not affect the equations of motion.

## The conserved current

The variation of the Lagrangian under the symmetry is integrated by parts. The Euler-Lagrange equation removes the bulk term, and the surviving boundary expression is constant along every solution.

## Examples

Invariance under a spatial translation gives conservation of momentum. Invariance under a time translation gives conservation of energy, and invariance under a rotation gives conservation of angular momentum.

To check your understanding, write the transformation, vary the Lagrangian and match a translation to momentum.

Noether's theorem is the reason conservation laws exist at all, and it converts a symmetry check into a conserved quantity without solving the equation.
