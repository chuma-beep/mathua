# Direct limits

A direct limit glues a directed system of modules into one module. It is the colimit of the diagram.

## Definition

Elements of the limit are equivalence classes of elements in the system, with two elements identified once they agree later.

## Universal property

Every compatible family of maps out of the system factors uniquely through the limit.

## Stages

A chain of n stages contributes n modules before the limit is taken.

To check your understanding, take the disjoint union, draw the cone and index the stages.

Direct limits are colimits of directed diagrams.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller numbers and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
