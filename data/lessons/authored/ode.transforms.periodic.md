# Laplace transforms of periodic functions

A periodic function has a Laplace transform determined by one period. The transform is a geometric series of shifted copies.

## Compute a periodic transform

Integrate the function over one period and divide by one minus the exponential of minus s times the period. The numerator is the transform of the first period. The denominator sums the repeated periods.

## Identify the period

The period is the smallest positive T with f of t plus T equal to f of t. Read it from the graph or the formula. The transform formula uses that T, not the frequency alone.

## Invert the periodic response

The inverse transform is a sum of shifted copies of the single-period response. The geometric series can be inverted term by term. The result is periodic with the same period.
