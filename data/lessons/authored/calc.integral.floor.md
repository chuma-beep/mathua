# Integrals of floor functions

The integral of a floor function is a sum of constant pieces. Each integer interval contributes its floor value times its length.

## Staircase integrals

Between consecutive integers the floor is constant. The integral over an interval is the sum of the values times the subinterval lengths. The result is exact.

## Counting jumps

The number of jumps across an interval is the number of integers strictly inside it. Each jump changes the floor by one. The total integral can be computed by counting the steps.

## The exact sum

For a floor from zero to n, the integral equals the sum of the integers from zero to n minus one. The formula is n times n minus one divided by two. No approximation is needed.
