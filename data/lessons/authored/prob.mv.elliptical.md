# Elliptical distributions

Elliptical distributions have level sets that are ellipses centred at a common point. They share the linear structure of the multivariate normal without requiring Gaussian tails.

## Representation

A random vector is elliptical when it equals a location plus a random scale times a fixed matrix applied to a uniform direction on the sphere.

## Covariance

When the scale has finite second moment, the covariance is a multiple of the matrix defining the ellipses.

## Examples

The multivariate normal, Student t and Cauchy families are elliptical, with different tail behaviour.
