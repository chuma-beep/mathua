# Characters of group representations

Characters of group representations studies a character records the trace of every group element in a representation. The lesson separates the setup, the computation and the interpretation so practice can test each move.

## Character at the identity

Character at the identity starts from the defining objects. Identify what is given, state the governing relation and check the hypotheses before substituting numbers.

## Direct sums

Direct sums carries out the computation. Work with explicit small data, simplify exactly and keep the signs and dimensions consistent.

## Orthogonality

Orthogonality interprets the result and checks it against the definition. Compare with a second example and state what the answer means in characters of group representations.

A complete answer names the relation, substitutes the data and states the result in the language of the problem. When a second method is available, compare the two results before moving on.
