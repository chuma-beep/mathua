# Doubling time

Doubling time is the time needed for an exponential quantity to grow by a factor of two. It depends only on the growth rate.

## The doubling formula

For a quantity A times 2 to the t over T, the doubling time is T. After k doubling periods the value is A times 2 to the k. The initial value scales the result but does not change T.

## Time from a rate

A continuous growth rate r gives a doubling time of ln 2 divided by r. The natural logarithm appears because the growth is continuous. The formula is independent of the starting amount.

## Reading the doubling time

A short doubling time means fast growth. Two successive doublings multiply the quantity by four. The same language applies to decay with a half-life and a negative rate.
