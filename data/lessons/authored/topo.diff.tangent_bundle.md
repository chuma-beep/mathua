# Tangent bundles

The tangent bundle collects every tangent space into one smooth manifold. Its dimension is twice the dimension of the base, and its rank is the dimension of the base.

## Dimension

If \(M\) has dimension \(n\), the tangent bundle \(TM\) has dimension \(2n\). The fibre over each point is an \(n\)-dimensional vector space.

## Rank

The tangent bundle is a vector bundle of rank \(n\) over an \(n\)-manifold. The rank counts the fibre dimension rather than the total dimension.

## The unit tangent bundle

Restricting to unit vectors leaves a sphere of dimension \(n - 1\) in each fibre. The unit tangent bundle therefore has dimension \(2n - 1\).

To check your understanding, double the base dimension, read the fibre dimension and subtract one from twice the dimension.

The tangent bundle doubles the dimension and keeps the rank.
