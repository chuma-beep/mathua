# Direct sums

A direct sum decomposes a space into subspaces that intersect only at zero. Every vector then has a unique decomposition into components.

## Dimension

The dimension of a direct sum is the sum of the dimensions. For two subspaces this holds exactly when their intersection is zero.

## Projections

A direct sum gives projection maps onto its summands. The projections are idempotent and sum to the identity.

## Decomposition

A map that preserves a direct sum acts block diagonally. Splitting a space into invariant summands is therefore the same as block diagonalising the map.

To check your understanding, add the dimensions, build a projection and block diagonalise.

Direct sums are the algebraic form of choosing independent coordinates.
