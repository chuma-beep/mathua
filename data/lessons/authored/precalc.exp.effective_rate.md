# Effective annual rate

The effective annual rate is the single yearly growth factor that matches a nominal rate compounded several times per year. It makes competing offers comparable.

## The formula

With nominal rate r compounded n times per year, the effective rate is one plus r over n, raised to n, minus one.

## Continuous limit

As the compounding count grows without bound the effective rate approaches e to the r minus one.

## Comparison

A higher nominal rate does not always give a higher effective rate, because the compounding frequency also matters.

To check your understanding, divide the nominal rate, take the limit and convert both offers.

Compare offers on the effective rate, because that is the factor that actually multiplies the balance.
