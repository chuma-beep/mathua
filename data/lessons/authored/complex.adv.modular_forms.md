# Modular forms

A modular form is holomorphic on the upper half-plane and transforms in a prescribed way under modular substitutions.

## Identify the weight

The weight k controls the transformation factor. Under a modular substitution the form is multiplied by a power of the denominator. Eisenstein series E4 and E6 have weights 4 and 6.

## Apply the transformation

Substitute the modular transformation into the form. The weight determines the exponent. A form of odd weight with the full modular group must vanish.

## Read the q-expansion

The variable q is e to the 2 pi i z. The q-expansion is the Fourier expansion at the cusp. The constant term and the first coefficients encode arithmetic data.

A complete answer states the governing relation, substitutes the given values and reads the result in the language of the problem.
