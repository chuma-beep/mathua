# Optimization: cylinder dimensions

A cylinder optimization problem usually fixes the volume or the surface area. The constraint reduces the problem to one variable.

## Cylinder volume

The volume is pi r squared h. Fixing the volume expresses h in terms of r. The surface area or another quantity then becomes a function of r alone.

## The constraint

## The optimal dimensions

Differentiate and set the derivative to zero. For a fixed volume and minimal surface area, the height equals twice the radius. The second derivative confirms the minimum.
