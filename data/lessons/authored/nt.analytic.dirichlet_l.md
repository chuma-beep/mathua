# Dirichlet L-functions

A Dirichlet L-function twists the zeta series by a character modulo q. The twist isolates primes in a chosen residue class.

## Characters

A Dirichlet character modulo q is a completely multiplicative function that is periodic and vanishes on non-units. The number of characters equals the totient of q.

## The principal character

The principal character is one on units, so its L-function differs from the zeta function only at primes dividing q. The number of such primes is the number of distinct prime factors of q.

## Nonvanishing at one

Dirichlet proved that no nonprincipal L-function vanishes at one. That nonvanishing is exactly what makes arithmetic progressions contain infinitely many primes.

To check your understanding, count the characters, compare with zeta and state the theorem.

An L-function is the zeta function seen through a character, and the character decides which residue classes survive.
