# The Seifert genus

The Seifert genus is the smallest genus of an orientable surface whose boundary is the knot. It is a knot invariant and a lower bound for the unknotting number.

## Torus knots

The torus knot \(T(p,q)\) has Seifert genus \((p-1)(q-1)/2\). The trefoil, which is \(T(2,3)\), has genus one.

## Connected sums

Seifert genus is additive under connected sum. The sum of two trefoils has genus two.

## The unknot

The unknot bounds a disc and therefore has genus zero. A knot of genus zero is the unknot.
