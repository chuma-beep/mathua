# Derived functors

Derived functors measure the failure of a functor to be exact. Ext and Tor are the standard examples.

## Construction

Take a projective resolution, apply the functor and take homology of the resulting complex.

## Vanishing

The n-th derived functor vanishes beyond the projective dimension of the module.

## Length

A resolution of length n produces n+1 derived functors, indexed from zero.

To check your understanding, resolve the module, find the dimension and count the terms.

Derived functors turn a non-exact functor into a sequence.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller numbers and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
