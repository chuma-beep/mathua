# Elementary matrices

Elementary matrices encode a single row operation. Multiplying by one performs that operation on another matrix.

## Row operations as matrices

An elementary matrix is the identity with one row operation applied. Adding a multiple of one row to another produces an entry below or above the diagonal. Swapping rows or scaling a row gives the other two types.

## Determinants

A row-addition elementary matrix has determinant 1. A row swap has determinant minus 1. A row scaling by k has determinant k, which is why the determinant tracks the volume change.

## Inverses and entries

The inverse of a row-addition matrix adds the opposite multiple. The inverse of a swap is the same swap. Entries of the elementary matrix record the operation directly.
