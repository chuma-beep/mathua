# The gamma function at half-integers

The gamma function extends the factorial, and at half-integers its values carry sqrt(pi) times a rational number.

## Factorials

\(\Gamma(n+1)=n!\) for every nonnegative integer \(n\), so gamma interpolates the factorial.

## Half-integers

\(\Gamma(1/2)=\sqrt{\pi}\), and the recursion produces \(\Gamma(n+1/2)=\frac{(2n)!}{4^{n}n!}\sqrt{\pi}\).

## Duplication

The duplication formula relates \(\Gamma(z)\Gamma(z+1/2)\) to \(\Gamma(2z)\), which pins the half-integer constants.

To check your understanding, evaluate at an integer, use the half-integer formula and apply duplication.

Gamma is the factorial with a square root of pi at the half-integers.
