# Equivalence classes

An equivalence relation partitions a set into classes of mutually related elements. The classes are disjoint, cover the set, and any member can represent its class.

## The three properties

Reflexivity, symmetry and transitivity are exactly what makes the classes partition the set. Dropping any one of them breaks the partition.

## The partition

Two classes are either identical or disjoint. If they shared an element, transitivity would relate every member of one to every member of the other.

## Quotients

The set of classes is the quotient set. A function on the quotient is well defined only when it gives the same value on every representative.

To check your understanding, check reflexivity, show classes cover and form the class set.

Equivalence classes are the set-theoretic version of identifying objects that behave the same. Well-definedness on a quotient is the recurring proof obligation.
