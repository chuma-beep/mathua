# Projective modules

A projective module is a direct summand of a free module. It lifts maps along surjections, which makes it the right notion for splitting sequences.

## Definition

Every surjection onto a projective module splits, so the module is a direct summand of a free module.

## Lifting

A map from a projective module into a quotient lifts to the source.

## Rank

Finitely generated projective modules over a local ring are free, so rank is well defined there.

To check your understanding, find a free cover, draw the square and localise.

Projective modules behave like free modules without being free.
