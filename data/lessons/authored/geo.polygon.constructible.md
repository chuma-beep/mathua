# Constructible polygons

A regular polygon is constructible with straightedge and compass exactly when its side count is a power of two times a product of distinct Fermat primes.

## The criterion

Gauss proved that \(n\) is constructible when \(n=2^{a}p_{1}\cdots p_{r}\) with distinct Fermat primes \(p_{i}\).

## The Fermat primes

Only five Fermat primes are known: 3, 5, 17, 257 and 65537.

## Small cases

The triangle, square, pentagon and hexagon are constructible; the regular heptagon is not.

To check your understanding, state the factorisation, list the known primes and check the triangle.

Constructibility is a number-theoretic condition on the side count.
