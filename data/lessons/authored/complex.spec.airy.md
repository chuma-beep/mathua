# Airy functions

Airy functions solve y''=xy and describe turning points, with a fixed Wronskian.

## Equation

The Airy equation \(y''=xy\) has two independent solutions, \(\operatorname{Ai}\) and \(\operatorname{Bi}\).

## Two solutions

Because the equation is second order, its solution space has dimension two and every solution is a combination of the pair.

## Wronskian

The Wronskian of \(\operatorname{Ai}\) and \(\operatorname{Bi}\) is a nonzero constant, so the two solutions are independent everywhere.

To check your understanding, read the order, count the basis and compute the Wronskian.

The Airy equation is the simplest model of a turning point.
