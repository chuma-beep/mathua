# Direction cosines

The direction cosines of a vector are its components divided by its length. Their squares sum to one.

## The direction-cosine formula

For a vector with components a, b and c, the cosine with the x-axis is a divided by the length. The other two cosines use b and c. Each cosine is between minus one and one.

## The identity

Squaring the three cosines and adding gives one. This is the three-dimensional Pythagorean identity. It is a quick check on a normalized direction.

## Recovering a component

A component is the length times the corresponding direction cosine. Given the length and one cosine, the component follows by multiplication. The sign of the cosine fixes the direction.
