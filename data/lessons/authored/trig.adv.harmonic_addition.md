# Combining sine and cosine into one wave

A sine wave plus a cosine wave of the same frequency is another sine wave. The amplitude is the hypotenuse of the coefficient triangle and the phase shift records their ratio.

## The amplitude

For a sine x plus b cosine x, the amplitude R satisfies R squared equals a squared plus b squared. A Pythagorean triple keeps R an integer. The amplitude is the hypotenuse of the triangle with legs a and b.

## The phase relation

The expanded form R sine of x plus phi has sine coefficient R cosine phi and cosine coefficient R sine phi. Thus R cosine phi equals a. The phase is determined by the two coefficients.

## Evaluating the combined wave

At x equal to zero, sine is zero and cosine is one, so the value is b. Evaluating at other exact angles gives further integer checks. The combined wave is useful for adding oscillations with the same frequency.
