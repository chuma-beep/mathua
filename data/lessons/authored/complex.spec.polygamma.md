# The polygamma function

The polygamma functions are derivatives of the digamma function, and their differences give harmonic numbers.

## Digamma

\(\psi(z)=\Gamma'(z)/\Gamma(z)\), and \(\psi(1)=-\gamma\).

## Harmonic numbers

\(\psi(n+1)-\psi(1)=H_{n}\), the \(n\)-th harmonic number.

## Poles

Every polygamma function has a pole of order one at each nonpositive integer.

To check your understanding, differentiate the log gamma, take the difference and locate a pole.
