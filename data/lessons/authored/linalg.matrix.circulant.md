# Circulant matrices

A circulant matrix has each row equal to a cyclic shift of the first row. Its eigenvalues are discrete Fourier coefficients.

## Circulant determinant

For a 3x3 circulant with first row a, b and c, the determinant is a cubed plus b cubed plus c cubed minus 3abc. This is the product of the three Fourier eigenvalues. The formula is a compact test of the matrix.

## Trace

Every diagonal entry of a circulant with first row a, b and c is a. The trace is therefore 3a. The other entries of the first row do not appear on the diagonal.

## Entries

The first row is a, b and c. The second row is c, a and b. The third row is b, c and a. The (1,2) entry is b by construction.
