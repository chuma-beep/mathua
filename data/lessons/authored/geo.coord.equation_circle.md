# Circle equations from centre and radius

A circle is the set of points at a fixed distance from its centre. Squaring that distance gives the standard equation.

## The equation

The circle with centre (h,k) and radius r is (x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2.

## Completing the square

Expanding the equation and completing the square recovers the centre and the radius.

## Translation

Changing the centre translates the circle without changing its radius.

To check your understanding, write the squared distance, group x terms and move the centre.

The squared form avoids square roots and keeps the equation polynomial.
