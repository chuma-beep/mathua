# The cup product

The cup product multiplies cohomology classes. It turns the direct sum of cohomology groups into a graded ring.

## Degree

The cup product of a class of degree p and a class of degree q has degree p plus q. The product is bilinear and associative. Degree is therefore additive under the cup product.

## Graded commutativity

The cup product is graded commutative. Swapping the two classes multiplies the product by minus one to the product of the degrees. The sign is trivial when either degree or the product of the degrees is even.

## Cohomology rings

The cup product gives the cohomology groups a ring structure. For complex projective n-space the ring is a truncated polynomial ring on a degree-two class. The first power that vanishes is n plus one.
