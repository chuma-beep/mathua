# Partial fractions with irreducible quadratic factors

An irreducible quadratic factor needs a linear numerator. The discriminant tells whether the quadratic can be factored over the reals.

## Irreducible quadratic factors

The decomposition includes a term whose numerator is linear. The denominator is the irreducible quadratic. A repeated quadratic factor needs one term for each power.

## Clearing denominators

Multiply by the full denominator and collect like powers. The resulting identity determines the linear numerator and the other constants. Substituting the roots of the linear factors simplifies the system.

## The discriminant

A negative discriminant means the quadratic has no real roots and is irreducible over the reals. A zero discriminant gives a repeated linear factor. A positive discriminant permits two linear factors.
