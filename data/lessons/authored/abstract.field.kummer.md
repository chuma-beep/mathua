# Kummer extensions

Kummer theory describes abelian extensions of a field containing enough roots of unity. They are generated by n-th roots.

## Setting

The base field contains a primitive n-th root of unity, so the n-th power map has the right kernel.

## Degree

Adjoining an n-th root generates an extension whose degree divides n.

## Galois group

The Galois group is a subgroup of the n-th roots of unity, hence abelian.

To check your understanding, adjoin a root of unity, adjoin the root and act on the root.

Kummer extensions turn abelian extensions into root adjunctions.
