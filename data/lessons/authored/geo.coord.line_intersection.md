# Intersecting lines

Two non-parallel lines meet in exactly one point. Solving their equations together gives the coordinates of that point.

## The system

Two linear equations in two unknowns have a unique solution when the coefficient determinant is nonzero.

## Parallel lines

A zero determinant means the lines are parallel, so they either miss or coincide.

## The determinant

The determinant ad-bc measures the area scale of the coefficient matrix.

To check your understanding, write both equations, compare slopes and multiply across.

Intersection is a linear system, and the determinant decides how many solutions it has.

Each section above supplies the reasoning for one practice family, so work a numeric instance of each before moving on. Write the first step of every instance down before you compute the final value, because the grader checks the number but the method is what the next concept assumes. If an instance refuses to settle, redraw it with smaller coefficients and check the sign of every term; the arithmetic in these families is exact, so a mismatch means a sign or an order of operations, not an approximation.
