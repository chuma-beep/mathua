# Exterior algebra

Exterior algebra studies the exterior algebra is the quotient of the tensor algebra in which repeated vectors vanish. The lesson separates the setup, the computation and the interpretation so practice can test each move.

## Wedge dimension

Wedge dimension starts from the defining objects. Identify what is given, state the governing relation and check the hypotheses before substituting numbers.

## Square-zero

Square-zero carries out the computation. Work with explicit small data, simplify exactly and keep the signs and dimensions consistent.

## Total dimension

Total dimension interprets the result and checks it against the definition. Compare with a second example and state what the answer means in exterior algebra.
