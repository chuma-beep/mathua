# Continuity of inverse functions

A continuous strictly monotone function has a continuous inverse on its range.

## Inverse functions

An inverse reverses the input and output. Its graph is the reflection of the original graph across the line y equals x. A point on the original graph gives a point on the inverse graph.

## Continuity

A continuous strictly monotone function has a continuous inverse. The monotonicity prevents jumps in the inverse. The inverse is evaluated on the range of the original function.

## Monotonicity

Strict monotonicity on an interval is the key hypothesis. A function that changes direction can fail to have an inverse. The inverse of a strictly increasing continuous function is also strictly increasing.
