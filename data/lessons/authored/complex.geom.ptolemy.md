# Ptolemy's inequality

Ptolemy's inequality holds for every four points and becomes an equality exactly when they are concyclic in order.

## The inequality

For four points \(a,b,c,d\), \(ac\cdot bd\le ab\cdot cd+bc\cdot da\), with distances written as moduli.

## Rectangle case

At the vertices of a rectangle the two products are equal, so the inequality is an equality.

## Equality

Equality forces the four points onto a common circle in the stated cyclic order.

To check your understanding, multiply opposite distances, use the diagonal and test concyclicity.

Ptolemy turns a metric inequality into a statement about four points on a circle.
