# The law of cosines

The law of cosines relates the three sides of a triangle to one angle. It extends the Pythagorean theorem to triangles that are not right-angled.

## The rule

The square of a side equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides minus twice their product times the cosine of the included angle.

## Finding an angle

Rearrange the rule to isolate the cosine, then take the inverse cosine. The result lies between zero and pi.

## Right-triangle check

When the included angle is a right angle the cosine is zero and the rule reduces to the Pythagorean theorem.

To check your understanding, square the unknown side, rearrange for cosine and set the angle to a right angle.

The angle in the formula must be the one between the two sides being multiplied.
