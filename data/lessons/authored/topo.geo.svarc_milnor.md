# The Svarc-Milnor lemma

The Švarc–Milnor lemma says a group acting properly and cocompactly by isometries on a geodesic space is finitely generated and quasi-isometric to that space.

## The orbit map

Choose a basepoint in the space. The map sending a group element to its translate of that point is a quasi-isometry when the action is proper and cocompact.

## The orbit count

Cocompactness means the quotient by the action is compact. The space is then a union of finitely many translates of a compact fundamental domain.

## Consequences

The group is finitely generated and has the same growth degree as the space. Hence the fundamental group of a compact manifold is quasi-isometric to its universal cover.

To check your understanding, choose a basepoint, read cocompactness and infer finite generation.
