# Lattices

A lattice is a partially ordered set in which every pair of elements has a least upper bound and a greatest lower bound. Meet and join are the two binary operations those bounds define.

## Meet and join

The join \(a\vee b\) is the least element above both, and the meet \(a\wedge b\) is the greatest element below both. Both must exist for every pair.

## Algebraic laws

Meet and join are commutative, associative and idempotent, and they absorb each other. Those identities can replace the order definition.

## Distributivity

A distributive lattice satisfies \(a\wedge(b\vee c)=(a\wedge b)\vee(a\wedge c)\). Boolean algebras are the complemented distributive case.

To check your understanding, find the least upper bound, apply absorption and distribute meet over join.

A lattice is an order with two canonical binary operations. The algebraic identities and the order definition describe the same structure.
