# Regular local rings

A regular local ring has maximal ideal generated by a number of elements equal to its Krull dimension. Regularity is the algebraic form of smoothness.

## Definition

A local ring of dimension \(d\) is regular when its maximal ideal needs exactly \(d\) generators.

## Embedding dimension

The minimal number of generators of the maximal ideal is the embedding dimension, and regularity says it equals the dimension.

## Smooth models

Local rings of smooth points on varieties are regular.

To check your understanding, find the dimension, minimise the generating set and locate a smooth point.

Regularity compares two dimensions that coincide only at smooth points.
