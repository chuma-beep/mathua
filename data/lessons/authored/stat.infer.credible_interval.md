# Bayesian credible intervals

A credible interval is a posterior interval with a stated posterior probability.

## Form the posterior

Combine the prior with the likelihood by Bayes' theorem. For a beta prior and binomial data the posterior is beta with updated parameters. The posterior summarises all uncertainty after seeing the data.

## Find the interval

Choose the equal-tailed or highest-posterior-density interval. The equal-tailed interval cuts the same posterior probability from each tail. The interval is read from the posterior distribution.

## Interpret the prior effect

A concentrated prior pulls the posterior toward its mean. A weak prior lets the data dominate. The interval width reflects both the prior and the sample size.
