# Highly composite numbers

A highly composite number has more divisors than every smaller positive integer. These record-setters concentrate prime factors into small exponents.

## Divisor counts

The divisor count of n is the product of one more than each exponent in its prime factorisation. Factoring first is faster than testing every divisor. The count is also written tau of n.

## The highly composite test

A number is highly composite when its divisor count is strictly larger than the count of every smaller number. Equality with an earlier record is not enough. One must check every smaller positive integer.

## Finding the next record

Search upward from a bound and keep the first number whose divisor count beats all previous counts. The sequence begins 1, 2, 4, 6 and 12. The records become sparse as the numbers grow.
