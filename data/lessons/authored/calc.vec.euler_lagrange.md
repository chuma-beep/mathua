# The Euler-Lagrange equation

The Euler-Lagrange equation is the stationary condition for a functional. It turns an optimisation over curves into a differential equation.

## Functional derivative

The first variation of an integral with Lagrangian L produces the Euler-Lagrange equation. For L equal to half y prime squared plus a y, the equation is y double prime equals a. The coefficient a is the derivative of the potential term.

## Solving the equation

For a constant right-hand side, integrate twice. The initial conditions determine the two constants. A zero slope and zero value at the origin give a pure quadratic.

## Straight-line check

For L equal to half y prime squared, the Euler-Lagrange equation is y double prime equals zero. Every straight line solves it. The straight line is the shortest path in the plane.
