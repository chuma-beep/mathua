# Homology of projective spaces

Real projective space has two-torsion in every positive degree below its dimension. Its integral homology is therefore not free, unlike that of a sphere.

## Euler characteristic

The Euler characteristic of \(\mathbb{RP}^n\) is one for even \(n\) and zero for odd \(n\). The alternating sum of the cell counts cancels in pairs.

## The first homology

For \(n \ge 2\), \(H_1(\mathbb{RP}^n; \mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\), of order two. The torsion reflects the antipodal quotient of the sphere.

## Modulo two coefficients

With \(\mathbb{Z}/2\) coefficients every degree from zero to \(n\) contributes one dimension. The total Betti number is then \(n + 1\).

To check your understanding, check the parity of the dimension, read the torsion order and count the degrees.

Projective spaces trade free homology for torsion.
