# Chebyshev Functions

**Chebyshev:** $\theta(x)=\sum_{p\le x}\log p$, $\psi(x)=\sum_{n\le x}\Lambda(n)$ with von Mangoldt $\Lambda$; PNT equivalent to $\psi(x)\sim x$ and $\theta(x)\sim x$.

## Prime Counting

### Explicit Formula
$\psi(x)=x-\sum_\rho x^\rho/\rho-\log(2\pi)$ over nontrivial zeros $\rho$; $\theta(x)$ bounds give $\pi(x)\asymp x/\log x$ (Chebyshev constants).

### Use
$\theta(x)\sim x$ is PNT restated.

## Example

$\theta(10)=\log2+\log3+\log5+\log7\approx6.43$.
