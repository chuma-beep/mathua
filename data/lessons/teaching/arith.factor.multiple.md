# Multiples of a Number

## What a Multiple Is

A multiple of a number is the result of multiplying that number by a whole number. The multiples of 6 are 6, 12, 18, 24 and so on. Zero is a multiple of every number because every number times zero is zero.

Multiples continue without end. A factor, by contrast, divides a number exactly and is never larger than the number. The two ideas are linked because a multiple of 6 has 6 as a factor.

## Finding Multiples in a Range

To count multiples in a range, divide the largest value by the number and subtract the count below the smallest value. For multiples of 7 between 20 and 80, the largest count is 11 and the count below 20 is 2, leaving 9.

Listing works for short ranges. Division is faster and less error prone when the range is wide. Both methods should agree, so one checks the other.

## Common Multiples

A common multiple of two numbers is a multiple of both. The least common multiple is the smallest positive one. For 4 and 6 the multiples are 4, 8, 12 and 6, 12, 18, so the least common multiple is 12.

Common multiples are useful when fractions need the same denominator. The least common multiple gives the smallest shared denominator, which keeps the later arithmetic simple.
