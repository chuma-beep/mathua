# Solvable Groups and Composition Series

**Solvable:** Has subnormal series $1=G_0\lhd G_1\lhd\cdots\lhd G_n=G$ with abelian quotients $G_{i+1}/G_i$; derived series reaches $1$.

## Solvability

### Examples
$S_3$ solvable: $1\lhd A_3\lhd S_3$ quotients $\mathbb Z_3,\mathbb Z_2$ abelian; $S_5$ not solvable for $n\ge5$.

### Galois Connection
Polynomial solvable by radicals iff Galois group solvable; $p$-groups solvable via nilpotent.

## Example

$A_4$ solvable but not nilpotent; composition series refines solvable series.
