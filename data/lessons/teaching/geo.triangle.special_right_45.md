# 45-45-90 triangles

This lesson develops 45-45-90 triangles through three connected ideas. Each section adds one layer, gives a worked way to think about the figure and ends with what you should be able to do.

## Equal Legs

Equal Legs is one part of the larger picture. Read the statements below, draw a small example and check each claim against the drawing. The aim is to recognise the pattern first, then apply the rule and finally check the result.

- Identify the objects and labels used in equal legs.
- Apply the relationship to a numerical or coordinate example.
- Check the answer against the geometry and the other sections.
