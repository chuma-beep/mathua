# Laurent Series

**Laurent series:** For $f$ analytic on annulus $r<|z-z_0|<R$,

$$f(z)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} a_n (z-z_0)^n$$

Negative powers give principal part (poles/essential), non-negative give analytic part.

## Annuli and Singularities

### Classification
Pole: finite principal part (e.g. $1/z$ has $a_{-1}=1$); essential: infinite principal part ($e^{1/z}$); removable: no principal part. Residue is $a_{-1}$.

### Domains
Annulus radii determined by nearest singularities: $1/(z(1-z))$ has annuli $0<|z|<1$ with different Laurent expansions.

## Example

$f(z)=1/z$ at $0$: $f(z)=z^{-1}$ is already Laurent with $a_{-1}=1$, regular part $0$; residue $1$.
