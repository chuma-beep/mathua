# Algebraic closure

## Roots of Every Polynomial

An algebraic closure contains a root of every nonconstant polynomial over the base field.

It is algebraically closed and contains no proper algebraically closed subfield over the base.

The complex numbers are the algebraic closure of the real numbers.

## Existence and Uniqueness

Every field has an algebraic closure, and any two are isomorphic over the base field.

The proof uses Zorn's lemma to assemble a maximal algebraic extension.

For finite fields the closure is the union of all finite extension fields.

## Finite Subfields

The algebraic closure of a finite field is infinite.

Its finite subfields correspond to degrees that divide one another.

A question about the closure often asks for the size of one finite subfield instead.

When the base field is finite, replace a question about the whole closure with a question about a finite subfield. The subfield of size q to the d sits inside the closure for every positive d. Its size follows directly from the extension degree.
