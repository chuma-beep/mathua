# Dividing a Fraction by a Whole Number

## Sharing a Fraction

Dividing a fraction by a whole number asks how large each share is when the fraction is split into that many equal parts. The numerator stays the same and the denominator is multiplied by the whole number.

Two thirds divided by four becomes two twelfths, which reduces to one sixth. The fraction is split into four pieces, so each piece is one quarter of the original fraction.

## Using the Reciprocal

The division rule also works here. Dividing by four is multiplying by one quarter. Two thirds times one quarter is two twelfths, which is one sixth.

The reciprocal method is safer when the divisor is itself a fraction or a mixed number. Convert the whole number to a fraction over one, invert it and multiply.

## Checking the Size

The quotient must be smaller than the original fraction because the divisor is greater than one. One sixth is smaller than two thirds, as expected.

If the answer is larger, the reciprocal was probably not taken. If the answer is the same, the whole number was probably treated as one.
