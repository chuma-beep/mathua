# Areas of composite figures

This lesson develops areas of composite figures through three connected ideas. Each section adds one layer, gives a worked way to think about the figure and ends with what you should be able to do.

## Decomposing a Figure

Decomposing a Figure is one part of the larger picture. Read the statements below, draw a small example and check each claim against the drawing. The aim is to recognise the pattern first, then apply the rule and finally check the result.

- Identify the objects and labels used in decomposing a figure.
- Apply the relationship to a numerical or coordinate example.
- Check the answer against the geometry and the other sections.
