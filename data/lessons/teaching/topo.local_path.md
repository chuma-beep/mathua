# Locally Path-Connected Spaces

**Locally path-connected:** $X$ is locally path-connected if every $x$ has a neighbourhood base of path-connected sets.

## Local vs Global Path-Connectedness

### Characterizations
Locally path-connected + connected ⇒ path-connected. Manifolds are locally path-connected (locally Euclidean).

### Counterexamples
Comb space is path-connected but not locally path-connected at the baseline; topologist's sine curve is not locally connected at limit segment.

## Example

$\mathbb R^{n}$ is locally path-connected via balls, which are path-connected; $\mathbb Q$ is not locally path-connected.
