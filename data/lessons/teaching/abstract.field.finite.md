# Finite Fields

**Finite field:** $\mathbb F_{p^n}$ is field with $p^n$ elements, unique up to isomorphism; $\mathbb F_p=\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z$ for prime $p$; multiplicative group $\mathbb F_{p^n}^\times$ cyclic.

## Prime Power Fields

### Existence
$\mathbb F_{p^n}=\mathbb F_p[x]/(f)$ with $f$ irreducible degree $n$; does not exist for $6=2·3$ (not prime power). Example $\mathbb F_4=\mathbb F_2[x]/(x^2+x+1)$.

### Frobenius
$x\mapsto x^p$ is automorphism; $\mathbb F_{p^n}$ is splitting field of $x^{p^n}-x$.

## Example

$\mathbb F_4$: elements $0,1,\alpha,1+\alpha$ with $\alpha^2=\alpha+1$; $|\mathbb F_4^\times|=3$ cyclic.
