# Galois Correspondence

**Galois correspondence:** For a Galois extension $K/k$ with group $G$, intermediate fields match subgroups of $G$ in an order-reversing bijection: bigger field, smaller group. Degrees match indices: $[K^H : k] = |G|/|H|$.

## Worked: subfields of the x^3 - 2 splitting field

Match fields to subgroups of $S_3$:
1. $S_3$ has subgroups: three order-2 (each generated by a transposition), one order-3 ($A_3$), plus 1 and $S_3$.
2. Order-2 subgroups fix cubic subfields $\mathbb Q(\sqrt[3]{2})$, $\mathbb Q(\omega\sqrt[3]{2})$, $\mathbb Q(\omega^2\sqrt[3]{2})$; $A_3$ fixes the quadratic $\mathbb Q(\omega)$.
3. So the degree-6 field has exactly three cubic and one quadratic intermediate fields — read off the subgroup lattice.

So the correspondence converts field hunting into subgroup listing.

## Worked: fixed-field degrees

For $|G| = 6$ and $|H| = 2$:
1. $[K^H : k] = |G|/|H| = 6/2 = 3$.
2. The fixed field of a transposition subgroup is cubic — matching the three cubic fields above.
3. So indices compute degrees with no field arithmetic at all.

So the dictionary runs both ways: subgroups give fields with predictable degrees.

## Solvability link

A polynomial is solvable by radicals exactly when its Galois group is solvable: $x^3 - 2$ (group $S_3$, solvable) yes; generic quintics ($S_5$) no.
