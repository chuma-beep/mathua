# Lebesgue Covering Dimension

**Covering dimension $\dim X$:** Smallest $n$ such that every open cover has refinement with no point in more than $n+1$ sets; $\dim\mathbb R^n=n$, Cantor $0$, subspace does not increase.

## Invariants

### Coincidence
For separable metric, covering, small inductive, large inductive dimensions coincide.

### Embedding
Menger-Nöbeling: $n$-dim compact metric embeds in $\mathbb R^{2n+1}$; Brouwer invariance of domain shows dimension topological invariant.

## Example

$\mathbb R^2$: $\dim 2$; Cantor set $\dim 0$; Hilbert cube infinite dimension.
