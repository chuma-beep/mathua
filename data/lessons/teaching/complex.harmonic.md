# Harmonic Functions and Mean Value

**Harmonic:** $u(x,y)$ is harmonic if $\Delta u = u_{xx}+u_{yy}=0$. Real and imaginary parts of analytic $f=u+iv$ are harmonic via CR; conversely harmonic on simply connected domain has harmonic conjugate $v$ making $u+iv$ analytic.

## Mean Value and Maximum

### Mean Value Property
Harmonic $u$ satisfies $u(a)=\text{average of }u$ on any circle around $a$ — characterizes harmonic functions.

### Maximum Principle and Poisson
Non-constant harmonic has no interior maximum; max on boundary. Poisson integral reconstructs harmonic on disk from boundary values.

## Example

$u(x,y)=x^{2}-y^{2}=\operatorname{Re} z^{2}$ is harmonic; its conjugate $v=2xy$ gives $f(z)=z^{2}$ analytic.
