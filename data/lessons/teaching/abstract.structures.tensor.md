# Tensor Products of Modules

**Tensor product:** $M\otimes_R N$ is $R$-module with universal bilinear map $M×N\to M\otimes N$, $(m,n)\mapsto m\otimes n$, bilinear and $mr\otimes n=m\otimes rn$.

## Properties

### Computations
$\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z\otimes\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\cong\mathbb Z/\gcd(m,n)\mathbb Z$; $M\otimes_R R\cong M$; $\mathbb Q\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z=0$ (divisible).

### Exactness
$\otimes$ right exact; $S^{-1}R\otimes_R M\cong S^{-1}M$ (localization base change).

## Example

$\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\otimes\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z=0$ since $\gcd(2,3)=1$.
