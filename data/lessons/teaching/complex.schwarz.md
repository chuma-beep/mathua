# Schwarz Lemma

**Schwarz lemma:** If $f$ analytic on unit disk $\mathbb D$, $f(0)=0$, $|f(z)|\le1$, then $|f(z)|\le|z|$ and $|f'(0)|\le1$; equality implies $f(z)=e^{i\theta}z$.

## Disk Automorphisms

### Schwarz-Pick
Invariant form: $|f(z_1)-f(z_2)|/|1-\overline{f(z_2)}f(z_1)|\le|z_1-z_2|/|1-\bar{z_2}z_1|$; automorphisms of $\mathbb D$ are Möbius $(z-a)/(1-\bar{a}z)$.

### Use
Bounds analytic self-maps of disk; proves Liouville-type results.

## Example

$f(z)=z^2$ on $\mathbb D$: $|f(z)|=|z|^2\le|z|$, $f(0)=0$, fits lemma with strict inequality.
