# Stone-Čech Compactification

**Stone-Čech $\beta X$:** Largest compactification of Tychonoff $X$, universal: any continuous $f:X\to K$ compact Hausdorff extends uniquely to $\beta X$; $\beta\mathbb N$ is ultrafilters on $\mathbb N$.

## Properties

### Size
$\beta\mathbb N$ huge ($2^{2^{\aleph_0}}$ points), not metrizable; $\beta\mathbb R$ even larger.

### Characterization
Completely regular iff subspace of compact Hausdorff.

## Example

$\beta\mathbb N$ remainder $\mathbb N^*=\beta\mathbb N\setminus\mathbb N$ is growth; Tychonoff spaces embed in $\beta X$.
