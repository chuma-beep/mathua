# Inclusion-Exclusion Principle

**Inclusion-exclusion:** $|\cup A_i|=\sum|A_i|-\sum|A_i\cap A_j|+\sum|A_i\cap A_j\cap A_k|-\cdots$.

## Counting via Overlaps

### Two and Three Sets
$|A\cup B|=|A|+|B|-|A\cap B|$; for three sets $7$ terms. Derangements $!n=n!\sum_{k=0}^n(-1)^k/k!$ use it.

### Application
Integers $\le10$ not divisible by $2$ or $3$: $10-\lfloor10/2\rfloor-\lfloor10/3\rfloor+\lfloor10/6\rfloor=3$ ($1,5,7$).

## Example

$|A|=3,|B|=4,|A\cap B|=1$ ⇒ $|A\cup B|=3+4-1=6$.
