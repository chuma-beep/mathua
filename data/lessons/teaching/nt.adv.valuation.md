# p-adic Valuations

## The p-adic Valuation

The p-adic valuation of an integer is the exponent of the prime p in its factorisation. It measures how divisible the integer is by p, and it turns multiplicative questions into additive ones. The valuation of a product is the sum of the valuations, and the valuation of a power is the exponent times the valuation.

## Valuations of Factorials

Valuations of factorials are computed by Legendre's formula. Count the multiples of p, then the multiples of p squared, then the multiples of p cubed, and add the quotients. The sum is finite because the powers eventually exceed the factorial argument, and it gives the exponent of p in n factorial.

## Valuations in Products

Valuations also control binomial coefficients and divisibility of products. The valuation of a quotient is the difference of the valuations, provided the denominator divides the numerator. This additive bookkeeping is the reason p-adic methods can replace ordinary size estimates in many divisibility arguments.
