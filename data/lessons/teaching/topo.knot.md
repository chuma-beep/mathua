# Knot Theory Basics

**Knot:** Embedding of $S^1$ in $\mathbb R^3$ or $S^3$; unknot trivial, trefoil crossing $3$ simplest nontrivial; knot group $\pi_1$ of complement invariant.

## Invariants

### Crossing and Polynomials
Trefoil $3_1$, figure-eight $4_1$, Jones polynomial distinguishes; Gordon-Luecke: complement determines knot.

### Complement
Knot determined by complement fundamental group.

## Example

Trefoil: $3$ crossings, Jones $q^{-4}+q^{-2}+q^2$ type; unknot Jones $1$.
