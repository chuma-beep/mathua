# Farey Sequences

## Farey Fractions

The Farey sequence of order n lists, in increasing order, every fraction between zero and one whose denominator is at most n, written in lowest terms. The sequence begins with zero over one and ends with one over one. Consecutive fractions satisfy a determinant relation that makes the sequence useful for rational approximation.

## The Length Formula

The length of the Farey sequence of order n is one plus the sum of Euler's totient function from one to n, with the totient of one counted as one. Each new denominator contributes exactly the fractions in lowest terms with that denominator, and there are phi of them. The length therefore grows quadratically with n.

## Mediants and Neighbours

Neighbouring Farey fractions have a mediant that lies between them and that first appears at a later order. This gives a systematic way to generate the sequence and to approximate real numbers by rationals with bounded denominator. The same structure underlies the continued fraction expansion of a real number.
