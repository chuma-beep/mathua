# Fundamental Group Calculations

**Fundamental group $\pi_1(X,x_0)$:** Homotopy classes of loops at $x_0$ under concatenation; $\pi_1(S^{1})\cong\mathbb Z$ (winding number), $\pi_1(S^{2})=0$, $\pi_1(T^{2})\cong\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z$.

## Computing $\pi_1$

### Tools
Van Kampen for wedge of circles gives free group; covering spaces give $\pi_1(\mathbb R^{2}\setminus\{n\text{ points}\})$ free on $n$; Klein bottle non-abelian $\langle a,b\mid aba^{-1}=b^{-1}\rangle$.

### Simply Connected
$\pi_1$ trivial ⇔ every loop contracts; $S^{2}$ simply connected, $S^{1}$ not.

## Example

$X=\mathbb R^{2}\setminus\{0,1\}$: $\pi_1$ free on $2$ generators (loops around each puncture).
