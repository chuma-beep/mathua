# Nilpotent and Solvable Groups

**Nilpotent:** Has central series $1=Z_0\le Z_1\le\cdots\le Z_c=G$ with $Z_{i+1}/Z_i=Z(G/Z_i)$. Finite $p$-groups nilpotent; nilpotent ⇒ solvable.

## Hierarchy

### p-Groups
Finite $p$-groups are nilpotent via upper central series.

### Examples
Upper unitriangular group is nilpotent (Heisenberg); $S_3$ not nilpotent.

## Example

$D_8$ (order $8$) is $2$-group, hence nilpotent; its centre is $\{1,r^2\}$.
