# Comparing Quantities

We compare quantities to decide which is larger, smaller, or whether they are equal.

**Key words:**
- **Greater than** (>): more objects
- **Less than** (<): fewer objects
- **Equal to** (=): the same number of objects

**Example 1:** Compare ⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ⭐ and ⭐ ⭐

Four stars is greater than two stars.
4 > 2

**Example 2:** Compare ● ● ● ● ● ● and ● ● ● ● ● ●

Both groups have 6 dots.
6 = 6

**How to compare without counting:**
Line up the objects in pairs. If every object has a partner, the groups are equal. If there are leftover objects, that group has more.

**Example 3:** 
Group A: ● ● ● ● ● ● ●
Group B: ● ● ● ● ●

Pair them up:
A: ● ● ● ● ● ● ●
B: ● ● ● ● ●

Group A has two extra dots after pairing. So 7 > 5.
