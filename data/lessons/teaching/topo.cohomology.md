# Cohomology

**Cohomology:** Dual to homology, $H^k(X)$ with cup product ring, de Rham via differential forms for smooth manifolds, $H^0$ counts components.

## Structure

### Ring
Cup product gives ring; singular cohomology homotopy invariant; Poincaré duality $H^k\cong H_{n-k}$ for compact orientable $n$-manifold.

### de Rham
Closed forms modulo exact; $H^0_{dR}$ constant functions.

## Example

$T^2$: $H^0=\mathbb Z$, $H^1=\mathbb Z^2$, $H^2=\mathbb Z$, ring generated by $a,b$ with $a\smile b$ generator of $H^2$.
