# Cauchy-Goursat Theorem

**Cauchy-Goursat:** If $f$ analytic inside and on simple closed $C$, $\oint_C f(z)dz=0$. Goursat removed continuity of $f'$ assumption — analyticity suffices.

## Consequences

### Antiderivatives
Locally $f$ has antiderivative $F(z)=\int_{z_0}^z f$ independent of path; Goursat's bisection of triangles proves existence.

### Analytic ⇒ Smooth
Cauchy-Goursat via Goursat lemma shows analytic implies infinitely differentiable.

## Example

$f(z)=1/z$ not analytic at $0$ inside $|z|=1$: $\oint_{|z|=1} dz/z=2\pi i\neq0$, so theorem fails due to singularity.
