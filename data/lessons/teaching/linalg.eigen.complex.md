# Complex Eigenvalues

## A rotation has no real eigenvector

A rotation in the plane generally has no real eigenvector, because no nonzero vector is mapped to a scalar multiple of itself. Its characteristic equation has a negative discriminant and therefore a conjugate pair of complex eigenvalues.

## Complex conjugate pairs

For a real matrix the nonreal eigenvalues occur in conjugate pairs. If p + qi is an eigenvalue then p - qi is also an eigenvalue, and the corresponding eigenvectors are conjugates as well.

## Trace and determinant

The trace and determinant remain real even when the eigenvalues are complex. The trace is the sum of the pair and equals twice the real part, while the determinant is the product and equals the squared modulus of the pair.
