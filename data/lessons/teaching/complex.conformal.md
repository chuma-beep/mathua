# Conformal Mappings

**Conformal:** Analytic $f$ with $f'(z_0)\neq0$ preserves angles (and orientation) at $z_0$; Möbius transformations are conformal on Riemann sphere.

## Angle Preservation

### Nonzero Derivative
$w=z^2$ doubles angles at $0$ where $f'=0$, not conformal there; elsewhere conformal.

### Riemann Mapping
Simply connected domain conformally maps to unit disk; orientation preserved via positive Jacobian $|\!f'|^2$.

## Example

$f(z)=e^z$: $f'(z)=e^z\neq0$ everywhere, so conformal everywhere; maps vertical lines to circles.
