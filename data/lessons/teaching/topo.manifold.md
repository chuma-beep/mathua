# Topological Manifolds

**Topological manifold:** Hausdorff, second-countable, locally Euclidean: every point has a neighbourhood homeomorphic to $\mathbb R^{n}$ (or $\mathbb R^{n}_+$ for manifolds with boundary). Dimension $n$ is invariant.

## Locally Euclidean

### Examples
$\mathbb R^{n}$ (model), $S^{1}$ ($1$-manifold via arcs), $S^{2}$ ($2$-manifold via stereographic charts), $T^{2}=S^{1}×S^{1}$ (compact $2$-manifold).

### Properties
Manifolds are metrizable (Urysohn), locally compact, and $S^{1}$ compact $1$-manifold, $S^{2}$ compact $2$-manifold. Discrete spaces are $0$-manifolds.

## Example

$S^{1}$: chart $S^{1}\setminus\{1\}\cong\mathbb R$ via stereographic projection; at $1$ use rotation, so every point has an $\mathbb R$-neighbourhood.
