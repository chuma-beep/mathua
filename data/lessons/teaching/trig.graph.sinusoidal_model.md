# Sinusoidal Models

## Midline and Amplitude

A sinusoidal graph oscillates around a midline. The amplitude is half the distance between the maximum and minimum values.

If the maximum is 9 and the minimum is 3, the midline is 6 and the amplitude is 3. Both come from the same two numbers.

The midline is a vertical shift, so it appears as a constant added to the sine or cosine term.

## Period and Frequency

The period is the horizontal length of one complete cycle. For y = a sin(bx) + d, the period is 360 divided by b in degrees.

Frequency counts cycles per unit and is the reciprocal of the period. A shorter period means a higher frequency.

A phase shift moves the cycle left or right without changing its shape, amplitude or period.

## Building a Model

Choose sine or cosine according to the starting value, then set amplitude, period, phase shift and midline in that order.

Test the model at the start, at a quarter period and at half a period. The values should match the described maximum, midline and minimum.

State the domain in which the model makes sense; a periodic model may not apply outside the observed interval.
