# Homotopy and Fundamental Group

**Homotopy:** Continuous deformation $H:X×[0,1]→Y$ with $H(x,0)=f(x)$, $H(x,1)=g(x)$. Loops up to homotopy form the fundamental group $\pi_1(X,x_0)$ under concatenation.

## Loops and Groups

### Simply Connected
$\pi_1$ trivial iff every loop contracts: $\pi_1(S^{2})=0$, but $\pi_1(S^{1})\cong\mathbb Z$ (winding number), $\pi_1(T^{2})\cong\mathbb Z×\mathbb Z$.

### Covering Spaces
$p:\mathbb R→S^{1}$, $p(t)=e^{2π i t}$ is the universal cover of $S^{1}$; lifting loops counts winding number.

## Example

Loop around $S^{1}$ once: class $1\in\mathbb Z$; twice $2$, etc. Null-homotopic iff winding number $0$.
