# Riemann Zeta Function

**Riemann zeta:** $\zeta(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}1/n^s$ for $\Re(s)>1$, analytically continued to $\mathbb C\setminus\{1\}$ with simple pole at $s=1$; Euler product $\zeta(s)=\prod_p(1-p^{-s})^{-1}$.

## Analytic Continuation

### Zeros and Poles
Pole at $s=1$ residue $1$; trivial zeros $s=-2,-4,\dots$; nontrivial zeros conjectured $\Re(s)=1/2$ (RH).

### Basel and Special Values
$\zeta(2)=\pi^2/6$, $\zeta(-1)=-1/12$ regularized.

## Example

Euler product: $\zeta(2)=\prod_p(1-p^{-2})^{-1}=\pi^2/6$.
