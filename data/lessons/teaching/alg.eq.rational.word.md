# Rational Equation Word Problems

## Setting Up the Equation

Rational equation word problems describe situations with rates, shared work, or proportions. The first step is to name the unknown and write the quantities that depend on it. A work problem might say one machine finishes a job in x hours and another in x + 3 hours, so their rates are 1/x and 1/(x + 3) jobs per hour. The equation comes from adding rates or from equating a combined rate to a total time.

## Solving the Equation

After the equation is set, multiply every term by the least common denominator to clear the fractions. The result is usually a polynomial equation, often linear or quadratic. Solving it gives candidate values, but the original equation is undefined wherever a denominator is zero, so any candidate that makes a denominator zero must be rejected even if it satisfies the cleared equation.

## Checking the Answer

Interpreting the answer is part of the work. A negative time or a negative rate usually means the setup has the wrong sign or that the situation is impossible. A rate answer should be stated with units, and a time answer should be checked against the story: if two people work together, the combined time must be less than either individual time. Writing the check as a sentence catches both algebraic and modelling errors.
