# Rotated Conic Sections

## When axes are tilted

A rotated conic contains an xy term.
The shape is the same as an unrotated conic.
The coordinate axes no longer line up with the conic axes.

The xy term appears when the conic axes are not parallel to the coordinate axes. Rotating the coordinate system aligns the axes with the conic and removes that term. The shape itself does not change during the rotation.

## The discriminant

For Ax^2 + Bxy + Cy^2 + Dx + Ey + F = 0 compute B^2 - 4AC.
A negative value means an ellipse.
Zero means a parabola and a positive value means a hyperbola.

The discriminant is unchanged by rotation, which is why it can classify the conic before any angle is found. Its sign records whether the quadratic part is positive definite, degenerate or indefinite. That is the algebraic reason the three conic types separate.

## Finding the angle

The rotation angle satisfies tan(2 theta) = B / (A - C).
Rotating by that angle removes the xy term.
After rotation the conic can be read in standard form.

The double-angle formula connects the coefficient B and the difference A - C to the rotation angle. Once the angle is known the new coordinates can be substituted and the conic written in standard form. In practice one finds 2 theta first because the tangent is simpler than the half-angle expressions.
