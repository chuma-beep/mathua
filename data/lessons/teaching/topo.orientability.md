# Orientability of Manifolds

**Orientable manifold:** Consistent choice of local orientation via atlas with positive Jacobian transitions; $S^{2}$, $T^{2}$, $\mathbb R^{n}$ orientable; Möbius band, Klein bottle non-orientable.

## Detecting Orientability

### Criterion
Orientable iff no embedded Möbius band; equivalently first Stiefel-Whitney $w_1=0$.

### Examples
$S^{2}$ orientable (normal vector field outward), $T^{2}$ orientable, Möbius band contains orientation-reversing loop.

## Example

Möbius band: central circle traversed once flips orientation, so non-orientable; its double is orientable cylinder.
