# Fractions, Decimals and Percents

## One Value, Three Forms

A fraction, a decimal and a percent can name the same number. Three quarters, zero point seven five and seventy-five percent are equal. Converting to one form makes a comparison straightforward.

Converting to a decimal is usually easiest. Divide the numerator by the denominator, and divide a percent by one hundred. The decimal forms can then be ordered by place value.

## Converting Between Forms

A fraction becomes a decimal by division and a percent by multiplying the decimal by one hundred. A percent becomes a fraction by writing it over one hundred and simplifying.

Seventy-five percent becomes seventy-five hundredths, which reduces to three quarters. Zero point six becomes six tenths, which reduces to three fifths. The reduced fraction is the simplest exact form.

## Comparing Mixed Lists

To compare a mixed list, convert every entry to a decimal or every entry to a fraction with a common denominator. Decimals are faster for a quick ordering while fractions preserve exactness.

Watch for percents that look large because of the number rather than the value. Eight percent is zero point zero eight, which is smaller than one fifth even though eight is larger than one.
