# Sylow Theorems

**Sylow theorems:** For $|G|=p^{k}m$ with $p\nmid m$, a Sylow $p$-subgroup of order $p^{k}$ exists; all Sylow $p$-subgroups are conjugate; $n_p\equiv1\bmod p$ and $n_p\mid m$; a unique Sylow $p$-subgroup is normal.

## Counting Sylow Subgroups

### Existence and Conjugacy
Sylow $p$-subgroups exist and are conjugate under $G$.

### Congruence and Divisibility
$n_p\equiv1\pmod p$ and $n_p\mid |G|/p^{k}$ restrict possibilities. If $|G|=12=2^{2}\cdot3$, $n_3\mid4$ and $n_3\equiv1\bmod3$ so $n_3\in\{1,4\}$; $|G|=15=3\cdot5$ forces $n_5=1$, so the Sylow $5$-subgroup is normal.

## Example

Group of order $12$: $n_2\mid3$ and $n_2\equiv1\bmod2$ so $n_2\in\{1,3\}$; $n_3\in\{1,4\}$ as above. Either case constrains structure.
