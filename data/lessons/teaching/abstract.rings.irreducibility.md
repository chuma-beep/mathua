# Irreducible polynomials

## What Irreducible Means

A nonconstant polynomial is irreducible when it factors only through a unit and a constant multiple of itself.

Over a field the degree of a factor must be strictly smaller.

An irreducible polynomial generates a maximal ideal, so the quotient is a field.

## Counting over Finite Fields

The number of monic irreducibles of degree n over a field with p elements follows a divisor formula.

For small n the count can be checked by listing candidates.

A quadratic is irreducible exactly when it has no root in the field.

## Testing a Candidate

The polynomial x squared plus one is irreducible over the reals and reducible over the complex numbers.

Over a finite field its status depends on whether minus one is a square.

A single root test settles the quadratic case without counting the whole family.

For a quadratic over a finite field, substitute every element or use the quadratic residue test. For higher degree, a root rules out irreducibility but a missing root does not prove it. State which test you are using before concluding.
