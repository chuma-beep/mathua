# Ordinal Numbers

**Ordinal numbers** tell the position of something in a list. They answer "which place?" instead of "how many?"

**First ten ordinal numbers:**
| Position | Ordinal | Abbreviation |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| 1 | first | 1st |
| 2 | second | 2nd |
| 3 | third | 3rd |
| 4 | fourth | 4th |
| 5 | fifth | 5th |
| 6 | sixth | 6th |
| 7 | seventh | 7th |
| 8 | eighth | 8th |
| 9 | ninth | 9th |
| 10 | tenth | 10th |

**Example:** In this line of animals:
🐶 🐱 🐰 🐻 🐼

- 🐶 is **first** (1st)
- 🐱 is **second** (2nd)
- 🐰 is **third** (3rd)
- 🐻 is **fourth** (4th)
- 🐼 is **fifth** (5th)

**Practice:**
- What is your position in a line of 10 people if you are the 7th person?
- If there are 5 cookies, which one is the 3rd cookie?
- Name the 10th month of the year.

Ordinal numbers help us describe order and sequence in everyday situations, from race results to steps in a recipe.
