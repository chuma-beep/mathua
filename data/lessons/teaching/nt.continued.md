# Continued Fractions

**Continued fraction:** $[a_0;a_1,a_2,\dots]=a_0+1/(a_1+1/(a_2+\cdots))$; convergents $p_k/q_k$ give best rational approximations; periodic iff quadratic irrational (Lagrange).

## Approximations

### Convergents
$\sqrt2=[1;2,2,2,\dots]$, $\pi=[3;7,15,1,292,\dots]$ gives $22/7$; $e=[2;1,2,1,1,4,\dots]$ pattern.

### Best Approximation
$|x-p_k/q_k|<1/(q_k q_{k+1})$; denominators grow, accuracy improves.

## Example

$[3;7]=3+1/7=22/7\approx\pi$ ($0.04\%$ error); next $355/113$ from $[3;7,15,1]$ is $2.7×10^{-5}$ error.
