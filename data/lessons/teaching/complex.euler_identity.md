# Euler's Identity

**Euler's identity:** The special case of Euler's formula at $\theta=\pi$,

$$e^{i\pi}+1=0$$

It links the five constants $0,1,i,\pi,e$ and follows from $e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$.

## Five Constants in One Equation

### From Euler to Identity
$e^{i\pi}=\cos\pi+i\sin\pi=-1+0i$, so $e^{i\pi}+1=0$.

### Related Values
$e^{i\pi/2}=i$, $e^{i\pi}=-1$, $e^{2i\pi}=1$. Each is a point on the unit circle reached by rotating through the angle.

## Example

Verify $e^{i\pi}=-1$: $\cos\pi=-1$, $\sin\pi=0$, so $e^{i\pi}=\cos\pi+i\sin\pi=-1$.
