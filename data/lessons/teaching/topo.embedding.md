# Embedding Theorems

**Embedding:** Injective immersion homeomorphic onto image; Whitney embeds $n$-manifold in $\mathbb R^{2n}$, Nash isometrically embeds Riemannian manifold in $\mathbb R^N$.

## Types

### Wild vs Tame
Alexander horned sphere is wild embedding of $S^2$ in $\mathbb R^3$; Schoenflies says tame $S^{n-1}$ in $S^n$ separates.

### Urysohn
Countable product of $[0,1]$ gives universal embedding for separable metric.

## Example

$S^1$ embeds in $\mathbb R^2$ as unit circle; $S^2$ embeds in $\mathbb R^3$ as sphere.
