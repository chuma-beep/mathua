# Proof by Contradiction

**Contradiction:** Assume $\lnot P$, derive $Q\land\lnot Q$ (false), conclude $P$. Distinct from contrapositive ($\lnot Q\to\lnot P$).

## Classic Proofs

### Irrationality and Infinitude
$\sqrt2$ irrational: assume $\sqrt2=p/q$ reduced, get $p,q$ both even contradiction. Primes infinite: assume finite list $p_1,\dots,p_k$, $N=p_1\cdots p_k+1$ has new prime factor.

### Structure
Assume ¬P → derive false → P holds.

## Example

Prove $\sqrt2$ irrational: assume rational, write $p/q$ lowest terms, $2q^2=p^2$ ⇒ $p$ even, $q$ even, contradict reduced.
