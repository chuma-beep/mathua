# Commutator Subgroup and Abelianization

**Commutator:** $[x,y]=x^{-1}y^{-1}xy$; commutator subgroup $G'=\langle[x,y]\rangle$; $G/G'$ abelianization, universal abelian quotient; $G$ abelian iff $G'=1$; $S_3' = A_3$, $F_n/[F_n,F_n]\cong\mathbb Z^n$.

## Abelianization

### Universal Property
Any homomorphism to abelian $A$ factors via $G\to G/G'$.

### Free Groups
$F_n$ abelianization $\mathbb Z^n$ via exponent sum.

## Example

$S_3$: $[ (1\,2),(1\,3)]=(1\,2\,3)\in A_3$, $S_3' = A_3$, $S_3/A_3\cong\mathbb Z_2$.
