# Euler formula for polyhedra

This lesson develops euler formula for polyhedra through three connected ideas. Each section adds one layer, gives a worked way to think about the figure and ends with what you should be able to do.

## Faces, Edges and Vertices

Faces, Edges and Vertices is one part of the larger picture. Read the statements below, draw a small example and check each claim against the drawing. The aim is to recognise the pattern first, then apply the rule and finally check the result.

- Apply the relationship to a numerical or coordinate example.
