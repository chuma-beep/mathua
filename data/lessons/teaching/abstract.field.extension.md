# Field Extensions and Splitting Fields

**Field extension:** $K/F$ with $F\subseteq K$ fields; degree $[K:F]$ is dimension of $K$ as $F$-vector space. For $\alpha$ algebraic with minimal polynomial $p(x)$ of degree $n$, $[F(\alpha):F]=n$.

## Building Extensions

### Simple Extensions
$F(\alpha)\cong F[x]/(p(x))$ where $p$ is minimal polynomial of $\alpha$. Example: $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)\cong\mathbb Q[x]/(x^{2}-2)$, degree $2$.

### Splitting Fields and Tower Law
Splitting field of $p$ adjoins all roots: $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)$ splits $x^{2}-2$. Tower law: $[K:F]=[K:E][E:F]$; $[\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,\sqrt3):\mathbb Q]=4$.

## Example

$\mathbb Q(\sqrt[3]{2})$: $x^{3}-2$ Eisenstein at $2$, irreducible degree $3$, so $[\mathbb Q(\sqrt[3]{2}):\mathbb Q]=3$.
