# Product Topology

**Product topology:** For spaces $X_i$, the product $\prod_i X_i$ carries the coarsest topology making each projection $\pi_j:\prod_i X_i\to X_j$ continuous. A basis consists of products of opens where only finitely many factors are proper.

## Infinite vs Finite Products

### Finite Products
Basis is $U_1\times\cdots\times U_n$ with each $U_i$ open in $X_i$. Finite products of compact spaces are compact (Tychonoff, finite case).

### Infinite Products
Only finitely many coordinates can be constrained at a time in a basic open. The box topology (all factors arbitrary) is strictly finer and not the product topology.

## Example

In $\mathbb{R}^{\mathbb N}$ with product topology, a basic open is $\prod_i U_i$ where $U_i=\mathbb R$ for all but finitely many $i$. Projections $\pi_n$ are continuous by design, and $\mathbb R^{\mathbb N}$ is metrizable.
