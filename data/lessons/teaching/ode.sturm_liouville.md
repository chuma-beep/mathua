# Sturm-Liouville Theory

**Sturm-Liouville:** $(p y')'+(\lambda w - q)y=0$ with separated BC, self-adjoint, real eigenvalues $\lambda_n$, orthogonal eigenfunctions with weight $w$, discrete spectrum, completeness.

## Spectral Properties

### Self-Adjoint and Real
Operator self-adjoint under $w$, eigenvalues real, eigenfunctions orthogonal: $\int_a^b y_n y_m w =0$ ($n\neq m$).

### Completeness
Eigenfunctions form basis; Legendre equation is SL with $p=1-x^2$.

## Example

$y''+\lambda y=0$, $y(0)=y(\pi)=0$: eigenvalues $\lambda_n=n^2$, eigenfunctions $\sin nx$.
