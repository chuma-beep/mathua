# Dimensional Analysis

## Conversion Factors

A conversion factor is a ratio equal to one, such as 12 inches over 1 foot. Multiplying by it changes units without changing the amount.

Write the factor so the unwanted unit cancels. The unit that remains is the unit of the answer.

Conversion factors can be chained when no single factor connects the starting and ending units.

## Chaining Factors

Multiply the given quantity by each factor in turn, cancelling units as you go. Only the final unit should remain.

For minutes to seconds through hours, use 60 minutes per hour and 60 seconds per minute, arranging each so the previous unit cancels.

Keep the numbers in exact form until the final step, then compute and round only if the problem asks.

## Checking a Conversion

Estimate the size of the answer before computing. Converting to a smaller unit should give a larger number.

A conversion that makes a large quantity smaller when the unit also became smaller is a sign that a factor was inverted.

Label every step with units; the cancellation pattern is the proof that the setup is correct.
