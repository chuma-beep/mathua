# Möbius Transformations

**Möbius:** $w=(az+b)/(cz+d)$, $ad-bc\neq0$, bijective on $\hat{\mathbb C}$, group $PSL(2,\mathbb C)$, 3-transitive, preserves cross-ratio and circles/lines.

## Sphere Automorphisms

### Circle Preservation
Möbius maps circles/lines to circles/lines; $w=1/z$ swaps $0$ and $\infty$.

### Group Structure
$PSL(2,\mathbb C)$ acts 3-transitively: any $3$ points → $0,1,\infty$.
