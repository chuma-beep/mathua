# Bifurcations in Nonlinear ODEs

**Bifurcation:** Qualitative change in phase portrait as parameter $\mu$ crosses a critical value — number or stability of equilibria/periodic orbits changes.

## Types

### Saddle-Node and Transcritical
$x'=\mu-x^{2}$: $0$ equilibria if $\mu<0$, $1$ at $\mu=0$, $2$ if $\mu>0$ (creation). $x'=\mu x-x^{2}$: two equilibria exchange stability at $\mu=0$.

### Pitchfork and Hopf
$x'=\mu x-x^{3}$: pitchfork at $\mu=0$ (one → three equilibria, supercritical). Hopf: complex pair crosses imaginary axis, limit cycle appears.

## Example

Pitchfork $x'=\mu x-x^{3}$: $\mu<0$ one stable at $0$; $\mu>0$ $0$ unstable, $\pm\sqrt{\mu}$ stable branches. Normal form for symmetry-breaking.
