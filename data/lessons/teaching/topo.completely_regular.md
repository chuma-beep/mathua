# Completely Regular Spaces

**Completely regular (Tychonoff if T1):** For each closed $A$ and $x\notin A$, there is continuous $f:X\to[0,1]$ with $f(x)=1$, $f|_A=0$. Normal ⇒ completely regular; metric ⇒ completely regular.

## Functions Separating Points and Closed Sets

### Tychonoff Spaces
Tychonoff = $T_1$ + completely regular. Product of Tychonoff is Tychonoff; $ \mathbb R^{n}$ is Tychonoff; Niemytzki plane is Tychonoff.

### Use
Tychonoff spaces are exactly subspaces of compact Hausdorff (Stone-Čech compactification).

## Example

Metric $X$: $f(y)=d(y,A)/(d(y,A)+d(y,x))$ separates $x$ and closed $A$, showing complete regularity.
