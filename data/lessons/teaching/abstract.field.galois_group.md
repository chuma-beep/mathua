# Galois Groups of Polynomials

**Galois group:** $\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)$ for splitting field $K$ of $f$; generic quintic has $S_5$, $x^2-2$ has $\mathbb Z_2$, cyclotomic $Q(\zeta_p)$ has $(\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)^\times\cong\mathbb Z_{p-1}$; discriminant square $\Leftrightarrow$ group $\subseteq A_n$.

## Polynomials

### Finite Field Extensions
$\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb F_{p^n}/\mathbb F_p)$ cyclic generated by Frobenius $x\mapsto x^p$.

### Generic Behavior
Hilbert irreducibility: generic quintic $S_5$, not solvable.

## Example

$x^3-2$: splitting field $\mathbb Q(\sqrt[3]{2},\omega)$, $|\operatorname{Gal}|=6\cong S_3$.
